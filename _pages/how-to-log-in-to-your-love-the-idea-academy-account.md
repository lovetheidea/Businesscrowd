This guide details the steps to how to log in to your Love the Idea Academy account, using your email, Google Account, or LinkedIn Account.

## Log in with an email and password

1\. Click **Login** at the top right. ![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2-300x126.png) 2\. Enter the credentials you used to sign up. 3\. Click **Log In**.

## Log in with Google or LinkedIn

1\. Click Login at the top right. 2\. Select Continue with **Google or LinkedIn** ![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/3-270x300.png) 3\. Follow the prompts to complete the sign-in process.
