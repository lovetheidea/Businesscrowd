About Love the Idea

Love the Idea is a UK business education platform helping entrepreneurs and employees grow through online courses, coaching programmes, and practical training content.

From e-learning fundamentals to leadership development, the Love the Idea Academy has helped thousands of learners across the UK sharpen their business skills — at their own pace, on any device.

As a BusinessCrowd member, Love the Idea share their training articles, blog posts, and events exclusively with the Crowd.
