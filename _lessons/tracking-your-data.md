We'll go over a simple yet thorough technique to track your most critical sorts of business data so you can better manage your business and ensure that everything is running smoothly and successfully.

### **How to Use This Report** 

The **Track Your Data Spreadsheet** is a Microsoft Excel spreadsheet, but can also be opened up and used with Google Spreadsheet. By tracking the data in this spreadsheet, you will be able to see how different tactics/strategies/other factors affect the performance of your business. 

While it may take some time to become acquainted with these numbers and the types of adjustments (if any) you might make when you see something, you should start tracking it as soon as possible so that you have it available later on. Some of these data points can only be obtained by gathering them every day, so if you don't start tracking them right away, you won't have access to all of the data later. 

The spreadsheet is divided into three sections, each with its own tab: **Sales, Inventory**, and **Weekly Report**

![Track Your Data Sections](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-7-Image-1-300x36.png)

### **Sales** 

This tab maintains track of each product's sales by day. The crucial bits of information may be found in Seller Central's **"Detail Page and Sales Traffic by ASIN"** report, which can be located under **"Business Reports."** It will allow you to see sales volume trends as well as how effectively your products convert.

You fill in the **Units Ordered**, **Gross Product Sales**, **Sessions**, **Buy Box Percentage**, and **Unit Session Percentage** fields for each product.

If you neglect to start populating the data for this report, you can always alter the date range on the "Detail Page and Sales Traffic by ASIN" report to go back in time and populate it.

![Sales](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-7-Image-2-300x127.png)

### **Inventory** 

For each day, this tab keeps track of various essential bits of product information. The "**Manage Inventory Health** " under Amazon's "Fulfillment Reports" section is the major source. Because that report can only show you information for the precise time it was run, it's necessary to run it at the same time every day (if possible). You'll also need to manually check the sales rank (BSR) of your product on the product detail page.

You fill in the **Available Units**, **Your Sales Price**, and **Sales Rank** for each product (BSR). It will determine the Available Inventory in Dollar Value for you automatically. Please keep in mind that anytime you or anybody else visits your product detail page, the "**Your Sales Price**" should represent what your product is selling for that day. It will not, however, contain any "Promotion" pricing, because that reduction is only available to consumers who submit a promo code.

![Inventory ](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-7-Image-3-300x122.png)

### **Weekly Report** 

This final tab compiles a week's worth of data to provide you with a snapshot of how your products are performing. After you've finished, either save a new version of the spreadsheet with the information for the week you're reporting on or open a new tab for each week's data.

**Sales in Units and Sales in Dollars** 

This section keeps track of our overall product sales in both units and dollars. It will automatically plot that information in the graphs below.

This information can be seen in the "**Detail Page and Sales Traffic - By ASIN**" report.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sales-in-Unit-and-Dollar-300x126.png)

**Weekly Totals in Units & Dollars for the Previous 4 Weeks** 

This section will track total sales for the past four weeks in both units and dollars, as well as automatically chart the data underneath it. 

This information is likewise derived from the "**Detail Page and Sales Traffic - By ASIN**" report, and it's quite useful for determining patterns over the last month.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/weekly-total-in-4-weeks-300x114.png)

**Product Sales & Sessions** 

This area will track the number of units ordered and the number of sessions per day, so you can see how well your products convert when clients visit your product detail page. 

You'll need to use the "**Detail Page and Sales Traffic – By ASIN**" report to gather this information.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Product-1-and-2-sales-and-sessions-300x124.png)

**Product Buy Box & Unit Sessions** 

This area will keep track of how many times your product has the Buy Box, as well as the Unit Session Percentage for each day. The "**Detail Page and Sales Traffic - By ASIN**" report also contains this information.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/product-1-and-2-buybox-and-unit-session-300x124.png)

Even if you don't think this data will be useful to you, you should do your best and start tracking it now. This would be an excellent job to delegate to a VA once you've completed it and can demonstrate how to do it. 

However, you should become quite familiar with the data contained inside it (you will become more familiar with it over time), since it will be critical in assisting you in optimizing your firm for optimal earnings.
