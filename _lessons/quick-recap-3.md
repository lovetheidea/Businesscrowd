Module 3 is all about locating a supplier and sending your first batch of merchandise to Amazon's fulfilment facilities.

Here are the things we learned in this module:

1.  Google is one of the great places to start looking for the manufacturer, wholesaler, and supplier of the products you're looking for.
2.  Using Alibaba.com to Find Suppliers and Sourcing Products. It's the most popular online shopping destination in the world's fastest-growing e-commerce market. Some people are hesitant to use a service like Alibaba because it does business with a lot of foreign countries.
3.  Learn how to contact suppliers for your product opportunities. 
4.  Learn the process of preparing your products to be sold through Amazon's Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) service. 
5.  Create your own design and product packaging. You'll need to hire your own designer unless the supplier you're using will do it for you.
6.  Minimize your risk by placing your first order for inventory on Amazon at a very low cost.

This is the end of this course module, but it is only the beginning of your Amazon Business journey. The next section of the Amazon Mastery Course: The Ultimate Guide to Starting and Running a Successful Amazon Business will teach you how to create a profitable Amazon business.
