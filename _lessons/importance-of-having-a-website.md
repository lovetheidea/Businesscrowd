The majority of consumers now conduct online research before making any purchases. Even businesses conduct online research before adding a new team member.

![Lesson 1: Basics of website creations featured image](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Lesson-1-300x178.png)

# **Professional Website** 

If you want to land a job with your dream company in today's job market, you should make yourself available online. Take the necessary steps to strengthen your digital reputation, such as creating a Professional Website that showcases your skills, capabilities, work portfolio, and so on. It will demonstrate to the company's hiring manager that you are serious about pursuing a career. 

![Business Website Media](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Lesson-1-media-300x189.png)

81% of the employers, as indicated in the 2017 Hover online recruitment survey, visit a portfolio if an option is provided, and 71% of the employers agree that their decision to hire a candidate on the website will affect an online portfolio _(Hover Blog, Study: Will Quality of Portfolio Site Influence Hiring Decisions? 2017)_

# **Business Website**

The same goes for your business in today's modern market, you should make it available online to **survive**.

![Personal Website Lesson Media](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Lesson-1-media.2-300x142.png)

According to Google Consumer insights, **_"78% of consumers have spent more time researching a brand or product online than they have researching in a store. This means that having your business available online increases your chances of being found by your target market compared to other businesses that do not have an online presence. "_**

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/h2aO7-consumer-insights-consumer-trends-consumer-product-research-statisti-1-300x160.jpg)

In today's modern market, your website is the best salesperson you can have as it builds an impression of your business online. Even if you can reach and engage through third-party social media sites, it is still best to have a location accessible to all of your customers 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Your website will serve as the backbone for your company's online presence, where they can go to learn more about it. It will provide all forms of communication and information about your company. As a result, your website must provide consumers with a clear understanding of what your brand represents and what products or services you provide.
