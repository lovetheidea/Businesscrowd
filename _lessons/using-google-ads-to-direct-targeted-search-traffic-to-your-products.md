This lesson will show you how to use the world's most popular search engine to drive targeted traffic to your Amazon product listing.

### **What is Google?**

Google is the world's most well-known internet search engine. Founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin on September 4, 1998. Google was developed with a search engine algorithm that ranked pages not only based on their content, but also on the number of other websites that linked back to them. Today, Google is more than just searching for definitions of words on the internet; with its rapidly expanding company, it now offers a wide range of functionality, including productivity and management tools such as Google Slides, Google Docs, and Google Spreadsheets, Google Calendar, and so on. You can even use Google Ads to run ads on this platform.

### **Google Ads**

Google Ads is an online advertising solution developed by Google. This tool lets businesses promote their services or product on the net through running ads that will let them gain traffics worldwide. These are the simplest to set up and the most likely to generate a profit for Amazon businesses.

#### **How do Google Ads work?**

This works by displaying your ad when people in your target demographic search the internet for the terms of products and services similar to yours. If you type in any services or product terms into the search box and click the search button, the Google search algorithm will return any results that are related to your search terms.

Take a look at the example below. I will search for an eCommerce course and you'll see a page that looks like this. As you can see, Google displays items that are relevant to your search terms. Displaying both sponsored or ad content and organic search results.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img14-245x300.png)

**Important Disclaimer:** If numerous advertisers are attempting to bring traffic to Amazon.com for the same term, Google will only allow ONE of those advertisements to appear at any given moment. The amount bet for that keyword will determine which ad it displays, so you may end up having to spend more than you want in order for your ad to appear.

As a result, this method may be best suited to your product's first launch, when you're more concerned with generating initial visitors than with making a profit.

#### **Types of Google Ads**

There are different types of Google Ads that advertisers can use to promote their businesses. Here are some of the most common: 

-   **Google Search Ads** 

Most common types of Google advertisements. This is the campaign that we have stated above. An ad that is based on text or keywords search, with that, said your ads will appear once someone within your target user area searches for a product or service similar to yours. Learn more about this campaign [here.](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722047?hl=en&ref_topic=10543918)

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img15-300x63.png)

-   **Google Display Ads**

This type of campaign allows you to reach people who aren't actively looking for the products or services you offer but are considered to be the right target audience because they have demonstrated a similar movement or action as your target audience demographics. These advertisements appear while they are browsing their favourite websites, watching a YouTube video with a friend, checking their Gmail account, or using mobile devices and apps. Learn more about this campaign [here.](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190?hl=en&ref_topic=10289774)

Here is an example of Google Display Ads:

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img16-300x37.png)

-   **Shopping Ads**

Product-specific Google ad campaign. Advertisers frequently use this method to target people who are ready to buy. This type of campaign is used to inform buyers about product information such as images, reviews, shops, and even product ratings. Know more about this campaign [here.](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454022?hl=en&ref_topic=6275320)

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img17-300x234.png)

-   **Video Ads**

Based on the name, this is a video-based campaign. Where your ads will reach audiences via YouTube, the web, or any apps that use Google video partners. [Here](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6340491?hl=en&ref_topic=10513318) you can know more about how it works and how it helps your business. 

-   **App Campaign**

Do you have an app for your company? Then this campaign is for you. Unlike other Google Ads, you do not need to design anything for this ad; just a few text lines, your starting bid, your campaign budget, language, and target location of your ads, and you are ready to go. The Google system will handle your ads' next process, including testing out different styles of ads to see which ones work and perform best for your business. Learn more about this campaign [here](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6247380?hl=en&ref_topic=10011588).

-   **Smart Ads**

You will only be charged per click from your ads in this campaign. You will create your own ads, and when someone searches for something related to your ad, your ad will appear. In comparison to other ads, it was less likely to require your management because Google Ads will run your ads. This campaign type is not the best option if you want complete control over who sees your ads, interacts with them, and chooses your company. Learn more about this ad [here](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7652853?hl=en&ref_topic=9024773#zippy=%2Csmart-campaigns%2Cother-google-ads-campaign-types).

-   **Discovery Ads**

This campaign type enables businesses to deliver highly visual, inspiring personalized ad experiences to people who are ready to discover and interact with your brand. What's the best part? It is possible to accomplish this with a single ad campaign. Learn more about this kind of campaign [here.](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9176876?hl=en&ref_topic=10307857)

We recommend sticking to Google Search Ads when running ads to drive traffic to your Amazon business. This advertising will most likely reach those who are already in the buying mode, as well as those who are likely to be interested in your products or services.

### **Setting Up Your First Ad Campaign** 

Create an account at adwords.google.com. The instructions for doing so may change over time, so get the most up-to-date instructions from Google and follow them. You may go over this page for more up to date information: [https://ads.google.com/home/resources/how-to-setup-googleads-a-checklist/](https://ads.google.com/home/resources/how-to-setup-googleads-a-checklist/)

Once you have completed all the necessary information, all you have to do now is sit back and wait for your ad to be approved. They are all carefully vetted before going public, so it may take a few hours for them to be authorized (especially for your first ones).

You should check the results of your ads each day to see how they are performing. 

**Tip**: Create a few additional ads for your campaign to compare their performance. Google will automatically cycle your advertisements over time, showing the best-performing one more frequently, but you can also choose to "pause" one if it isn't performing well.
