## **General Criteria For Your Product** 

How do you decide what to sell if you don't already have an opportunity? Remember, our primary concentration is on private label products, which entails creating and labelling our own brand.

To help you with this, consider the following criteria: 

### **1\. Price** 

We will need to purchase inventory if we use Fulfillment by Amazon. That is why pricing is one of the Characteristics of a Successful Amazon Opportunity. The selling price should fall somewhere between $10 and $50. Many of these categories will be eliminated by this criterion, including electronics and computers, appliances, building materials, hardware, power and hand tools, and so on.

### **2\. Possibility of Private Labeling**

This has anything to do with pricing. For example, if you see a refrigerator that could be a possibility, you'll discover that it's difficult to private label and will be prohibitively expensive due to shipping costs.

### **3\. Physically Small Products** 

You don't want large items because they will cause problems with shipping, order inventories, and storage.

## **The Search** 

The following are examples of categories you could search in, along with explanations on why or why not to search in that category:

### **1\.** **Category:** [**Appliances**](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=specialty-aps&bbn=256643011&rh=n%3A256643011%2Cn%3A%21468240%2Cn%3A13397451&ref=nav_em__nav_desktop_sa_intl_appliances_0_2_24_3)

This category is under the [Tools & Improvement](https://www.amazon.com/Tools-and-Home-Improvement/b/?ie=UTF8&node=228013&ref_=topnav_storetab_hg) department. This category is not where we would look for an opportunity because of the greater the weight of the product, the greater the FBA fees. Profit margins decrease as the size and weight of the product increase. There may be some opportunities with smaller appliances that you can find, private label, and turn into a fantastic opportunity, but if you want to be efficient in your search, "Appliances" is not the place to look.

![Shop By Product](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Shop-By-Product-1-183x300.png)

### **2\. Category:** [**Camera, Photo**](https://www.amazon.com/Camera-Photo-Film-Canon-Sony/b/?ie=UTF8&node=502394&ref_=topnav_storetab_camera) **&** [**Video**](https://www.amazon.com/b?node=7161073011&ref=lp_502394_nr_n_12) 

This is an intriguing category, under the [Electronics](https://www.amazon.com/s?bbn=172282&rh=n%3A172282&dc&qid=1646280123&ref=lp_502394_ex_n_1) department. When it comes to basic items like cameras, you should avoid buying complex products. Products with shipping and manufacturing issues should be avoided because they will cause the most problems in the long run. However, this does not apply to items such as cases and bags, hard drives, flash drives, and so on that can be found on Camera and Photo sub-category [“Accessories”.](https://www.amazon.com/s?bbn=502394&rh=n%3A172282%2Cn%3A502394%2Cn%3A172435&dc&qid=1635922014&rnid=502394&ref=lp_502394_nr_n_0) As prospective opportunities, these are a little more appealing. If you look into these options, you might be able to find a source, create some unique packaging, and establish your own brand. As a result, this category falls somewhere in the middle of your search criteria.

### **3\. Category:** [**Kitchen & Dining**](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=specialty-aps&bbn=16225011011&rh=n%3A%2116225011011%2Cn%3A284507&ref=nav_em__nav_desktop_sa_intl_kitchen_and_dining_0_2_17_3)

Subcategories such as "[Small Appliances](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=kitchen-intl-ship&bbn=16225011011&rh=n%3A284507%2Cn%3A289913&dc&qid=1635922172&rnid=284507&ref=sr_nr_n_11)" may not offer the best opportunities, but categories such as "[Events and Party Supplies](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=specialty-aps&bbn=16225011011&rh=n%3A%2116225011011%2Cn%3A901590&ref=nav_em__nav_desktop_sa_intl_event_and_party_supplies_0_2_17_11)", “[Bakeware](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=kitchen-intl-ship&bbn=16225011011&rh=n%3A284507%2Cn%3A289668&dc&qid=1635922108&rnid=284507&ref=sr_nr_n_1)” or "[Storage & Organization](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=kitchen-intl-ship&bbn=16225011011&rh=n%3A284507%2Cn%3A510136&dc&qid=1635922205&rnid=284507&ref=sr_nr_n_12)" may. When making a decision, use logic and consider whether there is a supply, an opportunity, and whether this product is worthwhile to pursue. In the following session, rather than just looking around, we'll dig deeper and discover which items are genuinely selling well, but for now, we just want to get a feel for looking around because it's difficult to tell whether something is a good opportunity or not at first glance.

### **4\. Category:** [**Sports & Fitness**](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=specialty-aps&bbn=16225014011&rh=n%3A%2116225014011%2Cn%3A10971181011&ref=nav_em__nav_desktop_sa_intl_sports_fitness_0_2_23_4)

Another category that falls somewhere in the middle is this one. It could have some good and some not-so-good opportunities.

-   -   -   **Treadmills**, **ellipticals,** and **exercise bikes** are examples of bad buys because they are large, heavy items that cost more to ship.
        -   **Kettlebells** and **dumbbells** are also not-so-great potentials because, while they are smaller goods with a nice opportunity for private labelling, the weight of the product, which can range from 20 to 80 pounds, makes it unsuitable for shipping costs.
        -   A **balance trainer** is a good example of an opportunity because it is not particularly brand-driven, is a small item, and does not weigh too much.
        -   **Yoga mats**, **bands,** **workout gloves**, **Pilates tools,** **water bottles**, and other items that aren't very brand-driven, are smaller, and don't weigh much are also possibilities.

### **5\. Category: Pet Supplies** 

This is a massive market. People buy a wide range of items for their dogs. You may not want to sell dog food, but selling dog collars or leashes may be worthwhile.

![Pet Supplies Category](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Pet-Supplies-Category-283x300.png)

### **6. Category: Toys & Games** 

This is also a huge market. However, a lot of it is brand-driven. 

![Toys and Games Category](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Toys-and-Games-Category-183x300.png)

**For example,** Crayola is under [Art & Crafts](https://www.amazon.com/s?i=specialty-aps&bbn=16225015011&rh=n%3A%2116225015011%2Cn%3A166057011&ref=nav_em__nav_desktop_sa_intl_arts_craft_0_2_25_3) sub-category

If you’re looking for crayons, that market may already be owned so that would not be a  good opportunity. However, if you look at more general items, you may find some good opportunities that are not brand-driven. 

Clicking on a category does not always make everything make sense. It also does not rule out the possibility. The crucial factor to remember here is that just because you click on a category doesn't mean you'll find fantastic chances. You must glance around and begin digging.
