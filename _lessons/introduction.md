## **What is Amazon?**

Amazon is a large online retailer that allows individuals and businesses to sell and display products for sale on the internet. On July 5, 1994, Jeff Bezos launched Amazon in Bellevue, Washington. 

It began as an online bookstore but has since expanded to sell electronics, software, video games, clothing, furniture, food, toys, and jewellery. Amazon is one of the most popular online marketplaces for both individuals and businesses, and the site is available in a variety of countries and languages. 

Amazon's Kindle, as well as the Amazon app store, are among its most popular products. Finally, Amazon provides software and infrastructure solutions to both businesses and individuals.
