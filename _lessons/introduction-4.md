The majority of Amazon users simply list their products and leave it at that. Either they produce results or they don't. Few people, including you and me, are focused on developing Amazon as a standalone business, or at the very least as a launchpad for a product, and driving traffic to it from both within and outside Amazon. When you start focusing on what we're teaching you in Module 6, you'll start leapfrogging your competitors.

**Tips for Success** 

-   **Learn & Apply**  

If your product is already available on Amazon, you can begin using what you've learned right away. While you're waiting, plan your marketing strategy for when your product is released.

-   **The more you do, the better outcomes will be** 

Increase the number of promotions and press releases you send out. Make use of the available resources. The better your results will be the more you do these things and the more consistently you do them.

-   **Monitor & track results daily**  

Examine your Amazon listing to see where you rank for specific keywords and what your Amazon bestseller ranking is. Check out your Amazon listing, see where you're ranking for keywords, and see what your Amazon bestseller ranking is. You'll probably do this anyway once you start getting sales, but check out your Amazon listing, see where you're ranking for keywords, and see what your Amazon bestseller ranking is. Check out all of these things on a daily basis. It will help you stay on track and increase the success of your product.
