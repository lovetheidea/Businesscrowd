LinkedIn only forms part of your marketing strategy. It cannot be everything. You have to mix your LinkedIn with other aspects of your marketing. This lesson discusses connecting your LinkedIn strategy to a broader campaign that will enhance marketing results.

## Why Create an Integrated Marketing Campaign

An integrated marketing campaign is a way to maintain a consistent voice across your networks and move prospects through a lead process that is vital for success. It also means that you can keep a conversation going on different platforms.

It also means you don’t have to keep thinking of new ideas. You can create one master campaign and mix that throughout the platforms you use. You can have LinkedIn as your main component or a part of a broader campaign.

## Ways to Integrate LinkedIn Into Your Campaigns

There are numerous options to help you link LinkedIn. Here are some options.

### Part of the Content Strategy

Whether you want to write unique, fresh blogs that only appear on LinkedIn or create summaries of those on your website, or perhaps a collection of introductions of news from across the internet, using LinkedIn’s article feature can be a rich way to create website engagement.

There are numerous options. It depends on your desired positioning as to what you do with LinkedIn. But you can use these articles to establish yourself as an authority in your industry. And you can also link to your website.

### Build a Mailing List

Email marketing is one of the most critical marketing avenues for businesses. For every £1 you spend, you make between £38 and £44 back. You can use LinkedIn to build a list that can be more profitable.

Use a signup form on LinkedIn or a link to a form to get people to subscribe to your mailing list. You can also use a lead magnet to get people to click through from a LinkedIn post and sign up on a website.

### Publish Other Content

Posts are a great way to get attention on LinkedIn. You can do lots of different things on these posts, including polls. But you can also share other content from other platforms.

This strategy is a way to get your content from other platforms seen by more people, which can improve the ROI. It also lessens the stress of creating new content for every platform.

## Final Word: Connecting Other Marketing Activities to LinkedIn

When it comes to the success of your brand, you will want to make sure you can link the platforms you use to each other. This strategy allows you to keep conversations going and improve ROI. LinkedIn is no different, and above are ways you can intermix your LinkedIn campaigns with other platforms.
