# **Beyond the Top 100** 

This lesson will teach you how to look beyond the top 100 best sellers in each Top Level category to uncover some fantastic opportunities.

## **How To Use Subcategories In Your Product Search** 

In the previous session, you learned how to easily identify the top 100 best-selling products in each Top Level category by using Amazon's own "Best Sellers Lists," which can be accessed via the links: [http://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers/zgbs](http://www.amazon.com/Best-Sellers/zgbs) 

However, since that list only goes through the top 100 products, how can you readily identify those that are listed somewhat higher, between 100 and 500? The solution is actually rather simple: Making use of **subcategories**.

When you select a Top-Level category, you will be shown the numerous subcategories that lie beneath it. By picking one of these, you will be sent to a page with the top 100 products in that subcategory. As seen in the video, you can then search for and choose products in these subcategories, then check their BSR (Best Seller Ranking) in their Top-Level category for any hidden gems that are just outside the top 100 but still under 500.

**Important:** You should still utilize their ranking in their **Top-Level category** (rather than the subcategory you're looking at) because this will give you a true indication of how well they're selling. Some things may rank very high in their subcategory, but if that subcategory does not handle a lot of volumes, such items will not have enough demand to meet our product selection criteria.

You can view their BSR in their Top Level Category on the product description page. The items in the red box in the image below indicate their Top Level Category ranking, which you should pay attention to:

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/unnamed-21-300x160.png)
