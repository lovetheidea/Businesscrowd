_Brand awareness_ is very important to your business. It is the first step in driving performance-marketing goals, and it influences consumer decisions when deciding between competing companies. In this lesson, you'll learn about the first steps involved in building your brand and diversifying your revenue. 

### **Brand Awareness Value** 

Even if you sell your products on Amazon, having your own brand website is a great way to establish a presence. This is also a great way to diversify your revenue because it isn't solely reliant on your brand/products being listed on Amazon.

This is a great first step because, even if your products are sold on Amazon, many customers will look up your brand on Google. Putting something out there helps to build a relationship between your product(s) and the potential customer.

### **Brand Website Quality** 

However, the quality of the brand's website is something to be cautious about. I've had the scenario where I've slapped up a website and individuals have expressed concern that they couldn't trust the product because the website was unappealing. 

People will buy your product from either your website (if you're selling it straight there) or Amazon since they feel more sure about the products if you have a strong, solid, professional-looking website showcasing your product and brand.

### **Brand Website Tips** 

Here are some solid tips for building your brand website: 

1.  1.  **The Website Needs to Look Good** 

Always present your product in an accurate way. Create a high-quality website and always be professional.  

1.  2.  **Link to Your Amazon Products** 

Your website can link to your Amazon products or, as I'll show you in the next video, you can set up an eCommerce store to sell your products directly from it as well.

1.  3.  **Include More Information** 

You can incorporate other essential material on your website that you didn't have the capacity for on Amazon or that was in a format that couldn't be used on Amazon, such as videos. You can include instructions and helpful hints, as well as anything else that will assist consumers to gain more value from your product and have a better time using it.

1.  4.  **Customer Service Contact Info**  

A solid Customer Service contact information field is essential. You need something to contact you, whether it's a fill-in form or just an email address where others may reach you. Those interactions can frequently result in positive Amazon evaluations. 

1.  5.  **Capture Emails**  

Capture email addresses from your website to create a customer list that you can use to advertise Amazon products.

Don't overcomplicate things if you already know how to accomplish them; it's just a matter of combining a few key features relevant to your Amazon business and product brand.

You want your website to be as high-quality as possible. You want something that entices consumers to buy; something straightforward. You can go above and beyond and expand it over time, but you should have something in place where consumers can find your product and brand if they search for it outside of Amazon right away. 

When your product really takes off on Amazon, you'll see a lot of people buying it and a lot of people thinking about buying it. They're not going to look on Amazon... They'll look elsewhere besides Amazon. With that in mind, you'll want to make sure they find a reputable website and product brand.

### **Things to Include on Your Site** 

The site shows different sections or elements you'll want to make sure your website has: 

1.  1.  **About Section** 

The 'About' section is where you can really highlight the value of your brand and how it stands out from the competition. You can gather additional information from the internet and your supplier's website if you like; anything that distinguishes your brand. This is also critical in providing excellent customer service because it provides additional information about the company to new and existing customers.

1.  2.  **Our Products** 

This can link directly to your Amazon business page or to a product page. 

1.  3.  **Product Link(s)** 

This website does not require you to have an online store. Instead, you can use Amazon to link to your products. We know major Amazon brands that don't care about creating their own eCommerce store and instead sell everything there. The product link on this example site just redirects to the Amazon homepage. You could take the same strategy on your website but connect straight to your Amazon product listing. Depending on your theme, you can place the links almost wherever on your site, but make sure they seem professional.

1.  4.  **Press** 

Including a 'Press' section on your website will help to establish your brand's credibility. Each press release title would be included separately. So, if you did one every day, you'd have a massive list. You should include the release date as well as a link to where people may discover it on the internet (so they can verify that it is a real press release). As previously said, having this part, as well as an ever-growing collection of valid press releases, greatly enhances your brand's credibility.

1.  5.  **Blog** 

A blog can be used for posting a few different articles and facts related to questions people might ask about your product. 

1.  6.  **Contact information**

People want to be able to contact the brand owner, so this is a must-have feature on your website. You want a complete address as well as a nice contact form that leads to a real person who will respond quickly. If you want to go above and beyond, you could even include a phone number; this will help to establish your brand's credibility. It's definitely something to think about. You could have someone manage the phone service from abroad, even if all they do is answer phones and direct people to email service from afar.

1.  7.  **Call-to-Action**

 This is critical to include and should be prominently displayed to encourage your site visitors to take the desired action, whether it's to read more, provide feedback, sign up, or make a purchase. CTAs are frequently used in the development of your sales road map, for example, creating an email list for your sales update, and so on.

1.  7.  **Testimonials**

Testimonials will demonstrate your credibility and expertise, increasing the trust of your prospects or leads and resulting in the success of your business. You can include testimonials from previous customers, business partners, and anyone else who can demonstrate your credibility with the services or products you offer.

### **Diversifying Revenue** 

Another benefit of having a website is that it can lead to a profitable source of income. Someone who owns a retail store or an online business, for example, might want to wholesale your products or become an affiliate after seeing them on Amazon or elsewhere. Because you have a brand website, they can contact you about it, and you can decide whether or not to do it.
