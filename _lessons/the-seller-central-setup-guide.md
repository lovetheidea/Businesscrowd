This course will show you how to set up your ‘Seller Central account. We'll cover how to run reports, add products, etc. Later, but for now, we'll concentrate on setting up your account so you're ready when we come to those later modules.

![The Seller Central Setup Guide](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/The-Seller-Central-Setup-Guide-300x185.png)

# **Getting Started** 

You must first create a "Seller Central" account before you can begin selling on Amazon.  If you already have an Amazon seller dashboard, login in. 

To create your account, follow these steps:

1.  Please visit [https://sell.amazon.com](https://sell.amazon.com/)

![Become an Amazon Seller](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Become-an-Amazon-Seller-300x133.png)

2\. When you scroll down you will see: **Reach Millions**, **Deliver Smiles**, and **Make Money** headers.

![Make Money headers.](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Image-5-300x104.png)

Below the Make Money, click see Pricing

![Make Money Module 1](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Make-Money-Module-1-284x300.png)

3\. Scroll down and choose your preferred Selling Plans on Amazon: **Individual** and **Professional**.

![Individual and Professional](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Individual-and-Professional.-300x111.png)

**What is the difference between “Individual” and “Professional” Selling Plan?**

The **"Professional"** option costs $39.99 per month plus the additional selling fee. The other selling charge is a percentage charged by Amazon whenever you sell something on Amazon (this is normal and cannot be avoided). 

The **"Individual"** is $ 0.99 per sale plus an additional selling fee. This plan only allows you to sell 40 products per month, but it saves you $39.99.

If you intend to sell more than 40 products per month, becoming a **'Professional**' is the best option. However, if you intend to sell on a smaller scale (i.e. less than 40 units per month), go with the **‘Individual**’ plan. 

We recommend going with the Professional Plan in this case. While the other plan saves you money, you can only accomplish everything I show you if you choose  **"Professional"**. Trust me, the $39.99 monthly cost won't matter.

4\. Click for “**Sign up for Professional.”** 

![Sign up for Professional](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Sign-up-for-Professional-300x56.png)

5\. Next, **Create your Amazon account.** 

![Creating Amazon Account](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Creating-Amazon-Account-277x300.png)

6\. Enter the needed information. Then click **Next**.

![Create Account](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Create-Account-223x300.png)

An email confirmation will be sent to the email address you provided. 

7\. The following information must be shared as the next step in the Amazon seller registration process:

-   -   -   **Your company's address**: This is the country in which your company is based. It is critical that this is correct, as Amazon will be verifying it.
        -   **Your company's type:** State-owned business, Publicly-owned business Privately-owned business Charity None, or I am an individual.
        -   **Your complete name:** Make sure to share your real complete name as Amazon will be verifying it.

8\. Next, Amazon will require you to input certain personal information. Therefore, you're going to ensure that they can verify your identity via a phone number, either a call or a text message. They simply do not want people creating accounts in bulk or engaging in such behavior, so go ahead and verify this so you can proceed past this step as well.

 This includes a form of identification (such as your passport or driver's license number). You may also be asked to provide your phone number. This is for verification purposes. When the required fields are already completed click ‘**Next**.’

![Complete Information](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Complete-Information-300x40.png)

9\. Choose your Marketplace. This is the location of an Amazon store (e.g., Amazon.ca, Amazon.co.uk, etc.) where you want to sell, and then click '**Next.**'

![Informations](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Informations-300x31.png)

 10. You will next be prompted to input your credit card details. This is due to the $39.95 monthly cost. This is a free service for the first month, but you must input your credit card information here in order to access it. If you need a change in your billing address. Carry it out. You can really use a prepaid credit card to get this little amount of credit to address the information, so if you want to do so, you may.

![Billing Information](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Billing-Information-300x186.png)

11\. Add your product(s) and Amazon store information. You'll be questioned about your Amazon store and the products you intend to sell. Once done, click ‘**Next.**’

![Store Information](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Store-Information-300x226.png)

12\. Finally, after completing all of the other steps in the verification process, you will be asked to confirm the business address you provided. A new screen will appear after clicking the **'Confirm'** button, assuming the address displayed is correct. It states that you will receive a postcard with a verification code at that address. When you receive the card, enter the provided code into the **'Enter code below'** field and click 'Next' to complete the verification process.

![Address Validation](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Address-Validation-300x51.png)

Once you've entered all of the requested information, click "Continue" to get access to your real Seller Central account. That is basically all you need to do at this point. There is a lot of functionality inside your Seller Central account, which we will discuss in more detail later: viewing goods, processing orders, and so on. It's designed for individuals who have no prior experience with internet marketing or operating an online company. I'm sure you're all a few levels ahead of those individuals, but anyway, everything is very simple to use. You do not need to be concerned about any of that at the moment. It's just a question of setting things up so that you may go on to the next stage of product research. Then, after you've identified a suitable product, a supplier, and completed the other steps, you can actually add those items to your Seller Central account.
