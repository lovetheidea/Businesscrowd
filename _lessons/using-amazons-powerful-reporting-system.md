This lesson will show you how to use Amazon's reporting system to access highly useful data about your business. We'll also go through how to use a daily tracking spreadsheet to help you manage and grow your business.

### **What are the benefits of knowing your numbers?** 

Most successful firms have one trait in common. They are continuously striving to learn everything they can about their business and the market they operate in. This covers how well their sales are doing, how they compare to their competitors, which marketing methods are working and which aren't, how efficiently they manage their inventory and a slew of other factors.

While many companies struggle or spend a lot of money to establish a system to report on such data, Amazon has made it quite simple for us by providing us with a big number of very thorough reports. Unfortunately, most Amazon sellers are unaware of these reports and choose not to use them, which can be a costly mistake that leads them to make poor business decisions or none at all.

Knowing your numbers is essential for building your business and will provide you with the data you need to transform it into a million-dollar machine.

This lesson will go through a few of the most significant reports you can utilize and explain:

-   -   -   What reports are the most valuable 
        -   What information you can get from them 
        -   When you want to look at them 
        -   What decisions one might make from each

### **Business/Sales Reports** 

#### **Sales Dashboard** 

This can be found in Business Reports, which includes the features _Sales Snapshot_, _Compare Sales_, and _Sales_ by Category. The data displayed in the Sales Dashboard is refreshed once every hour. The purpose of this is to see how your sales are going that day and to also compare sales over time. 

#### **Detail Page and Sales Traffic – By Date** 

One of the popular reports on Amazon. It is used to determine the amount of traffic your product detail page receives and how well it converts after buyers arrive. Allows you to check how many times buyers have returned your products, which could indicate a problem with the product.  

#### **Detail Page and Sales Traffic – By ASIN** 

This is the same as the previous report (By Date) but breaks down the information by product. In most cases, data for these reports are available for up to two years.

### **Inventory Reports** 

#### **Manage Inventory Health** 

This replaces the Inventory Health page. This page combines the best of the Manage Excess Inventory and Inventory Age pages. It can assist you in optimizing your inventory while staying within your storage or restocking limits, lowering costs, and improving your Inventory Performance Index (IPI) score. 

As mentioned earlier the purpose of this page is to determine when you need to order more inventory so that you do not run out.

### **Customer concessions reports**

#### **FBA customer returns report**

To figure out how many things are being returned and why they are being returned. This can help you figure out if you're experiencing any production or shipping issues, as well as allow you to reach out to customers and see if you can fix whatever issue they had with your goods.

### **Advertising Reports** 

#### **Performance Over Time** 

To show how your campaigns in total are performing day by day.

#### **Performance By SKU** 

The purpose of this feature is to show how well the ads for each of your products are performing each day. 

#### **Bid by keyword**

The purpose of this feature is to know how your bid compares to the average winning bid and can help you determine how to bid. You can use this report to find out the amount to bid on each keyword. 

#### **Other ASIN Report** 

The purpose of this is to know which ASINs are purchased after customers click on your ads can help you analyze your campaign performance and make necessary adjustments to meet your business objectives.

#### **Campaign Performance Report** 

The purpose of this is to review keyword results by looking at the impressions, click rate and attributed sales. A similar set of information is provided to you when you view your campaigns but in a downloadable format. 

**Note:** This session addressed some of the most important reports that you may use to begin to start, but **Amazon also offers a variety of other reports**. Please take a look at them to see if you may benefit from them as well.
