The purpose of this lesson is to welcome you to Module 2 and let you know what to expect from this module.

To get started, make sure you've completed a couple of Module 1 tasks: setting up your Seller Central account.

## **Teaching You How to Find Opportunities**

Module 2 is all about how to find the biggest and best opportunities on Amazon. In order to be successful with this business, or any business selling physical products, you don't start by finding products to sell. Instead, you search for red hot opportunities people are already looking for. When it comes to Amazon you will be taken step-by-step through the process.

We hear this question, and we asked it a lot before we opened the doors: "Won't all the opportunities be gobbled up if you let so many people in?" Remember the example: to make $70,000 per year in profit, only 16 things must be sold per day. There are over 84,000 products on Amazon that sell 16 units or more per day. There are numerous opportunities to make this business model work for you. There are thousands upon thousands of items that we will never saturate, so don't be concerned about competition. 

### **Good Opportunities for You**

After completing Module 2, you'll not only have a list of 15 - 20 solid product opportunities that you have found but also great product opportunities that you will pursue within Module 3.

In order to succeed in this module, consider these tips:

-   -   -   **Take One Step at a Time**

We, as entrepreneurs, are driven by our desire to do everything now. That's exactly what I want you to do, it will take time. For instance, in Module 1, the most important step was setting up your Seller Central account.

In Module 2, the big step is getting your list of products and honing it down to 3 good product opportunities. In Module 3, you’ll learn everything you’ll need to know about finding suppliers, domestically and internationally.

-   -   -   **It Necessitates Effort and Hard work**

You'll notice that each of the primary videos in this module has an action step. Pull up your own categories and do some digging; from there, you'll be able to create your own formula for success.

-   -   -   **You Don’t Need a “PERFECT” Product**

Don’t get caught in the “analysis/paralysis” issue. You don't have to have the world's best product opportunity. People are getting caught up in browsing bestseller lists but still being unable to find a product. Amazon tells you which products are selling well and which you may make a lot of money selling. Trust me, if you randomly chose one of those and used what you're going to learn in the coming modules (Modules in Part 2 of Amazon Mastery Course), you'd probably perform rather well. We'll take it a step further and assist you in narrowing down those possibilities and selecting decent ones, but they don't have to be perfect.

This module is where we really kick things into high gear, so make sure you are as up-to-date as possible. Don't be concerned if, for whatever reason, you find yourself having a few troubles here and there and falling a little behind. This training will be available to you indefinitely as part of your subscription, but try to remain on track... you will see tremendous improvements at the conclusion of this.
