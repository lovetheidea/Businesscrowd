The goal of any marketing platform is to ensure that you’re moving audiences from the platform to your sales channel. TikTok is no different. In this article, we look into how you can move audiences from watching your videos on TikTok to other parts of your marketing and sales process.

## Why is it Important to Move Audiences?

The average user on TikTok spends 58 minutes on TikTok every day. Their 58 minutes can be in several daily visits or one. If you’re displayed early, by the time your audience has completed watching videos, you can be sure your audience will have forgotten about your brand.

Therefore, you should move audiences from TikTok to other parts of your marketing activity. In addition, it is critical to consider that social media only accounts for about 1% of all sales. In contrast, SEO and email marketing account for the most digital sales. And research has found that it takes between 5 and 15 engagements before a completed sale.

Therefore, adding more engagement points can help you sell more.

## Where to Send Audiences

There are numerous locations to send potential leads.

The most obvious is an email subscription list. You can use a lead magnet or other freebie to direct users to a landing page before collecting their email addresses. Or use a site like MailChimp to get them to subscribe to your mailing list.

Another option is to get them to go to your website. Once landed on your website, a tracking cookie will enable you to use remarketing to promote to them on other platforms like Google, Facebook and Instagram.

Finally, you could send them to other social media channels. Examples could include YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram. Many TikTokers try to connect audiences to their Instagram accounts. Therefore, you can build an effective following on these channels.

There are some advantages to building up other social media followings. For one, Google takes social media followers into account when ranking websites. In addition, it is vital to consider the number of engagements required for sale. If four out of five sales take more than five engagements, moving them to other social media platforms can help you speed up this process.

## Call-to-Actions

Consider how you can move audiences from one platform to another. The best way to do this is to use call-to-actions inside the video. Use numerous call-to-actions within each video. Each call-to-action must include several features:

-   Provide ownership to the audience.
-   Use verbs to encourage action
-   Provide value to the reader.
-   Make the offer urgent.

And experiment with your audience. Test what call-to-actions work best for them, what platforms they want to connect with, and when call-to-actions work best. Don’t forget that LinkedIn can be a powerful platform to connect to.

Also, carefully place your call to action. The end of the video might seem logical, but not everyone reaches the end, especially if the video is over a minute on TikTok. Therefore, you might want to add call-to-actions at the beginning and end of the video.

## Final Word: How to Move Audiences Off TikTok And Into Sale Funnels

Moving audiences from one platform to another is always a challenging prospect. It requires the right call to action, a good logical step for the audience, and perfect delivery by you. Above are some of the steps/tips you can use to improve your sales funnel and bring on more customers using TikTok.
