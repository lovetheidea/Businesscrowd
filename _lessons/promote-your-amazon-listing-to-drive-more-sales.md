Unlike the product on your online store (if you have one) Amazon listings are more difficult to promote. With so much competition in the marketplace, sellers must craft their listings to be highly visible both ON and OFF Amazon's website.

![Promote your Amazon Listing to Drive More Sales](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Listing-Launch-Image-3-300x185.png)

Remember that the key to reaching your ideal customers is to make it easy for them to find you. Make sure to maximize your listing visibility, whether it is on Amazon or elsewhere.

### **ON Amazon Website Promotion**

Here’s how you can promote your amazon products directly to Amazon:

-   -   -   **Create Discounts** 

To put it simply, everyone enjoys discounts (who doesn't?). With that said it is one of the most effective ways to improve your Amazon ranking. Being at the top of the rankings increases the visibility of your product listings can result in sales wins in the long run.

-   -   -   **Buy Sponsored Product Ads**

Amazon Sponsored Products Ads are Amazon ads that increase the visibility and sales of your desired product listing in Amazon search results. Your listing visibility increases, more likely resulting in your number of sales increase. Seller will pay for these ads only if customers click on them.                                                                                                                                                                    

-   -   -   **Maintain Good Product ratings**

What do you usually look for as a buyer? I'm sure you check to see if there is any feedback on a product before purchasing it online. Positive reviews not only increase the credibility of your product but also play a significant role in determining whether shoppers choose to click on your listings.

-   -   -   **Keep up the excellent shipping performance**

Your shipping performance is also an important factor in the visibility of your Amazon listing. Make sure you have a firm grip on it. Check your shipping metrics regularly and make adjustments to your processes to improve your shipping performance. Signing up for Amazon FBA is also a good option because you will no longer have to worry about shipments as a seller.

-   -   -   **Launch A Lightning Deal**

Lightning Deals are a type of paid promotion that offers time-limited discounts and provide sellers with a one-of-a-kind opportunity to increase sales and visibility. This kind of promotion encourages people to buy sooner rather than later because the discount is only available for a limited time.

### **OFF Amazon Website Promotion**

-   -   -   **Use Social Media**

Nowadays, social media is regarded as one of the most effective means of increasing brand awareness and promoting a business. Remember to post content that is both exciting and relevant. You can promote your Amazon listings on social media in a way that feels natural rather than invasive, such as sharing blog posts that include links to your listings, post content such as giveaways, and more.

You can also try to post content using social media promo codes by Amazon to provide value to your followers on how to save money. More information on this one can be found [here](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/help.html?itemID=67T7489P3LUZVZ8&language=en_US&ref=efph_67T7489P3LUZVZ8_cont_60951).

-   -   -   **Blog** 

Blogs can help you increase the visibility of your listing online if you consider all of the factors when creating an SEO-friendly blog. Some tips on creating blog posts will be discussed in the next lessons.

-   -   -   **Partner with Influencer**

Working with an influencer to promote your Amazon listing, similar to getting product reviews from customers, can significantly increase the credibility of your product.

TIPS: Reach out to influencers who have the same follower base as your brands, as this increases the likelihood of converting those followers to your Listings consumers.

-   -   -   **Run Ads on Facebook or Google**

Facebook and Google are the most well-known platforms for every user; as a result, these two are the best places to spend your money on advertising. In addition to their large user base, these two have sophisticated ad targeting tools. They enable you to target your ads to people who are most likely to convert and become customers. More details regarding these two will be discussed in the following lessons.
