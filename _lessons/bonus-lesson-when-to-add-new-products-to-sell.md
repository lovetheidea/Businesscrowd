The goal of this lesson is to teach you when and how to add new products to your Amazon business.

Now that you have mastered the art of launching and promoting an Amazon product - you've added the product, optimized everything on the detail page, and devised some great promotional methods. Now that you're there, you can consider what new products you'd like to include.

### **When to know if it is time to expand your product set?** 

Before adding any new products, make sure to keep the following in mind: 

-   -   -   **Is your product profitable?** 

We can definitely say it's profitable if your inventory is under control and sales are coming in.

-   -   -   **You’re providing great customer service.**  

You don't want to introduce any new products until this issue is fixed completely. Before introducing any new product, ensure that you have mastered providing 5-star customer service.

-   -   -   **The first product is fully promoted.**  

You need to ensure you know and have applied all the promotion concepts we've taught you in previous modules. 

When you believe you have already hit the right track, as we have indicated in previous lessons, that is the time we recommend for you to add your new product.

### **What product should be added?** 

The products you want to add should ideally be related to your initial product and can also be sold under the same brand. The explanation behind this is as follows:

-   -   -   You should have a solid, coherent brand with multiple products when you start expanding outside of Amazon. 
        -   If the market is familiar then it means easier expansion. It’s also best to have the option of knowing what is in and trending right now. Even in different markets on Amazon, products differ both in terms of how they perform and in their setup. You'll have to dedicate time to many different markets.  
        -   However, is there **another product you want to sell? Go for it!**

### **If your first product fails to perform well on Amazon...**

We've all had products that didn't perform well or didn't work out for various reasons. That's fine, but is there anything else you can do to promote that product on Amazon? Perhaps the promotion you've already done requires a little more time. However, if you don't feel that is the case and you don't want to pursue that product any further, don't worry; everything you've learned about launching a product on Amazon can be applied to any other product.

  
  

**BIG TIP:** Launching another product is the simplest approach to make extra money once you have one product online and SELLING on Amazon. If you continue with the same general market as your previous product, it will be easier and you will have an existing base of experience and clients, but you don't have to.

With that said, this is the next action you should have taken **Make a plan for your next product.** Start thinking about what product you want to launch next, no matter where you are with your present product. Maintain your focus on getting your first product live and profitable, but start thinking about expanding your Amazon business beyond that one product RIGHT NOW!
