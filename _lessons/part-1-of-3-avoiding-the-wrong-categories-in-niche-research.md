# **Keys to Fast Results** 

### **Trust the system**

It's always prudent to assess what you're learning and decide if it's the best course of action for you, but believe us. We understand how to achieve results for you. This is a tried and true method with enormous potential, therefore trust the system.

### **Don’t Waste Time** 

Avoid wasting time on possibilities that "may" work. I'm going to show you several categories where you should avoid wasting your time. Spend no time thinking, "Oh, I'm sure I could make it work for me" or "I've heard of someone doing very well with this." We are professionals; thus, do not spend time on possibilities that "may" work. That is a certain formula for failure and will seriously impede your development and success with this program.

# **What Not to Sell** 

While there are hundreds of products to offer, there are a few critical products to avoid. Whether you currently sell these or are considering selling anything, you should strongly consider choosing an alternative product to offer than these.

# **Avoid These Categories for Fast & Easy Profits**  

-   **Kindle ebooks** – There are less than a hundred novels on Kindle that earn $10,000 or more per month in royalties. Most likely, it is less than twenty. Thousands of people benefit from tangible goods on a monthly basis. Profitability is so much greater with tangible goods than with Kindle ebooks.

-   **Books** – The entire point of books is that they are not written for financial gain. Some individuals succeed, while others struggle, but if you want quick, simple results, avoid selling books. Concentrate on the physical goods side, which we will discuss in more detail in Module 2.

-   **MP3’s Software** – A lot of people ask, “Can I sell this software package?” Maybe, but is that the best thing for this? No, not whatsoever. Focus on real physical products. Those are the biggest opportunities on Amazon. 

-   **Magazines** – Another one that few people will have considered, but it's another significant one to avoid on Amazon.

-   **Apps** – While there are certainly excellent possibilities for selling applications outside of or on Amazon, this is not the business model you want to concentrate on. 

# **“Restricted” Categories** 

Along with the categories mentioned previously, there are some categories on Amazon that require additional approval to sell in. While some sellers sell products in these same categories, we suggest avoiding them for your first product in order to get started fast and with minimal difficulty.

-   Postage stamps
-   Collectable Coins
-   Fine Art
-   Holiday Selling Requirements in Toys & Games
-   Jewellery
-   Join Amazon Subscription Boxes
-   Made in Italy
-   Music & DVD
-   Requirements for selling Automotive & Powersports products
-   Services
-   Sports Collectibles
-   Streaming Media Players
-   Video, DVD, & Blu-ray
-   Watches

In order to find more details about the approval process and what Amazon wants (it varies for each of the above-listed categories), you can visit the Amazon help page:  [Categories and products that require approval](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/help.html?itemID=200333160&language=en_US&ref=efph_200333160_relt_200332540)

# **What to Sell?**

You want to offer actual physical goods with this business model because that is where you will discover the simplest success, the quickest success, and the most scalability. All of the marketing advice we'll offer you next lessons will be most applicable to tangible goods.

Another consideration is that you do not want to be selling other people's goods, although this is a necessary part of the game. You do not want to be selling other people's drop ship goods or anything along those lines. While the majority of what we teach will work for that, is it the ideal use if you want to generate huge profits?

Certainly not!

Promote your own brand. Sell your own tangible goods and you will begin to notice huge returns.
