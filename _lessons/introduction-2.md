This lesson welcomes you to Module 3 and explains what you can expect from this module.

### **Step 1: First Things First** 

You should have already set up your Seller Central account and identified several product opportunities by now (Module 2). If that's the case, all you need to do now is find a supplier and you'll be in the game. Once you've identified a supplier and ordered some inventory (Module 3), we'll begin bombarding you with information on how to optimize your product listing by learning how to market and sell your products (Module 4). 

### **This module will show you the following:**

1.   How to find and connect with suppliers for the product potential you discovered in Module 2. 
2.  And how to identify domestic suppliers and overseas suppliers, and how to set up accounts with both.
3.  You will also discover how to obtain a product label and packaging design. It's incredibly simple and inexpensive. Most of the time, the supplier will take care of it for you. It's a required step in placing your first inventory purchase, which is the final item we complete in Module 3. This is crucial because now is the moment to order a very small amount of merchandise, separating those taking action from those who only gather information. You'll need to get some merchandise to FBA before you can start selling.

### **Tips for Success** 

It's difficult to cover every supplier/product situation due to the wide variety of suppliers, the supplier's location, the product being ordered, labelling requirements, design requirements, packaging requirements, and ordering requirements. Believe me, you'll be able to navigate this in no time, but from a training standpoint, it's difficult to create training that is tailored to every single, minor situation you may encounter.

Since that is true, here are some guidelines you can follow to maximize your performance: 

#### **A. Get as Much Information as Possible From Suppliers** 

You may have a lot of questions for us regarding specific product situations, labelling, and design specifications. The best source of information for those types of questions will come directly from suppliers, as suppliers are required to be knowledgeable about that type of information. Therefore, you should inquire as to what information must be included on the label and what the label design specifications are. This will be discussed in greater detail in a subsequent lesson in this module. You can learn a great deal from suppliers, even if you do not end up purchasing from them. That is why, whenever possible, I recommend contacting suppliers by phone.

#### **B. Don’t Be Afraid to Fail or Take Risks** 

In this more intensive module, where you are required to take a lot of action and start taking some risks (e.g. ordering some inventory), do not be afraid to fail or take risks. Sometimes you may have an awkward interaction with a supplier, but don't worry about it. Everyone has been new at one point or another. Do it, learn from it, and move on. At the end of this Amazon Mastery Course, you will have been blown away by your progress.  

Everything starts to fall into place after you place your inventory order, and in some ways, it'll be easier from here on out because you'll already have your product. Because it's about marketing and promotion, you'll be able to do the majority of your work on Amazon and with our tools, which we'll provide in the second part of this course. All you have to do now is make sure your product inventory is in stock and use everything you've learned about Amazon marketing and promotion to keep your results growing at a rapid pace.
