The Amazon Listings Launch Formula Module show you how to improve your ranking, make your listings visible, and gain the trust of your ideal customers.

Here are the things we learned in this module:

1.  Know the list of things you need to do when launching your first Amazon Listings.
2.  Learn what is the importance of social reviews and how you get your first Amazon product reviews.
3.  Different ways to promote your listings. On or Off Amazon promotion.
4.  Use and create Blog posts to drive traffic to your Amazon Listings.
5.  Importance of Press Release and learn the Working Backward Approach by Amazon when promoting new products.
6.  Know the products you can launch that is under $100

The majority of the lessons discussed in this Module were designed to prepare you for the next module. Make sure you've learned everything we've talked about so far in order to move on to the next section.
