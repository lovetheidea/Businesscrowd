In this lesson, we'll go over how to use Amazon's Seller Support, which is available to all sellers, as well as different types of communication options to use and which situations are suitable for each.

### **Seller Support Overview** 

While Amazon is a customer-centric corporation, they also recognize that its sellers are an important component of its operation. They offer a dedicated Seller Support section to assist its sellers, as well as a good collection of online documentation to address any queries we could have. 

### **Accessing seller support** 

You can contact Seller Support by clicking the **"Get Support"** button on any screen where it appears. Depending on which screen you were on, it will frequently direct you to the relevant assistance section. If you don't see the button on your screen, go to the top and click on "**Help**," and it will appear.

When you go to the help section in Seller Central, you will be taken to their main support screen, where you can browse the links on the left for more information about different types of issues you may have.

To begin, simply choose the best choices for your problem and drill down into them until you reach the final contact screen, where you enter the details needed about your problem.

Then you need to fill in all of the needed contact information and then choose the best way of contact:

-   -   -   Email
        -   Phone

Choosing the most appropriate contact method.

**Phone**: When you choose this option, a Seller Support person will contact you (you do not have to phone them), and it is best used when you have an urgent need (be sure it is truly urgent) or something you'd like Seller Support to walk you through. Seller Support is great at this (teaching users how to do things in their account step by step), and here are some good examples of when you should call them:

-   -   -   What is the best way to start a parent-child relationship?
        -   What is the Brand Registry procedure?
        -   What are the many types of promotions and how do I know which one to choose?

**Email**: Selecting this option will start an email dialogue that may take some time to conclude due to the time it takes for each email to be responded to. This may be your best option if you are not in the United States and cannot get phone calls from Seller Support. Another reason to use this method is if you have a situation where you really need their response documented (for example, if you need clarification on an Amazon policy).

### **Special Seller Support Option – Removing Negative Seller Feedback** 

As mentioned in the previous lesson, practically all negative seller feedback may be erased by using the Contact Seller Support function and giving the order number for which the feedback is being sent. While you can go to Orders then Customer Feedback Problems to find the relevant form, there is another option:

-   -   -   Click on Customer Feedback inside your Seller Central account from the home page. Widget for measuring performance.
        -   To find negative vendor feedback, go to View All Feedback.
        -   When you discover one, make a note of the order number and duplicate it.
        -   Click Contact Seller Support at the bottom of the screen.

You will automatically be directed to the right form, where you can enter the order number and the reason why the feedback should be removed.
