LinkedIn isn’t just about creating a profile, it is about doing regular activities that can help you attract potential leads, and good contacts and build a reputation. Spending a few minutes per day doing regular activities on LinkedIn can be a great investment.

In this lesson, we look at the regular activities that should be done on a LinkedIn profile. We will also explore what you shouldn’t do.

## Regular Updates

Regular updates, or posts, are a great start. These should focus on numerous elements of your professional life. For instance, you should do updates on the latest work you’ve completed, new work that you’re undertaking, or perhaps new qualifications.

Updates can also include polls. Polls can be a great way to get engagement with followers and connections. Polls don’t have to be related to your work, some people create Whatever Wednesday polls where they ask less obvious and unrelated questions like Star Trek vs Star Wars or Dogs vs Cats.

## Regular Articles

Articles on LinkedIn are like blog posts. But they cannot be the same as any blog posts that you’ve created for your website. This would create a duplicate problem, which Google would then penalise your website for. Because LinkedIn is more likely to have a higher domain authority, it is likely to get precedence.

However, there are other options, for instance, you might want to do a summary of the blog articles you’ve written on your blog, including links to the blogs you’re referring to. Or you might want to do a shorter version of an existing blog post.

You can do something completely unique, like a roundup of industry or business news or articles from contacts. The latter of these adds an additional benefit of being one of your contacts, and perhaps improves engagement in the long-term.

## LinkedIn Groups

You can also join LinkedIn groups for relevant topics. In these groups, you can discuss the current going ons, respond to posts and create posts yourself.

The important thing in this is to ensure you’re engaging regularly in the conversations, not just starting them. The more conversations that you participate in, the better the results of your campaigns.

## What Not to Do!

There are also some things that you don’t want to be completing on LinkedIn. These things include the following:

-   Sending out loads of messages to potential leads to request work, this is essential spam.
-   Advertising your services in groups without asking permission 
-   Copy and publish articles from other websites, or third parties.
-   Leaving your profile empty for several days/weeks without any activity.
-   Publishing unprofessional news stories, although you might find that some personal stories are well-received.
-   Don’t publish content that looks too salesy.

## Regular LinkedIn Marketing Activities to Complete

There are numerous marketing activities that you should do on a regular basis on LinkedIn. These are listed above and should be done every week. The worst activity is to have a high level of activity and then drop off. This can cause significant issues with trustability and whether engagement levels will drop off.
