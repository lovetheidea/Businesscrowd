Email is often deemed a numbers game. The more subscribers you have, the more emails you send, and the more people who read your content - the better results you will get. Yet only one of those numbers that you have more control over - how many people read your content.

That is because you can’t control how many subscribe to your mailing list. You can make it easier, more enticing, and promote it to more people, but there is limited control. And while you can send more emails, you run the risk of falling into the spam arena, which will limit deliverability.

And making changes to either of these two has a limiting impact on the final result. Statistically, the average email growth rate is just 2.5% per year. But with copy, you can make drastic improvements that far exceed the natural email list growth rate. For instance, doubling your open rate can provide 95% more people reading your content compared to growing your mailing list.

For instance, the average open rate is 21.33%. While other statistics report higher open rates, these often include various touch points including welcoming emails, transaction emails, and review emails (which all tend to have higher open rates).

Some of the campaigns that I’ve written can have open rates of more than 40-50%. So effectively, one out of every two people on the list is reading the email content. And it is more than possible for anyone. But a compelling copy is critical to get it right.

## The Subject Line

The first factor for any email is to get the subject line right. The subject line is the first impression and you need to ensure that it tells the audience what the email is about, entices them to open the message, and offers value to the reader.

Research has proven several key aspects that can help to improve open rates for people.

-   Open rates increase by 67% when they have a number in them.
-   A question mark can increase open rates by 20%.
-   Using an emoji inside a subject line can improve open rates by 20%.
-   Adding a currency value will increase an open rate by 29%.
-   Using a prospect’s name inside the subject line can increase open rates by 39%.

So here are some interesting subject lines that match these criteria.

-   David, Save 15% off our App Development Service Today
-   Save £100 off our website development service today, David
-   Looking for a new website? 15% exclusive offer inside
-   ☺You’ll Smile Too When You See Your New App David

Not all of the key elements can be used in every subject line, you can use a couple of them in each subject line to ensure maximisation of the email open rate.

## Main Content

The main content of the email helps you to build trust. All content within the main body should reinforce what you’ve mentioned in the subject line, and expand on that. At the same time, you should consider some of the best practices:

-   Mentioning who you are.
-   Should be less than 200 words.
-   Include media (video or images).
-   Share buttons.
-   Add legal requirements (physical address, company registration numbers, etc.).

Try to be specific for emails, the fewer words you use, the better the results in many cases. Using videos in emails can improve conversions and engagement.

Share buttons are also encouraged because it is a great way to reach those who aren’t connected to your email marketing list.

## The Call to Action

The Call to Action is a piece of text, often included within a button that directs the reader to complete the next step in the journey. A call to action is essential for every email, even if it just redirects the reader to your blog or website.

Call-to-actions need to be optimised so more readers take action. The average click-through rate (the action we’re after) is 2.5%. But that isn’t representative of how many people who have read the email who have clicked, it is taken from the total list.

So an average mailing list of 500 contacts, with a click-through rate of 2.5% can expect 12-13 people to engage with the email.

Even a small change can drastically increase results. For instance, getting the click-through rate to 3.5% can see 17-18 people click through. And a 5% click-through rate can see 25 people click through.

The difference in sales can be amazing. If you go by the 1% conversion model, it would take just 4 emails to make a sale with a 5% click-through rate, which could be a sale a month. But 8 emails (or a sale every 2 months) with a 2.5% click-through rate.

To create a perfect call-to-action, there are several components that you should include:

-   Provide ownership to the reader
-   Create a sense of urgency
-   Give value to taking action
-   Use an action word within the call to action

In addition, you should avoid the following:

-   Don’t use Send or Submit
-   Make all call-to-actions more than one word
-   Learn More or Get Started should be used rarely
-   Don’t guilt trip your audience
-   Use only one call-to-action per email

So some of the best call-to-actions might look like this:

-   Claim Your 15% Discount Today
-   Download Your Free Report Before They Run Out
