### **Managing Your Team**

Now that you've assembled your team, they're looking to you for guidance. Ascertain that you have a thorough understanding of their tasks, roles, and responsibilities. They will wait for your direction or else you will be wasting your money.

The most common role that people forget or simply do not understand is to hire a **Project Manager**. Like what we have mentioned in the previous lesson this is going to be the key to your success. Your project manager will be in charge of working with the team and providing guidance. 

### **![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/2-300x184.png)**

Remember to create an online portal for your team to use, such as teamwork.com. This will assist your project manager or you in tracking what is going on in your business, such as your team's individual task progress, which would also inform you who is falling behind on deadlines, who is overloaded with tasks, and who is available for new tasks.

### **Project Manager**

The project manager is in charge of the day-to-day management of the project and must be knowledgeable about all aspects of project management. Project managers work on specific projects with definite outcomes, time constraints, and budget constraints. 

Usually, you have to pay them a bit more than what you are paying the rest of the team, but it is worth it. Spend your time and resources training the project manager to understand your business and your projects.

### **![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/1-300x184.png)**

Tasks that you can assign to your Project Manager:

-   Planning what work needs to be done, when it needs to be done, and who will do it
-   Examining and managing the risks associated with specific projects
-   Motivating team members and a second person to you who need to set good examples 
-   Delegating tasks to the right people
-   Coordinating work done by various people
-   Help you in assessing your team members' work progress
-   Ensuring that the project is completed on time and within budget
-   Dealing with changes to the project as they arise
-   Ensuring that the project meets the expected outcomes and benefits
-   Reports to you on project activity

### **Training Your Team**

Whether you are a new team business owner or a seasoned CEO, developing your team is an essential part of your business growth. It also does not apply solely to new hires. People need training and support throughout their careers to develop their skills and work effectively. 

**Create training material for the team.** 

It will be difficult to train your employees if it is only on your mind; therefore, make sure to create some training materials for your team and show them around your business. Don't assume that if you hire a highly experienced freelancer, they will already understand how you want them to handle your business. Please keep in mind that every business owner has a unique approach to running a business and managing a team.

This will take some time to create, but once you create them, you will only have to update them as needed but the time saved in training one by one is priceless.

List of training materials you may want to include:

-   -   -   Create training videos. 
        -   Training portal that you can easily give and take away access to. Build on a WordPress website with membership protection. Have your team document their tasks and processes or changes to the training portal. 
        -   Checklists
        -   Worksheets
        -   Documentation of their tasks processes and more.

Why is it so important to create training material? The first reason why it is important is if you have new hires, you can just send them through the training. It is important that your training is ongoing, as things change, make sure to update the training. When new team members are hired, the project manager is responsible for ensuring that they receive the necessary training.

### **End of Day Reports**

End-of-day reports (EOD Reports) are a type of business report document that is typically updated by employees to record all of their daily activities. A typical EOD report emphasizes a number of critical diurnal aspects such as:

-   Tasks completed within the day
-   Challenges encountered while carrying out tasks. In that way, you are aware of why the task has been delayed.
-   Potential issues have been identified. 
-   Indicates the current status of unfinished projects.
-   Displays completed tasks
-   The goals for tomorrow

This is a great tool for seeing what your team worked on that day. They are able to share what they have completed, what is currently in progress, and where they require assistance. This is very beneficial because you don't actually "see" them working.

For this report, you can use [Jotform.com](https://www.jotform.com/) ( FREE ). This can be added to your online project management tool for review by yourself and the project manager

**What To Put On The EOD**

-   -   -   Day/Date
        -   Completed Tasks
        -   Open Tasks
        -   Issues/Problems
        -   How productive was your day (1-10) and why?

You want to ensure that the end-of-day reports are simple enough that your team doesn't dread them but detailed enough that you (and your project manager) can see what was accomplished and where they need assistance.

**TIP:**  If you have more than one team member doing the same job ( 2 writers), have them see each other’s EOD reports and it will push them to do a better job than the other

### **Common Obstacles**

We cannot deny that there are numerous advantages and benefits to outsourcing certain business processes to offshore service providers, but there are also risks and trade-offs associated with this strategy. Listed below are some of the common problems or obstacles you might encounter when outsourcing.

**Time Difference**

When working with someone in a different timezone than you, this is a common stumbling block. It is possible that your team is working while you are sleeping, making communication difficult.

**Culture**

Understanding cultures is important; you should take the time to learn about your team's background in order to better manage them.

**Vacation**

Different countries celebrate different holidays, which are most likely not the same as yours. 

**Weather / Blackouts**

Depending on where they are (as in the Philippines), they are frequently hampered by weather-related issues that prevent them from working. There are also scheduled blackouts, which many areas must deal with.
