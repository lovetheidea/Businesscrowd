### **Why Your Amazon Description is Important** 

-   Converts visitors into customers. When someone clicks on your product title or image, they are directed to your product listing. What you put in your product description is one of the main things that will entice them to buy. So make sure to make the most of it.

Keep in mind that increasing your conversion rate will result in more traffic. Furthermore, a higher conversion rate leads to higher Amazon rankings. If your product converts better than one of your competitors in the same price range, it not only makes you more money but also makes Amazon more money. Amazon will then feature and rank your product higher. 

-   Allows your product to stand out from the competition.
-   Content for the sales page. This is where you should include the majority of your relevant keywords for product rankings.
-   Most of the sellers, don’t utilize their product descriptions. That is why you will have an advantage in this game.

### **How can I make my product description stand out if I don't use HTML tags?**

**1\. Put yourself in the shoes of your target audience.**

Investigate your intended audience. Consider yourself as an audience member; what do you believe they want to know about the product? Conduct some research and tailor each possible question they may have about your product. As previously stated, look at your competitors; yes, it is sneaky, but it will help you sort out the things you need to know to have an advantage; look at the same product you have and current reviews; what do customers always ask?

**2\. Keep it simple and avoid overselling.**

Yes, you read that correctly, everyone became ill after reading all those flowery selling words. Humanize your content, strike a balance, and connect with your audience.

**3\. Keywords, Keywords, Keywords.**

Remember how we talked about finding the right keywords to use for your product? This is the ideal situation for them. But don't just put all of your random keywords on a list; make it look natural.

**4\. Don’t plagiarize.**

We do recommend being sneaky with your competitors, but not in the sense of simply copying everything they have. That isn't the point here. Always ensure that your product description is one-of-a-kind and perfectly tailored to your product.

**5\. Proofread your description.**

Before publishing your description, double-check it for misspelt words and grammatical errors.

**6\. Include benefits, call-to-action**.

Don't forget to include what your product's benefits are to your customers and why they need your product.

**7\. Lastly, Join the A+ Content.**  

It may be necessary to be more adaptable when telling a story. Words alone aren't always sufficient. I'm sure you'll agree that we preferred to emphasize eye-candy content over fluffy words. Amazon allows you to use A+ Content, which provides design templates and allows sellers to add images and text to their product description listings.

### **What is A+ Content?**

A+ content is a premium content feature that enables sellers to change the product description of their branded ASINs by incorporating rich texts, videos, and images.

When used correctly, adding A+ content to your product detail pages can result in higher conversion rates, increased traffic, and increased sales. By using A+ content to create Amazon product descriptions, you'll help consumers digest your listing details with its easy-to-read format and images, preventing them from scrolling too quickly through the information you're using to persuade them.

### **Other Elements of a Product Detail Page** 

There are numerous fields to fill out when creating an Amazon product. Even though most of those fields are optional, you should concentrate on the ones that appear on the product page.

Because each product is different, it's difficult to provide specific examples or training for each one. If you recall, I showed you how to quickly create an Amazon product listing in Choosing the Right Product for your Amazon Business Module. Here in this module, you learned how to optimize your title, images, and product description. Those are the most important things to consider.

Other fields will appear when you add/set up your product. Fill out as many as you can. You are not required to complete all of them, but please complete as many as possible. You must do so.

Write like a salesperson (focus on the customer). Consider what your prospective customers might expect to read and what they might be interested in purchasing. 

And make certain that the ones you do fill out are **ACCURATE**. It's best to leave a field blank if you don't know the answer. In some fields, precision is not as important. If you don't have exact measurements or weights, that's fine. However, in more sensitive fields, such as voltage for an electronics product, you want to be precise.

Along with the title, description, etc., the following other fields are essential: 

-   -   **Features Bullets -** Seller can make up to five Feature Bullets because they appear at the top of the Amazon page, these are extremely important. Make certain that these sell the product. Think about what would really sell a person to want to buy your product.

![Features Bullets ](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Features-Bullets-300x173.png)

According to Amazon, this is where you can highlight the features of your products. As the final bullet point, you may include warranty information or included accessories.

REMEMBER: For each key feature of the product you must include its benefits. See the image below as the best example provided by Amazon.
