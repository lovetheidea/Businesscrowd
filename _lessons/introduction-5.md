Some people have had trouble finding products, suppliers, or even getting started. Everyone is dealing with problems. Some people will find it easier than others, depending on where they're from, the category and product they want to sell, and where their supplier is located, but everyone is working through these issues, and We want to congratulate you on sticking with it because now is when the real fun begins. Everything up to this point has been geared toward getting you a product on Amazon.

### **What you will learn in Amazon Listings Launch Formula?**

-   **Launching Your Amazon Product** 

Everything you've done up to this point has resulted in your product being listed on Amazon. The next step is to launch a marketing campaign.  

-   **Creating the Framework for Future Promotion** 

In this module, you will lay the groundwork for everything we'll do in the future to promote your Amazon product, not only in terms of your education but also in terms of your actual Amazon product.

-   **Sales and Reviews**

This sales and review database is extremely valuable for a variety of reasons. It will, for example, increase the credibility of your product and your Amazon account, as well as make everything more stable.
