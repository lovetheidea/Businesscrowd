## **What is Alibaba**

If you're unfamiliar with Alibaba, it's a third-party website that is unrelated to Amazon or anything else we do. It’s the most popular online shopping destination in the world's fastest-growing e-commerce market. It's a database that allows product vendors and manufacturers to connect with and interact with people who are interested in purchasing their goods. 

Some people are hesitant to use a service like Alibaba because it does business with a lot of foreign countries, such as China. People may be apprehensive about doing business with Alibaba, but I can assure you that it is a fantastic resource. They have numerous checks and balances in place to ensure that all of their suppliers are trustworthy. That being said, it is your responsibility to conduct due diligence. This means that at the end of the day, you are in charge of your own affairs. You must conduct checks and balances to ensure that you are working with reputable companies and that you understand how things operate.

Before you do anything else on Alibaba, I recommend visiting the [**Help Center**](https://service.alibaba.com/page/home?pageId=122&language=en) and then the [**Safety & Security Cente**r](https://activities.alibaba.com/alibaba/safe-security-center-account-protection.php). Check to see if your concerns have been addressed. You can either ask a question or browse the available data. The Help Center is full of useful information, but make sure you read through the Safety & Security Center first.

## **How to Begin with Alibaba** 

The first thing you need to do is create an account at Alibaba.com. This will allow you to reach out to their suppliers. Use this link to create an account at [Alibaba.com](https://passport.alibaba.com/icbu_login.htm?spm=a2700.8293689.scGlobalHomeHeader.6.33d067afCw19S4&tracelog=hd_signin)

After you create an Alibaba account, you can use the site in a number of ways. Using **Search Box**, let’s choose **Suppliers**.

![Search Box](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Search-Box-300x58.png)

Then, let’s type our keywords in the **search** field, e.g. "silicone spatula", and click **Search**. You'll notice some other options to filter down your search based on your reference such as:

-   Supplier Types, 
-   Sort by: Best Match or Response Rate
-   Location

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/unnamed-1-1-300x108.png)

Let’s don't worry about these things for a while. Our goal, for now, is to find as many product suppliers as possible. 

Now we want these results to be sorted by the criteria above the listings: **Supplier Types**, **Location, and Sort By whether Best Match or Response rate.**

There are two types of Supplier in Alibaba:

-   **Trade Assurance** is a free service offered by Alibaba.com that aims to build trust between buyers and sellers. Trade Assurance safeguards your interests in the event of a shipping or quality problem. 

 According to Alibaba, you're covered if:

-   -   -   Your products are not shipped on time as per the contract with your supplier
        -   Your products do not meet the quality standards as per the contract with your supplier. 

-   **Verified Supplier** is a supplier for whom independent third-party institutions have assessed, certified, and/or inspected certain aspects of its company profile, management system, production capabilities, and product and process controls via online and offline means. The verified badge will be shown along with the supplier profile.

 ![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Verified-Supplier-300x122.png)

There are some other things that are very important to understand. This is in relation to the product and the types of situations for which you could be held liable. If you're selling a cooking product in the United States, for example, you'll want to make sure it's FDA-certified. As a result, when looking for a product, you'll want to look for certifications that are relevant to your product. When I select FDA, my search will be narrowed even further.

## **Looking at the Products** 

There are a few details you should pay attention to when reviewing the products on Alibaba:

###  **Features:** 

The first one is the product **features**.

You must seek out products that are appropriate for you. When you click on a product, you'll see a picture of the item. Keep in mind that this is only one product. Because the supplier is likely to have other products, scroll down and read what they have, as well as the **Quick Details.**

It's critical that you've done your research at this point. For example, if you're looking for spatulas, make sure you've visited Amazon and looked at the other products. It is beneficial to read the negative reviews for those products in order to understand what problems the product may have. For example, if there are complaints that a spatula isn't heat resistant and non-sustainable, you can check the **Quick Details** for the Alibaba product you're interested in to see if this detail is taken into account.

![Quick Details for the Alibaba Product](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Quick-Details-for-the-Alibaba-Product-300x88.png)

All in all, it's good to know what features you're looking for in your product. 

### **Contact Details:** 

The next important thing to look at is the contact details. This will demonstrate how to contact the supplier; first, check the company's contact information to see if they have a website. Take advantage of this opportunity to look around their website to see if they have a legitimate business. Another benefit is that you can see all of their other products. By looking at these other products, you may come up with better ideas than your original product idea.

Here’s how: 

1.  1.  Search your desired product on the search bar make sure you are searching via the Supplier Dropdown menu.
    2.  A list of suppliers with your desired product will be shown on the page.
    3.  Under each Supplier, you can see **Contact Details.** Click it.
    4.  You will be directed to the Supplier Contact Information page. The company might have a website on Alibaba if it does not have its own website. This is just a page on Alibaba where you can find some extra information. 

![Supplier Contact Information page](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Supplier-Contact-Information-page-300x72.png)

The other reason why we need to see their website is that you can see all of the other products they have. Looking at these other products may provide you with better ideas than your original product idea.

An example is this one: [**Sheng Jie (Dongguan) Silicone Rubber Product Factory**](https://dgshengjie.en.alibaba.com/?spm=a2700.shop_co.112.1.5e33706d8Y8Jyt)  

In the case of a company that looks like it could be a viable option, we do not recommend contacting them until after doing some research. We must make sure to go through the above process for each product. Make sure everything looks good, that makes you feel comfortable with it, and that it looks like a solid option. 

If you like the product, get back on the initial product page and click on the **Compare** button below it on the Search Results page. Look at as many products as you can until you get a good solid list.  

![Compare button ](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Compare-button-300x122.png)

### **Compare:**  

Once you’ve found and selected compare for 20 products, you click on **Compare** in the little window that opens up. 

![Compare: ](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Compare.png)

When you click it, a new window will open, allowing you to view all of the products you've chosen. You're looking to compare prices and see which products look particularly appealing. If you want, you can just **select all** and then **contact the supplier**, but we like to double-check everything. We want to make sure that things are looking good in terms of price. Anything between $1 and $1.50, and even up to $2.00, in our opinion, is a good price. You'll also want to obtain as many quotes as possible. 

![contact the supplier](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/contact-the-supplier.png)

### **Contact Supplier:**  

When everything looks good and you're ready to proceed, click **Contact Supplier**. This will take you to a page where you can send a mass email to all of the suppliers. At this point, it's critical that you're clear and concise.

Remember, if you're looking for a $1.50 product, don't bother asking for a minimum order of 10 when they tell you it has to be 4000. Make an effort to be realistic. If they're giving you a minimum order quantity of 4000, you might be able to get 500. Don't try to go too low because they won't respond or take you seriously if you do.

You also want to make sure that when you communicate with them, it's not as if you're sending out one message to a bunch of suppliers, but rather as if you're meeting the needs of each individual supplier. For example, if my request was for much less than their minimum order quantity, I would not send a message to all of these people. Instead, I'd contact each of them on a one-on-one basis.

For instance, a product/supplier may offer their product for $0.49 to $1.21 per piece with a minimum order of 5000 pieces. With a price of $0.49 per piece and a minimum order of 1000 pieces, the total cost is only $490. When you compare this to another product that costs $1.35 to $1.65 per piece and requires a minimum order of 1000 pieces, you can see why you need to contact these suppliers individually.

When contacting a supplier, educate yourself on what they have to offer and tailor your email accordingly. Here's an example of an email:

Hello,

My name is \_\_\_\_\_\_ and I'm writing to you on behalf of my company. We sell on Amazon.com and are considering expanding our business.

In line with that, we'd like to include your spatulas in our product line. We are, however, concerned about starting with 5000 pieces. We are very aggressive and like to move forward, but we'd like to see if you'd be interested in collaborating with us to start with a lower piece count of 1000 units.

Please contact us at your earliest convenience and we will stay in touch.

I hope to hear from you soon.

Kind regards,

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

The point is to sound professional, to make sure you're properly representing yourself, to spell check your message, to use proper capitalization, and so on. Make it clear to them what you require of them.

## **Tips to Remember when working with Alibaba**

In order to get the most out of Alibaba, here are a few tips to remember: 

1.  **You may have to contact them several times.** For example, you could have contacted them and waited for a response, but nothing happened. You may need to contact them again after that. Try to send a better email and follow up this time. As a result, you must ensure that you follow up with them. This shows that you are a serious candidate for them.
2.  **You want to come across as knowledgeable and authoritative.** Even if you lack experience, speaking in terms that put the supplier at ease, as if you're an expert, will go a long way. This ability stems from ensuring that you understand what they're offering as well as what they've already stated. Do not request information that has already been provided to you. You want to communicate with them in a way that demonstrates you've done your research on who they are and what they have to offer.
3.  **Check your Alibaba messages as well as your email on a regular basis.** Also, make sure to check your spam folder in your email. In some cases, the email will be delivered directly to your spam folder. We don’t want to miss the chance of getting in touch with them. 
4.  **Be sure to respond as soon as possible.** When they respond, you reply a week later with, "_Sorry, I've been busy._" "_I'll get back to you as soon as possible."_ **There's no way they'll be taken seriously.** Do not be belligerent or rude. Be respectful to them and you'll receive the same respect from them.  

Once you've identified the best supplier at the best price, you'll begin working with them one by one. Maintain a professional demeanour in all of your communications. Believe me, it has a significant impact.
