### **The Steps for Adding a Sub Account** 

Sub-accounts are ideal for sellers who have business partners, workers, or outsourcers that want access to their account but require different capabilities than the account owner. This lesson will show you how to add sub-accounts to your seller central account and grant them the necessary permissions.

In order to invite new users:

1.  1.  Click User Permissions in the Settings menu.
    2.  Enter the new user's contact information and click Send invitation. Repeat for all new users you want to add.
    3.  Follow the instructions in the email for your new users.

**The User Permissions Screen** 

The User Permissions screen can be accessed from your Seller Central Account settings

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Image-1-Module-8-300x54.png)

Add the email address of the person you're granting access to and click **"Send Invitation."** They'll need to create a new Seller Central Account once they've received their invitation. They will be given a Confirmation Code at the conclusion of the setup.

Select **"Confirm"** once they provide it to you to make sure it matches what's in your User Permissions screen.

You can then set up their permissions.

You can edit user permissions.

**Editing User Permissions** 

They will have no access to anything by default. You'll need to give them permission to use the **"Edit"** feature next to their Sub Account to access the portions of Seller Central you want them to use:

To change the permissions of a new user, follow these steps:

1.  1.  1.  Click User Permissions in the Settings menu.
        2.  Click the Edit button next to the account you want to modify.
        3.  Click the button next to each tool for which you want the user to have access to your account.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Image-2-Module-8-300x125.png)

Select **"Continue"** when done.

Take note that user permissions are available only to Professional sellers
