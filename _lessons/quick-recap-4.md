In this lesson, you learned how to develop outstanding product titles that will drive massive amounts of keyword traffic to your site. There are numerous fields to fill out when creating an Amazon product. We're talking about Amazon keywords internally to Amazon Search Algorithm known as A9. This is where your product ranks when consumers search on Amazon for keywords related to your product. Copywriting is a strategy for creating persuasive content for Marketing and Sales to generate conversions and sales.

You're essentially writing words that will be used in the video, Amazon product descriptions and titles, and other places to entice someone to buy your advertised/marketed product. The first impression your buyers will have of you will be through written text. They will find your product in a list of other products and based on the effectiveness of your product title, they will click on your product link and read the product description. This lesson had taught you everything you need to know to grow your Amazon business.
