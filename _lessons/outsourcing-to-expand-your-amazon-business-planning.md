### **What you should expect in this lesson?**

In this lesson you will expect the following:

-   Deciding what to outsource
-   Making a plan for outsourcing
-   Typical outsourced tasks and roles

### **What Should You Outsource?**

-   Skills that you are not particularly good at or enjoy doing (example: writing)
-   Repetitive Tasks (back-linking, blog commenting)
-   Expert knowledge (example: SEO Search Engine Optimization, website building, SMM)
-   Activities that detract from your primary activities

### **Plan for Managing Activities**

-   Approach to assess which tasks to outsource
-   Process of assembling a team to complete the work
-   Process of monitoring the progress of completed work
-   Process of assessing completed work

The question you may be asking right now is who will manage all of these people. Make sure to hire a **Project Manager.** You can hire someone you can rely on and who understands the type of business you want him or her to manage. Must be approachable, a team player, good at making decisions, and so on.

### **Project Management**

Project management entails the planning and organization of a company's resources in order to complete a specific task, event, or duty. The stages of project management are as follows: planning, initiation, execution, monitoring, and completion. 

The person who does this task is called the project manager. They are an essential team member for managing not only the project but also the team. As a result, you only need to communicate and collaborate with the project manager. This prevents you from having to manage and work with the entire team.
