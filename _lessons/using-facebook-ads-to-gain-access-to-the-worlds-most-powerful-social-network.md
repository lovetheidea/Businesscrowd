The goal of this lesson is to show you how to use Facebook ads to tap into the most powerful social network. 

### **What is Facebook?**

Facebook is the most popular social media site, and it was designed to connect families and friends online. Over the years, this platform has always found ways to improve its user interface experience, including the addition of a feature that allows business owners to use this platform to showcase their businesses through their advertising section, rather than simply connecting people you know.

### **High-Level Strategy** 

As previously mentioned in this course, Amazon is an eCommerce platform where you can gain a large number of valuable customers. It also has millions of different sellers and constantly intensifying competition, making it difficult for sellers to drive sales and generate income, especially those who are just getting started.

Running Facebook Ads for Amazon can assist you in generating leads and conversions, increasing your income, growing your audience, and improving your rankings in Amazon search engine results. It is one of the most cost-effective and scalable ways to generate traffic outside of Amazon when used and executed correctly.

There are several different ways to utilize Facebook Ads, but the one covered here is: 

-   Creating a great offer for your product (has to be really good!) 
-   Putting that offer in front of the right audience via Facebook targeting 
-   Sending them straight to your product detail page on Amazon 
-   Sending them straight to your landing page where you can get valuable data on how they interact that can be useful when you will do your next advertisement.

### **Amazon Product Advertising Through Facebook**

With billions of active users each month, Facebook is widely regarded as one of the most popular social media platforms. With this large number of people, it’s impossible you can’t generate traffic through your listings.

Here are some of the advantages you can gain when you are driving traffic to your Amazon listings through Facebook Ads:

-   Specific interests and demographic groups, such as age, geographic location, and income level, can be targeted to increase conversion rates. And you can use this information in your future marketing efforts.
-   You can collect valuable customer information such as email addresses. 
-   Reaching out to new groups of people, even if they are less likely to buy right now, is still the best way to try and test out funnelling in those people who can be added to your conversion rate growth.
-   Can direct visitors to your Amazon listing or a landing page where you can collect their email in exchange for a coupon code.
-   Because most Amazon sellers do not drive traffic to their Amazon listings from outside sources, this can be your stepping stone to getting ahead of your competitors.

#### **Methods of using FB ads drive traffic to your Amazon listing**

-   **Directing traffic directly to your Amazon listing**

The disadvantage of driving traffic directly to your Amazon listing from Facebook ads is that people browsing Facebook aren't quite ready to buy yet, unlike Amazon scrollers.

You will not be able to drive a large volume of traffic with a single ad using this method. This is not to say that running this type of promotion is no longer profitable or that it is not regarded as a valuable source of traffic.

Yes, it will take some time to see results, but believe me when I say the end result is well worth the effort. Your conversion rate may also be low if your audience is still unfamiliar with your brand or product, which is why your ads must be informative. Include this in your advertising strategy to educate potential customers about you, your brand, and what you have to offer.

-   **FB ads Traffic →Landing Page → Amazon Listings**

First things first before we dive deeper into this method, what is Landing Page?

By just its name, we can see its purpose; it is where an audience lands on a specific page when they click on a link in an email or from any online ads such as Facebook ads, Google ads, Instagram ads, and so on. In short it is a standalone web page created specifically for a marketing or advertising campaign and is designed with a single focus or goal, known as a call to action.

Maybe you're having doubts about yourself right now. "Why do we still use this method?" Why not just send traffic directly to your listing from your Facebook Ads?" Sending traffic to a landing page before they see your Amazon listing not only allows you to collect customer emails, but it also filters out the vast majority of people who have no intention of purchasing. Using the landing page, you can also analyze your customers' behaviour, which you can use to improve the next advertisement you run.

#### **Task you need to do for your Amazon FB Ads**

**Task 1:** Make sure you already optimized your listings. We assume you already have an optimized listing by now, as we discussed previously if you recall. If not, we recommend that you return to this lesson (**Product List Optimization**) before moving on to the next task.

**Task 2:** A landing page, as we've discussed in previous sections, allows you to collect important information from your customers (e-mail addresses), filter traffic, and encourage or incentivize sales through various offers.

We recommend the following to ensure that your landing page complies with Facebook's terms:

-   -   -   Your landing page should be hosted on your own website.
        -   Include a privacy link on your landing page.
        -   Include links away from your landing page in your navigation.

**How to create an Optimized landing page?**

-   -   -   **Determine the purpose of your landing page.** Set the specific goal you want to achieve with this landing page. For us, we are going to create a landing page to generate traffic through our Amazon listing.
        -   **Use action words in your headline.** Having that eye-catching and intriguing kicker headline is a sure-fire way to win.
        -   **Make your offer as clear as possible.** Create content that is simple to understand while still emphasizing the main benefits of your products.
        -   **Make the content brief and straightforward.** Avoid using fluffy and flowery words that, instead of directing traffic to your listing, cause them to avert and flee.
        -   **A captivating video or image is required.** Keep in mind that the majority of viewers are drawn to eye candy content.
        -   **Make certain that the landing page is mobile-friendly.** Keep in mind that the vast majority of people use their mobile phones to access Facebook. And it's likely that they'll see your ads while still on their phone.
        -   **Don’t forget to add a fantastic Call to Action.** Always keep in mind to tell your viewers what should they do next.
        -   **Use a distinct URL.** And make sure that your landing page is relevant to your ads.
        -   **Include any social proof** that will lend credibility to your brand.
        -   Finally, make certain that your autoresponders, thank you page, and any other key elements that require a response or action are operational.

#### **Setting Up FB Ads for Amazon**

**Step 1: Create Facebook Page for your business**

Nowadays people even use social media to check the legitimacy of businesses from which they want to buy offers or products. We have various social media platforms that you can use to promote your business, one of which is the Facebook Social Media Platform. If you don’t have any social media pages for your business yet, then this is the right time to create one. You can start on this one: [Facebook page creation](https://www.facebook.com/business/pages/set-up). 

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img4-300x115.png)

With the growing number of businesses promoting their products and services online, Facebook decided to add a feature called the Facebook Business page where business owners can promote their own businesses. Moreover, it is an excellent place to cultivate an audience, create ads, engage with their customers, and so on. This is where you will direct Facebook traffic. You can completely customize it to reflect your brand and include links to other social media platforms as well as your website. It is FREE to create so we recommend that you make the most of it by posting not only your company's promotions, but also information about your products, accomplishments, and so on (engagement is important).

Owners can even add administrators to their page to assist them in managing it. If you have two or more businesses then you can create a business page for each.

**Step 2: Create Your ad Campaign** 

-   -   -   This [link](https://www.facebook.com/ads/manager) will head over you to the ads manager. From there select “**Campaigns”**.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img5-300x22.png)

-   -   -   Click “**Create”.** There will be a pop-up tab that will be shown, this tab will be asking about your marketing objective. Here you will need to choose “**Conversions**” under the Conversion section.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img6.-300x214.png)

-   -   -   Click “**Continue**”, on the next page you can edit your campaign name.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img7-300x300.png)

-   -   -   When you’re done click “**Next**” then on the next page you will need to choose your conversion event location, select “**website**”

           **Note:** The interface may look slightly different, as Facebook is constantly improving and upgrading its systems, but the general functionality should be the same. 

**Step 3: Set your Budget and Schedule**

The advantage of running this type of ad is that you can keep your costs under control. You will not be forced to spend money you cannot afford, and you will be the one to decide how much money you can spend on this ad.

You can start small while you figure out what works for business marketing. Don't forget to include an end date in your ad, and make sure it corresponds to the end date on your landing page.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img8-300x213.png)

**Step 4: Set your Target Audience** 

This is the most important and crucial part of your campaign. In order to market your listing successfully, you need to target the right audience. Try to make it as specific as possible to gather more interest.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img9-300x217.png)

-   -   -   Here you can also edit the specific age range, location, and gender of your target audience. This will help Facebook to show your ad to the right audience based on the categories you have added.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img10-300x272.png)

-   -   -   In the “**Detailed Targeting**” section you can add the demographics, interests, and behaviour of your target audience. Make sure to include Amazon.com here to target those who are with similar interests on this website.

**Step 5: Ad Creative Section**

Since you are done setting your detailed target audience, as well as your budget now you need to proceed with this section. 

-   -   -   First, you will be directed to a page where you need to choose the page where you want to show your ad.
        -   Next, choose your ad structure whether it’s a carousel, a single image or video, or a collection of images. 

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img11-300x228.png)

-   -   -   Under the **Ad creative** section, you can include images, add videos, (they also have the option to create a video in this section), and your ad copy. Make sure to include eye-catching images or videos and a straight to the point and engaging ad copy.  

Fill in the following fields: 

1.  1.  1.  1.  **URL:** The link that you will be sending people to. Here, it will be a link to your product detail page on Amazon. 
            2.  **Post Text:** This ad will appear above your image and should grab the attention of the customer.
            3.  **Call to Action:** Choose the button you want to appear. We chose "Shop Now" for this ad.
            4.  **Link Headline:** This will be the headline that appears below your picture, similar to the headline in a newspaper. 
            5.  **Display Link:** This can be the same URL as yours, but if your link is particularly long, you can make it appear better by shortening it. It's quite acceptable to utilize your URL> for the time being. 
            6.  **Description:** It will appear directly beneath your Link Headline and should inform your buyer of the Promotion Code that they must input when visiting your product page.
            7.  **Image:** Upload the image that will be used in your ad. 

**IMPORTANT:** Amazon has a strict rule about graphics in ads that do not contain more than 20% text. They want their photographs to be eye-catching, not like a billboard with text all over it. That's why you already have spaces to input your ad copy. If your ad is rejected, this is most likely the reason, and you'll need to rework your image to remove as much text as possible (if any). Choose "Create Ad" from the drop-down menu.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img12-224x300.png)

-   -   -   When you're finished with the preceding section, you can specify where you want your traffic to go. Either to your Amazon listings or your landing page. Include the URL of the page to which you want your traffic to be directed.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img13-258x300.png)

Once you've completed everything and are confident in the information you've provided. You can now press the "Publish" button. That's all, CONGRATULATIONS! Your advertisements are now ready to go live. You only need to wait for Facebook to approve it, and you're done. Your next task will be to monitor the progress of your ads, keep track of their effectiveness (for future ad creation), and make any necessary adjustments.
