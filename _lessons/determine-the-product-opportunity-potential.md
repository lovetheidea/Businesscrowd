In this lesson, you learn how to quickly determine a potential Amazon product you have or are thinking of creating, so you can decide whether it is worth pursuing. 

![Determine the Product Opportunity Potential](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Determine-the-Product-Opportunity-Potential-300x185.png)

## **3 reasons why** **_Product Opportunity Potential_** **is useful to know:** 

-   **You’re already selling one or more products (pick one)**: I've shown you how to find good Amazon chances for selling physical things, but perhaps you already run an eCommerce site, sell products on Amazon, or have some physical product that you're selling and want to know if it's worth selling on Amazon utilizing the Private Label business model. If you sell a variety of products, you may be wondering which one to concentrate on because you should concentrate on only one for the time being. In this scenario, going through categories may not make sense, thus this is how you can decide whether or not any product is worth selling using this business strategy.
-    **You have access to a product**: You may have access to a product through another source; perhaps you know someone who manufactures or supplies it. 
-   **An opportunity comes your way to sell a product**: People may recognize you as an authority in selling products on Amazon in the future or right now. 

## **Evaluation Steps** 

1.  **Does the product have private label potential?** 
    

Can you make the product quickly and cheaply on your own? Because we are not in the business of selling other people's products, you want a product that can be placed inside packaging and labelled to make it your own, one-of-a-kind product. You're creating your own product brand, which will allow you to do a variety of things, including manage prices and make a lot more money. You do not want a large, technical item weighing 50 pounds because it will be difficult to ship, label, and handle. Smaller, non-mechanical items are preferable.

2.  **Determine the top keyword(s) for the Product** 
    

You can either use [Google's keywor](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/)d tool or look on Amazon directly to do this.

3.  **Search for keywords on Amazon** 
    

As soon as you've figured out what your best keyword is, you can do a quick search on Amazon to see what products are currently available. 

4.  **Look at Top 3-5 Products & Use Rules of Thumb** 
    

It entails evaluating the top three to five products using the rules of thumb to determine if they are worth pursuing. 

## **Rules of Thumb** 

-   **Bestseller Ranking in Top-Level Category** 

a) 0-100: EXTREMELY GOOD   
b) 101-500: VERY GOOD   
c) 501-1,000: GOOD   
d) 1,001-5,000+: OK 

-   **\# of Reviews (Look at Top 3 Similar Products):** 
    -   0-50: VERY LOW COMPETITION 
    -   51-100: LOW COMPETITION 
    -   101-500: MODERATE COMPETITION 
    -   500 - 2000: HIGH COMPETITION
    -   2000+: VERY HIGH COMPETITION

Product Opportunity Evaluation Potential is a useful quality to have while starting your own Amazon business, both for coming up with ideas and for people proposing ideas to you. You'll rapidly understand how to decide whether or not a product is suitable for selling on Amazon.

There are some general guidelines that can help you analyze your product potential. Although not all exceptional possibilities will completely fulfill these criteria, attempt to get as near as feasible.

Remember, the goal is not to choose a PERFECT product, but rather to choose a GOOD one and run with it, learning along the way.

Overall, this appears to be a solid opportunity. If you were looking at a product that wasn't selling well (i.e. didn't have a decent Best Seller Rank) or was the only one selling well while the rest were terrible, it probably wouldn't be a product you want to pursue.
