The goal of this lesson is to understand why your seller account is deactivated, how to avoid it, and how to get your Seller Central account reinstated if you have had a Deactivated Seller Central account.

# **Why does an account get deactivated?** 

An Amazon Seller account can get banned for a lot of different reasons. Some examples are: 

-   Supplier issues 
-   Use of an unreliable drop-shipper (which means you couldn’t control fulfillment)
-   Administrative/account management mishandling 
-   Lack of knowledge (things you were supposed to do that didn’t get done, like responding to people’s messages quickly, not entering tracking numbers, etc.) 
-   Poor Reviews (Remember: Unhappy customers lead to trouble)
-   Violation of Amazon policy
-   Creating Multiple Seller Account

As you can see, there are a few different reasons why Amazon Seller Central accounts can get suspended, but if you do what we teach you, it won’t happen to you. That's why if this is not your situation, then don't worry about this as it doesn't really apply. If it does happen to you then you have this lesson as a recovery resource, and for the people who have had this happen, this is definitely for you. 

# **How to Recover From a Deactivated Account** 

Sellers who have been suspended are frequently frustrated by the fact that they are losing money every minute their account is suspended. You might think, OK, I'll just make a new one! — **Oops!** That's not quite as simple as that, and it's also against Amazon's Terms and Conditions.

When dealing with a deactivated seller account, the few things you should do are: 

-   understanding why your account was suspended, 
-   reviewing your seller performance notification within Seller Central to determine the cause of your suspension, and
-    developing a **Plan of Action** to reinstate your account. What is a Plan of Action? In your suspension letters, you should include a plan of action (POA). It's a way for you to get your Amazon seller account back up and running.

According to [Amazon’s Guidelines](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/help.html?itemID=201623610&language=en_US&ref=efph_201623610_relt_G201623610), your plan of action should address the following three issues:

1.  What was the root cause of the problem?
2.  What steps will you take to resolve the problem?
3.  What steps will you take to avoid future problems?

You must ensure that you follow Amazon's guidelines when developing your plan of action. Your plan of action must be thoroughly thought out. Here are some things to consider when developing your plan of action.

-   **Keep it simple and to the point.** Every day, Amazon receives thousands of appeal letters, and the last thing Amazon wants to read is a lengthy explanation and unnecessary rants. It's best to keep the content factual and straightforward. State exactly what is required of you, including the root cause, actions you will take to resolve the issue, and steps you will take to prevent the same thing from happening again. Rather than introducing your product, business, or customer, concentrate on the facts and events that led to the problem**.**

-   **Give a rationale for everything.** You will be informed of the reasons for your account's suspension when you receive your suspension notification. A variety of issues have resulted in some deactivations. If you want your Amazon seller account reinstated, your appeal letter should address everything in the suspension letter. It should go without saying that there is a fine line between explaining the situation and acting defensively about it. To avoid becoming overly defensive about your situation, structure your letter properly and in a professional tone.

-   **Include facts.** Make sure you have all of the evidence to back up your claims, such as correspondence, screenshots, and invoices. Include supporting documentation for all products or ASINs that are being evaluated.

-   **Highlight relevant sections in your supporting documents.** You may need to highlight these in your letter as well so that the person reviewing your appeals can see them more easily.

For the complete process of reinstatement of your seller account on Amazon, you may want to check this link: [**Appeal an account deactivation or listing removal**](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200370560?language=en_US&ref=efph_G200370560_cont_200205250)

Make sure you don't make the same mistakes you did with your reinstated seller account. Whatever you do with your newly reinstated account, don't do the same things you did with your old account because you already know it can get you in trouble, so don't make the same mistakes or you'll be wasting your time.

It is extremely simple to avoid suspension. Simply do what we teach: use Amazon Fulfillment, sell products Amazon likes, do the right things, and treat customers right. If you do all of that correctly and do not engage in dropshipping or other similar activities, you should be able to avoid suspension.

To wrap up this lesson, remember: 

-   Don't make the same mistakes as the last time. You really want to be in this for the long run. 
-   Do things right.  
-   Sell good products.  
-   Ship them on time (use FBA so you don't have to worry about that).  
-   Treat Amazon customers well.  
-   Do good customer service.  

There will always be times when you push your limits too far, and that's when you will get into trouble. Clearly, it isn't something you want to do and isn't a good strategy. Black hat stuff and pushing the limits are just short-term solutions. You want to invest for the long term to make big money, not just for the short term.
