### **Choosing your Brand Name** 

-   **Don’t try to be perfect** 
    

You can change your company's name at any time. Obviously, if your brand name is printed on product labels, you won't be able to change it until you order a new batch. Many people spend a lot of time trying to come up with the perfect brand name, but it isn't that difficult. The marketing you do outside of picking a brand name, such as targeting keywords, will have a bigger impact on your results.  

-   **Pick something that will be needed by your market** 
    

This is kind of obvious. You don't want something that has nothing to do with your market. Examine what other products are available and choose something that will appeal to your target market.

-   **Ideally, pick something with a domain available (godaddy.com)** 
    

You don’t, by any means, need to go buy the domain right now (you can if you want).  If you have a brand name in mind, go to godaddy.com and type it in to see if the dot-com is available. You'd like it to be so that you can use it in the future. You want something different, something that hasn't been done before.

-   **Do an Amazon search & trademark search**  
    

Simply type your brand name idea into the Amazon search bar to see if anyone else has already taken it. 

-   **Choose something that can easily remember**
    

-   **Don’t choose a brand name that will be Too similar to your competitor's brand name.**
    

Avoid using a business name that is too similar to the names of your competitors. This could confuse potential customers, and the worst part is that other businesses could accuse you of trademark infringement.

### **Choosing your Product Name** 

-   **Be descriptive! Pick something simple that resonates with your audience**  
    

You want to come up with something that will relate to your audience; something that they would expect. Don't make them guess what your product is. 

-   **Be Creative**
    

Give it a twist. Create a name for your product that is easy to remember and makes it sound creative, as long as the name serves the purpose of the product.

-   **Ideally, include the main keyword(s) in the product name**  
    

It'll most likely be just one word, but it could be multiple words. This will assist you on Amazon. It's not required, but it's preferable because your target audience is already looking for that keyword.

### **Questions to Ask Before Naming Your Product:**

1.  Is it better to have a long or short product name?
2.  How much is your product worth?
3.  Who is your target market? What kinds of things do they already buy?
4.  How do you think your customer responds to your product name? Do they prefer real words or come-up words?
5.  Is your product's name similar to that of your competitors?

**It's NOW the time to act.**

1.  Using the tips in this lesson, create a brand name for your single product.
2.  After you've decided on your brand name, come up with a name for your product.
3.  Make use of these in your product design and packaging. When you begin working with a designer or a supplier to create your product design and packaging, they will require this information.
