Ecommerce relies heavily on the review economy. Any product sold on the internet, particularly on Amazon, relies heavily on customer reviews. This is why you'll learn how to get reviews for your Amazon product as part of the Listing Launch Formula; primarily, so your product has some solid social proof.

![The Social Proof Reviews](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Listing-Launch-Image-2-300x185.png)

It's important to remember that you don't want to go out and buy fake reviews. People have used sites like fiverr.com in the past, which is not a good idea because it appears fake and can get your Amazon product and account into trouble. That is not something you should do. In fact, you are not required to do so.

When requesting a product review or seller feedback, Amazon follows a set of rules. Be sure to read those [rules](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/YRKB5RU3FS5TURN?language=en_US&ref=efph_YRKB5RU3FS5TURN_cont_201972140) carefully. The question you may have right now is, “**How can I get reviews of my product and my seller account is still new here on Amazon**.” Worry no longer, watch the video below to know the options you may use for getting your first Amazon reviews:

### **Early Reviewer Program**

Amazon launched this program to assist sellers in obtaining their first reviews. This program encourages customers who have already purchased a product to share their genuine experience with that product, whether it is a 1-star or 5-star review. 

**How does it work?** Amazon contacts randomly selected buyers of participating products and requests a review. This program is beneficial to both sellers and buyers. Buyers are compensated with a $1-3 gift voucher, sellers gain exposure, credibility, and ranking, and the most important part is that Amazon provides a big orange badge on reviews for participating products, increasing trust in the product and brand. 

**How can I join this program?** To participate, go to 'Seller Central > Advertising > Early Reviewer Program' and submit your product**.** If your product has fewer than five reviews and costs at least $15, you will be eligible.

**Does it cost a fee?** Unfortunately, Yes. Each eligible product costs $60 (plus applicable taxes), and Amazon charges this fee from the first review generated by the program.

### **Amazon Vine Program**

Amazon Vine invites Amazon's most trusted reviewers to share their thoughts on new and pre-release items, allowing their fellow customers to make more informed purchasing decisions. Customers are invited to become Vine Voices by Amazon based on their reviewer rank, which reflects the quality and usefulness of their reviews as judged by other Amazon customers.

**How does it work?** Vine members receive free products from participating vendors who have submitted their products to the program. Vine reviews are the unbiased opinions of Vine Voices. The seller does not have the authority to influence, change, or edit the reviews. Vendors can usually expect detailed and well-articulated reviews because the Amazon Vine program is by invitation only for high-quality reviewers. 

**How can I join this program?** 

Sellers who want to participate in Amazon Vine must:

-   -   -   Be sure to have your brand registered in the Amazon Brand Registry.
        -   Have fewer than 30 reviews on the product detail page.
        -   Have a buyable FBA offer in 'New' condition.
        -   It must not be an adult product.
        -   At the time of enrollment, the service had already begun.
        -   Have stock on hand.
        -   Have an image and a description ready.
        -   Willing to give away each product for free

When you have the required items above. These are the next thing you need to do:

-   -   -   Navigate to the Advertising tab > Vine in Seller Central.
        -   If you have a brand registered, you will see a 'Enroll in Amazon Vine' button. 
        -   On the following screen, enter your ASIN and then click Begin Enrollment.

**Does it cost a fee?** It used to be free, but now it costs a penny. For each parent ASIN you enrol, you will be charged a $200 enrollment fee.

### **Reviews from your own eCommerce website**

This strategy is intended for those who already have an eCommerce store before opening an Amazon store. (If you don't have any, don't worry because the first two methods of getting your first product reviews are also good and one of the best starting points.) You can start from there to get some truthful and honest feedback on your product from your legitimate customers and share them on your social media platform, directing buyers to your Amazon listing. Having a caption that informs your followers that you are now on Amazon to cater to more customers worldwide from there you can now have these proofs that can tell your new target customers on Amazon that you are a reliable and trusted merchant. 

#### **Some Questions to Ask Them When They Write the Review**

They can comment on: 

-   -   -   Product Appearance is the packaging, design, and actual product of the product pleasing to the eye? 

-   -   -   Did the product fulfil its promises?
        -   Shipping. Does the item arrive in full condition, does it arrive on time, etc., 
        -   Usability of Product 
        -   What they appreciate about the product
        -   Why they would recommend it to other people 

Here are some sample email templates for product reviews that you can send to people who have used your products legitimately. The advantage here is that because it's your own website and store, you can choose to get reviews from people who are extremely satisfied with your products.

### **Email Template for Requesting Reviews from Your Customers**

**Email Template 1**

**Subject Line:** Enjoy a better experience – Help us in improving our product.

Hi \[CUSTOMER'S NAME\],

Thank you for choosing \[BUSINESS NAME\]. We are committed to providing the highest quality to our customers and your feedback is one way to make a difference.

Will you please take a few moments to provide your honest feedback on our Store <insert direct URL>?

Please contact us if you have any immediate feedback or concerns about your \[purchased product or service\]. We want to hear from you and are happy to answer any questions you may have.

Thank you for your time and consideration!

\[YOUR FIRST NAME\]

\[COMPANY NAME\]

**Email Template 2**

**Subject Line:** Help us offer you better results

Hello, \[CUSTOMER NAME\],

Thank you for \[describe the client relationship, whether loyal or new\]. It has been my pleasure to offer you quality products from our \[BUSINESS NAME\].

Online reviews are remarkably important to businesses like ours in today's digital world and educate others about us. 

We value your honest feedback; would you mind taking a few moments to leave us a review?

You can leave us a review by clicking this link <direct URL to your online eCommerce store>.

Thank you so much for taking the time out of your day to help us!

Best wishes,

\[YOUR NAME\]

**Please note that you are not allowed to:** Specifically, ask anyone to give you a positive review. You must always ask for honest product feedback.
