**IMPORTANT NOTE:** If you already have a product on Amazon or are in the process of launching one, **IGNORE THIS LIST** (for now). 

If you've already invested the time and effort necessary to research, source, and launch your existing product, there's no reason to choose from this list. Your current opportunity is just as good, if not better than any on this list.

Don’t hurt your progress by trying to start all over with one of these products. Simply continue to market your existing product and prepare for some MASSIVE results!

# **Who This List is For**

Finding a good product to sell or a good supplier to start with has proven difficult for some people – perhaps because they had trouble getting their Seller Central to account set up and had to scramble to catch up, or because they spent a lot of time chasing a product opportunity that didn't pan out (couldn't find a supplier, etc.).

So, in order to assist everyone in getting a product live on Amazon – to be able to apply everything in this course and gain experience selling on Amazon – we've compiled a list of products that can be started for less than $100.

Once you understand the fundamentals of selling on Amazon and apply our advanced marketing systems, you can always source additional products and continue to build your Amazon gold mine.

# **Criteria for Products on this List** 

1.  Can be sourced domestically (in the USA) 
2.  Good sales potential on Amazon
3.  Can be privately labelled 
4.  Inexpensive to purchase and can be purchased in limited quantities 

Because of these criteria, the majority of the items on this list are related to health, beauty, or pets. The reason for this is that almost all other products are made in other countries due to lower manufacturing costs, but these products that go on or are inhumane or animal are closely monitored and regulated, and they almost always have to be made in the United States (or in other developed countries).

# **Important Notes About this List** 

The list is a list of main keywords to use for targeted product sourcing, as you can see below. You'll still need to find a supplier (which should be relatively simple) and complete all of the other steps in **Product Supplier for your Amazon Business Module.**

Don't get caught up in trying to find the perfect item from this list; they're all excellent. Simply choose one and run with it. Get it live on Amazon, start making money, and then start looking for other products once you've gotten it to a point where you're consistently making sales and staying in stock.

# **How to Get Started Minimize Startup Costs** 

We know people who have sourced almost all of these products and discovered that a new vendor can get started with a small quantity (10-20) and a low cost per unit (less than $5-$10) with the right supplier and the right negotiations.

You must, however, negotiate with the supplier and inform them that you will be increasing your sales volume soon and that you only want to test their products in a small quantity at first.

You should also keep your designing simple and try to batch design tasks to keep your startup costs low (e.g., have the manufacturer do the design, including a basic text logo OR have an inexpensive designer from Elance.com do the label design with a basic text logo).
