In order to sell physical products on Amazon, you need an Amazon Seller Central account. This lesson explains what it is and how to set it up.

![Amazon Seller Central: Selling on Amazon](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Image-3-300x185.png)

## **Amazon Seller Central** 

To sell on Amazon, you must create an account at what's called Amazon Seller Central. With this account, you actually sell physical goods on Amazon. To sign up, go to [https://sellercentral.amazon.com](https://sellercentral.amazon.com). If you haven't already, please review the lesson "**The Seller Central Setup Guide**"  to learn how to quickly set up an Amazon Seller account.

With a Seller Central account, you can sell pretty much any physical product. Although you can sell physical books, we'll focus on selling other kinds of physical products across Amazon's thousands of categories. So unless you're a wholesaler and someone else is selling your products for you, you will need this account.  

**TIP:** You'll want to keep this account separate from anything else you're doing on Amazon**.** It is possible to have several accounts on Amazon that serve different functions, such as a Kindle Publishing (KDP) account, a personal account for shopping or an Amazon Associate's affiliate account. I recommend keeping your Seller Central account separate from all of these. You may use the same email address, but even so, I recommend you use a different email address just to make everything completely separate.
