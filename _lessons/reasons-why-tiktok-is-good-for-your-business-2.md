## Connect With Influencers

Influencers are very present on TikTok. They publish content that varies from product reviews to challenges. These influencers can be very effective at building traffic for brands by just mentioning them to their followers.

You can see how brands like Squarespace and Amazon continuously use influencers to drive traffic to their website. Connecting with influencers can improve your brand’s visibility and help you attract more followers to your channel too.

## Repurposing Content

There are opportunities to create new content from already published updates on other platforms. You should not re-use content from these platforms, but you can look at editing the content, adjusting it, making it shorter or longer and then republishing it on TikTok.

Republishing content allows you to build links between different platforms.

## Great User Engagement

TikTok has one of the highest engagement rates online. The average user tends to spend about 95 minutes a day on the platform. And they engage with the videos. This time allows you to collect information about what audiences want, how they like interacting, and what products they like.

In addition, it gives you plenty of opportunities to build engagement points, such as comments, replies, and more with audiences.

## Budget Friendly

While some consider websites, SEO and email marketing as the most cost-effective, TikTok is one of the best for costs. If you’re looking to do some TikTok promotion, the CPM is much lower than other platforms such as Facebook, LinkedIn or other platforms.

Therefore, people who are on a tight budget might be able to get better results from TikTok.

## Versatility of Ads

For those looking to build a following using adverts on TikTok, there is a lot of versatility about how you can use adverts. These adverts can appear in different locations across the platform, so you can choose what your brand represents and improve your returns based on the placement of the advert.

You can also experiment with the ads to see what works best for your brand.

## Final Word: Why Use TikTok for Business

TikTok for business is critical for success. It allows brands to reach a large audience through influencers and core audiences. And it can help you sell products/services at a cost-effective rate through a trusted and highly engaged platform.
