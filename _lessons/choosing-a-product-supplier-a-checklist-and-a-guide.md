### **Things to double-check with your suppliers** 

-   Name of the Supplier Company
-   Company Contact Person
-   Contact Information: 
    -   -   Email Addresses, 
        -   Phone Number, 
        -   Website address, 
        -   Social Media, and 
        -   Other contact information
-   Supplier Address
-   Are they legitimate businesses?
-   Do they have your ideal product?
-   What are their Product Price Tiers?
-   Minimum Order Quantity
-   Shipping Method
-   Have they sent inventory to Amazon FBA before
-   Do they provide labelling and packaging services?
-   Do they provide product customization?
-   Payment Options (Type and Terms)
-   Certification (FDA and Etc., if applicable)
-   Turnaround time

### **Things to Consider When Choosing the Best Supplier on Alibaba.com**

1.  You can start your search by using more generic terms. Starting too specific with your search can result in eliminating good options. Be sure to look at the "Related Searches" section as well for other opportunities you may have missed.
2.  ![Search by Using More Generic Terms](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/search-by-using-more-generic-terms-300x28.png)**Search Filters:** Using the sidebar, you can filter your results based on specific regions, countries, categories, and more.

 The three primary ones that we use are:

**A. “Region of Suppliers”** This allows you to initially look at suppliers in your preferred regions.

       **“Country of Suppliers”** if you know there is a specific country that you only want to initially deal with.

![](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Country-of-Suppliers.png)

**B. “Categories”** so that you can be sure the products you are looking for are the correct type of products.

![Categories](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Categories.png)

**C. “Supplier Type”** allows you to choose your ideal supplier.

![Supplier Type](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Supplier-Type.png)

**D. “Product”** This lets you select a product that's either available for shipping or a product that includes free samples.

![Product](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Product.png)

**E.** Set the **“Min. Order”** and **“Price”**

![Min. Order and Price](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Min.-Order-and-Price.png)

**F. “Management Certification”** — so that you know that the products you need come from trusted and legit sources.

![Management Certification](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Management-Certification.png)

**G. “Product Certification”** guarantees that the products you are looking for are safe to use on the market.

![Product Certification](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Product-Certification.png)3\. To find verified manufacturers, you'll want to use the "Supplier Type" option. These options do help to increase the chances of a safe and positive trading experience because, for example, if a member has been a verified supplier for several years, they may be an even better trading partner because of their long history with Alibaba.

**NOTE:** You don't have to use manufacturers that have all of these options when using these options, but it's a good idea to start with all of them and then remove them if no qualified candidates are found. 

![Supplier Type](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Supplier-Type-1.png)

**A.Trade Assurance:** It is a free service offered by Alibaba.com that aims to build trust between buyers and sellers. Trade Assurance safeguards your interests in the event of a shipping or quality problem. 

**B. Verified Supplier:** They are a supplier for whom independent third-party institutions have assessed, certified, and/or inspected certain aspects of its company profile, management system, production capabilities, and product and process controls via online and offline means. The verified badge will be shown along with the supplier profile.

4\. **Company Name & Profile:** We recommend searching Google.com for the seller's company name and past history to see if there are any negative reports about them. Also, look at the company profile on Alibaba for more information, which will usually include a link to their website if one is available.

![Company Name & Profile](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Company-Name-Profile-300x134.png)

5. **Shipping Fee: It** is the fee for making each unit, including shipping it to your country.

![Shipping Fee](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Shipping-Fee-300x104.png)

6. **Min. Order (also known as MOQ or Minimum Order Quantity):** This is the minimum amount of inventory they have stated they will sell. Don't get too attached to this number at first. When you contact the supplier, they will often work with you and this number can be negotiated. The purpose of posting this is to separate tire-kickers from genuine buyers.

![Min. Order (also known as MOQ or Minimum Order Quantity)](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Min.-Order-also-known-as-MOQ-or-Minimum-Order-Quantity-300x110.png)

7\. **Response Time:** This is the time frame in which the company is expected to respond to the contact form. The lower the number, the more likely the company will respond quickly to your request. While this number is important to remember, don't let it stop you from making contact. Just keep in mind that larger companies may need to be contacted multiple times before responding.

![Response Time](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Response-Time-259x300.png)

8. **Images:** You should look at the product images, but don't just rely on the sample images provided in the initial search results. Most manufacturers do not list all of their products and upgrade options on their websites, so check out the company's website and ask them where you can see all of their products when you contact them. They will usually have them on their website, but some will send you a pdf catalog.

![Images](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Images-300x288.png)

**NOTE:** Make sure to ask the manufacturer what options are available for the products as well. Sometimes, they will only show you the cheapest option since they assume that is all you care about. You want to know if there are options available to make your product stand out from the competition, and adding a premium component is as simple as asking.

9. **Contact Supplier:** This is where you can use the provided form to contact the supplier for the first time. Make sure you come across as professional when contacting the supplier, and double-check your grammar and spelling. When contacting them for initial information, request their company registration number and business licenses, as well as their bank reference letters. This is a simple strategy to help ensure that you are dealing with a legitimate company. If they refuse to provide this information, they are most likely not a serious company.

![Contact Supplier](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Contact-Supplier-300x84.png)

10. **Buyers Help:** Review Alibaba’s buying [Help Center](https://service.alibaba.com/page/home?pageId=122&language=en) section to stay up to date on their recommendations for best practices plus they have an updated list of “Banned Paid Members” that is an updated list of companies that have been removed from Alibaba.com due to suspected fraudulent or other illegal activities reported by Alibaba members.
