### **Introduction to dashboards and shortcuts**

We've been going through and looking at different reports, and it's obvious that some of these metrics will be more helpful for your business than others. In certain instances, you may wish to monitor certain data on a regular basis to assess the health of your business or a particular marketing campaign. You can use dashboards and shortcuts to quickly access these metrics without having to navigate to a particular report.

#### **Dashboards**

Dashboards are flexible and may be used for different purposes. For example, you could create an overview of how your site is performing by displaying summaries of different reports as widgets together on a single page. Or you could gather a list of critical business metrics that show the state of your business at a glance or compare different reports side by side.

Click **“Customization,”** then **“Dashboards”** in the left-hand navigation to view the reports that you’ve collected. To create a new dashboard, click Create, select a blank or starter dashboard, and give your dashboard a name.

![Introduction to dashboards and shortcuts](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Introduction-to-dashboards-and-shortcuts-300x156.png)

You can add widgets to a dashboard by clicking **“Add Widget”**. This will allow you to name the widget and choose a visualization type for it to display on the dashboard. The data may be shown as a number, a timeline, a map, a table, a pie chart, or a bar graph.

![Dashboards](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Dashboards-300x194.png)

You can use some of these visualizations for standard or real-time metrics. Use the "Add a metric" pulldown menu to the right to search for and add the measure you wish to include. You can even add filters to the report widget once you've brought it into the dashboard, just like we did with the view level filters earlier.

You may style a dashboard once you've built it by choosing "Customize Dashboard" and selecting a layout. You may also move existing widgets across the dashboard by dragging and dropping them. Mousing over a widget reveals an edit icon, which you may use to modify the data entering the report widget. It also displays a delete icon, allowing you to remove the widget from the dashboard.

Dashboards are classified into two types: private and shared. Within that view, a private dashboard is only accessible to you. Anyone with access to that view may see a shared dashboard. Each user may have 20 private dashboards and 50 shared dashboards per view. If you share the dashboard with other people, they may customize what appears on their dashboard, but their changes are only visible to them. Another user cannot modify your initial shared dashboard.

![Dashboard](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Dashboard-300x133.png)

To share a dashboard, just click the **"Share"** button at the top. **"Share Template Link"** will give a link to your dashboard template that can be linked to any other view, but don't worry, no Analytics data will be included.

If you want to share your dashboard template with a larger audience, you can upload it to the **"Google Analytics Solutions Gallery"** by selecting **"Share in Solutions Gallery."** Google Analytics users may share various kinds of modifications, such as dashboards, in the Solutions Gallery. It's also a fantastic location to discover dashboard layouts that you can import and then personalize for your own company.

![Dashboards](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Dashboards-3-300x156.png)

#### **Save Reports**

You can also save reports to view later by clicking the **"Save"** button at the top of most reports. When you save a report, it includes any customizations you've made to it. If you've filtered the data table, for example, that filter will be applied automatically when you access the report from the Customization area under Saved Reports.

![Save Reports](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Save-Reports-300x157.png)

Use dashboards and Saved Reports to quickly access and share data that is important to your business. Check out the "Google Analytics Solutions Gallery" for some great ideas.
