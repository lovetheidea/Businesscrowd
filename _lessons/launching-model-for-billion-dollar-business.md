When a corporation intended to launch a major product, they had a few options in the past. First, they could launch a huge media campaign, spending a lot of money on advertising, or they could approach physical retail establishments (which is difficult with a new product with little credibility) and either tap into existing networks or go out and hustle themselves to get it done. However, in this training course, you learned how to use Amazon as a launching pad to develop a big product brand using the new product launch model.

**Quick Recap** 

**Step One:** 

Look for a niche in which you can create a product. 

**Step Two:** 

Source your product and create your own brand. 

**Step Three:** 

Driving traffic and sales for your product. 

**Step Four:** 

After you have stabilized the result of your first product, you can add more products to your brand. 

**Step Five:** 

Consider diversifying your product's revenue streams. The goal of this course is not to turn you into an Amazon seller, but to help you develop a product brand, with revenue diversification as a crucial component.

**Step Six:** 

Do you want to keep expanding your brand, introducing more goods, and increasing your marketing efforts (which is fine and a wonderful idea)? Or do you want to create a new brand in the same or a different market with what you've learned? You could even consider selling the brand in the long run. To truly develop a sellable brand, you'll need at least 12 months of reliable sales history, but that's a terrific start. You'll never make as much money running a business as you will selling it, according to a mentor we know. It's something to think about, and it's obviously a long-term strategy.
