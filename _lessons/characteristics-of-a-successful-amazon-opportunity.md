To build a successful business selling REAL products on Amazon, you must first have a product. This lesson teaches you what qualities to look for to find the best opportunities on Amazon that people are already looking for.

![Characteristics of a Successful Amazon Opportunity](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Characteristics-of-a-Successful-Amazon-Opportunity-300x185.png)

## **Qualities of a Successful Amazon Opportunity**

It's a big misconception that when people think about selling physical products they first need to find a product and then find a supplier. That’s actually a backward way of going about it. The best approach is to find the opportunity first.

**These 7 qualities will help you find the perfect opportunity:**

### **1\. Bestseller Top-Ranked Products**

If you found a product by searching for a category or a keyword, you're looking at that top product and evaluating how well it performs in its top-level category. This will provide you with your first indicator of the potential. That information may be found on Amazon.

### **2 . Bestseller Similar Products**

The general approach is to look for opportunities by browsing Amazon categories. Then, when you find a category that looks promising, you'll conduct a keyword search to see what other similar items are available and how well they perform in their top-level category.

**For example:**

If you found an iPhone and thought it may be a good opportunity, you would conduct a keyword search. Because it is an entirely brand-driven product, the iPhone is the only listing that is doing well. However, if you identified a more generic product and performed a keyword search, and discovered that there are 20 other products that are quite similar and all doing well, then it indicates there is a lot larger opportunity for individuals like you and me to capitalize on.

### **3\. Number of Reviews of Primary Product**

Reviews for popular items usually build naturally over time, but if you come across a product that is selling well but has a low amount of reviews, that could be a terrific opportunity. It is, in fact, a new chance that you can seize.

### **4\. Number of Reviews of Similar Products**

Even if the product you're considering has a lot of reviews, it doesn't imply it's a bad opportunity. If the top product has a lot of reviews but none of the others has, that could still be a great opportunity. Getting the second and third spots might still imply a lot of sales. On the other hand, if the following 20 products have a lot of reviews, it may be difficult to compete and you should search elsewhere.

### **5\. Selling Price**

We determined an optimal selling price between $10 and $50 through our own testing. When you find a product on Amazon that matches all of the other criteria and is priced between $10 and $50, that’s a real sweet spot. Purchasing inventory becomes more expensive if it is overly expensive. If it's too cheap, shipping charges can eat up your entire profit margin.

### **6\. Private Label Potential**

Can that product be turned into your own product brand? This will make more sense once we start walking through specific examples.

### **7\. Profit Margin**

You won't know what your profit margin will be until you begin the process of locating suppliers, but you should aim for 50 percent or higher. That's when all of your efforts will be rewarded. Price has a big impact on profit margin. You don't have to be a low-cost Amazon product, and I don't suggest it. I propose becoming a premium-priced product because you'll be able to have that premium-priced product rated above everyone else and make more money in the process if you use all you learn in this course.
