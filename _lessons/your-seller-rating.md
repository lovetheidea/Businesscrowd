Though your Amazon seller rating is not visible on the product listing page or even on your store page, you should always maintain it. Because your Amazon seller rating can be a game-changer for your company's success. If you want to build a solid and sustainable business, a good rating will be extremely beneficial.

### **Converting Ratings To Reviews**

As you sell more and more products you are going to get customers posting seller feedback that actually either contains a product review or at least elements of it. They do this simply because they do not understand the difference between seller feedback and a product review.

When this happens you will just need to contact them directly. The last thing you want to do is tell your customer that they did something wrong. So how do we get them to change their seller rating to a product review without blaming them?

So instead of telling them, they put the product review in the wrong place. We tell them that Amazon has put their product review in the wrong place!

Below is a skeletal system of the message you should send them; we don't want everyone sending out the same email, so it's up to you to fill it out in the style that suits you:

-   -   -   Say Hello, use their name (very important)
        -   Thank them for their seller rating
        -   Explain that it appears Amazon has put it in the wrong place, in your seller rating and not as a product review
        -   Ask VERY nicely if they could spare a couple of minutes to post it as a review
        -   Paste their seller feedback into the email
        -   Give them the link to submit a review for your product
        -   Tell them they can just copy and paste their feedback into the review form and it will only take them a couple of minutes
        -   Make sure you add that you understand they have a busy life but you would really appreciate their help
        -   Thank them again for their seller feedback and for being a customer

Use this method when you see a product review as a seller rating. Do not be disappointed if your customer does not change it; you will be asking for a review in your email sequence anyway, so hopefully, they will leave a product review then.

### **How to Remove Almost All Negative Seller Feedback**

The goal of this lesson is to teach a little bit about seller feedback and to show you how to remove your bad seller feedback from Amazon.

#### **Removing Negative Seller Feedback** 

The purpose of seller feedback is to allow buyers to express comments on the seller rather than the things supplied by the vendor. Customers, on the other hand, may utilize it to submit product feedback, which may result in a lower seller rating if the feedback is bad.

#### **What Is the Importance of Amazon Seller Feedback Removal**

During the previous module, we discussed Buy-Box and its importance of it. Having negative seller feedback will significantly reduce your buy-box winning percentage. 

In addition to that having low seller feedback, it can affect your selling rates. Like any other customer, you will be more confident in purchasing a product from a seller with a higher score than those with low ratings. For all of these reasons, it is critical that you actively manage your Amazon seller Feedback as a seller.

Amazon will only remove feedback in the following circumstances:

-   -   -   The feedback contains some obscene or profane language.
        -   Personal information is included in the review. 
        -   The feedback was supposed to be a product review rather than seller feedback. 

You can check your seller feedback by navigating to your Seller Central account, choosing **_Performance_**, and then Comments to ensure you're getting the right **_feedback_**. As previously stated, you should look for reviews on the seller (that's you! ), not the goods.

When evaluating the feedback, search for ratings of 3 or lower, as these are the ones that will help you lower your seller rating. You could even want to look at 4 and below because a 4 will lower your rating if you desire a 5-star rating.

So, once again, you'll want to seek feedback from the seller. If the bad feedback is about the product, it should not be included in seller feedback, and Amazon will remove it for you. To get rid of the incorrect feedback, do the following:

1.  1.  1.  Log into your Amazon Account
        2.  At the top menu, move your cursor on Performance and select Feedback.
        3.  Look for feedback that has a negative rating (3, maybe 4, below)
        4.  Open the drop-down menu under Actions on the right side and select “**Request Removal.**”
        5.  At the bottom of the page, click the "Contact Seller Support" link.
        6.  Select “**View Case**” to the right of the prompt. It will then redirect you to the Feedback Removal Request page, where you can select “**Get Help**.”
        7.  Lastly, write a response to Amazon explaining why they should strike the feedback.

Give Amazon a day or two to answer after you've performed these steps. If your request is refused and they say they don't want to remove the feedback, simply resubmit it since you'll most likely have a different Amazon rep reviewing it, one who may remove the remarks. 

Don't get too worked up if they still refuse to remove the feedback. The last option is to publicly respond to the negative feedback on your account. It is preferable to make a brief apology in writing. Make sure your response is consistent with the customer service brand you're promoting to your customers.

**Note: If you do this anytime you receive any bad feedback, you will be able to maintain a higher feedback rating than your competition.**
