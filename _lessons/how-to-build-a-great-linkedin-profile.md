LinkedIn is a business-centric social media platform, an excellent platform for promoting your credentials as a qualified freelancer. However, it’s often poorly used and understood. But it has the potential for quick changes that can significantly affect any business.

The start of any successful LinkedIn campaign is to build an excellent LinkedIn profile. One that can showcase your skills, experience, qualifications, and personality. This can be done through several different activities and amendments, which we’re going to cover in this article.

## The Headshot

The first factor is to consider your headshot. It is one of the first elements that will draw the attention of the LinkedIn user. These need to be very professional and there are certainly aspects that you can do to make sure that you portray yourself in the best light.

-   Dress smartly, as if you would in the office.
-   Look your best in the photo.
-   Use the best lighting.
-   Stand in front of a blank/neutral setting.
-   No action shots.

The headshot can be done professionally, as this might improve a user’s perception of you.

## Background Image

A good profile needs a background image. These images are different from the headshot and need to be more generic, but also relevant. A simple heading image that includes something related to your work, so something with a computer in it is perfect.

You could be more fancy, and create an image that includes awards, certificates, training, and something related to your work. But you don’t want to create an image that is too busy, as this can be very distracting and might reduce engagement with the rest of your profile.

## Headline

The headline is a professional text introduction to you, but it is more than a job title, which others use on the platform. There are several different ways that you can entice users to engage with you.

-   Create an elevator-pitch (how you add value and why they should work with you).
-   Share something personable.
-   Ditch the buzzwords or use them in non-standard ways.
-   Ask rhetorical questions to encourage a response.
-   Add a call-to-action.

There is a limit on the headline, at 120 characters. This is less than the meta description for SEO, so it demonstrates how short a space users have.

## A Summary (About) Section

A good ‘About’ section is a sales pitch that can help attract audiences to your services. The summary is less a pitch explaining what you can do, but a story about your career. Stories are more engaging and attract the attention of the audience. They can also be more personable and set you apart from others who are within the same niche.

You should also look at reducing buzzwords, such as ‘expert’, ‘certified’, and ‘passionate’, these are overused and can often just align you with others in the industry.

A good ‘about’ section will include some relevant skills and past projects. You might also want to show a passion for learning. This can be done in the About section as well as the qualifications section.

In this section, you can include information about your services and products.

## Featured

You can add a featured element that can highlight case studies. These can be documents that you’ve created yourself. This is a perfect location to add statistics to put projects into perspective and in context. For example, you can say when you’ve improved usage, reduced server loads, etc.

## Experience

It is important to note down your experience. Experience is really important, as it demonstrates your skills and that you’re trustworthy. And if you’ve worked with high-end clients, it can help to boost your valuation.

Include the length of time of each role.

## Qualifications

Qualifications are also an important part of your profile. You need to include all formal and information training you’ve had. The qualifications section is a great location for demonstrating a thirst for knowledge.

If you can, include images of any qualifications that you have.

## Final Word: How to Build a Great LinkedIn Profile

Building a good LinkedIn profile is critical for success. Above are some of the tips that you need to ensure that you have a great LinkedIn profile that will appear on searches and attract potential clients.
