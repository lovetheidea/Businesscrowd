Amazon's Lightning Deals are paid promotions in which a limited number of discounts are offered on an item for a short duration of time. They are featured on [Today's Deals](https://www.amazon.com/gp/goldbox) or [Prime Day](https://www.amazon.com/primeday)\* page. 

Note: Each Lightning Deals is limited to one per customer only. That can last from 4 to 12 hrs, or until the products are all claimed.

It has an energizing effect on your customers, making them want to purchase the product right away because such an opportunity cannot be passed up.

### **Setting Up your Amazon Lightning Deals**

To be eligible for lightning deals, both sellers and individual ASINs must meet the requirements needed.

 Requirements:

-   Seller overall ratings must be at least 3.5 stars.

-   The seller must be an Amazon Professional Seller with at least five Seller Feedback Ratings per month.

-   The Product must be eligible for Amazon
-   There must be enough inventory before sales can begin. To meet demand during the sale period, more than 20 items of the product must be on hand.
-   Include items variations.
-   The price of the item must be reduced by at least 15%.
-   Used products are not allowed to participate in Deals.

Useful link: [https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G202111490?language=en\_US](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G202111490?language=en_US)

If you meet the requirements, you can now set up your very own Lightning Deal. To launch the Lightning deal, take the following steps:

-   Login to your **Seller Central Account**. 
-   Go to the **Advertising** tab, under it choose **Deals.**
-   Click on **Create a new deal.**
-   Select the item you want to promote.
-   Set the time frame, you want your deals to run. Amazon will offer you set dates where you can run your deals. 
-   Set up the Lightning Deal. Submit the offer price, as well as item quantities and variations.
-   Lastly, once you have already reviewed the details of the deal you have added. You can now **Submit** your Deal.

Status of Lightning Deals mean:

-   **Draft** - Deal is not yet submitted.
-   **Under Review** - Your submitted deal is now been reviewed.
-   **Needs Edit** - You need to edit the Lightning Deals information provided and submit it again.
-   **Pending** - Your Deal is not yet scheduled, but eligible.
-   **Approved** \- Your deal is now approved and scheduled.
-   **Suppressed** - Not eligible.
