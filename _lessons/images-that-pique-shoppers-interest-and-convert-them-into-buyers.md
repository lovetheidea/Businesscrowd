One of the most important aspects of your Amazon listing is the images you use for your products. The trick is to use clear and high-quality images to entice Amazon customers to click on your product.

This lesson will teach you traffic strategies and tips, as well as how to get product images for your Amazon product.

![Images that pique shoppers' interest and convert them into buyers](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Module-4-images-5-300x185.png)

### **Why Your Amazon Product Images Are Important** 

-   **Increase Sales:** When people search for products using keywords, their eyes are often drawn to the product image in category listings. They will most likely read your product title after being drawn to your product image and then decide whether or not to click on your product.
-   **Improve Product Ranking:** A product page will rank higher in Amazon search results if it receives more clicks and generates a higher volume of sales. More clicks on product> Great Sales> Great Product ranking
-   **It brings the product to life (reduces drawbacks of online retail)**

If you provide high-quality images to the buyer and consider how they are displayed, your product will appear more real and reduce the buyer's risk.

#### **Here is a list of Amazon's Product Image Requirements:**

-   Images must accurately depict the product for sale.
-   The images must correspond to the product title.
-   The product must fill at least 85% of the image.
-   For the best zoom experience on detail pages, files must be 1600px or larger on the longest side. Zoom has been shown to increase sales. If you are unable to meet this requirement, the smallest file for zoom is 1000px and the smallest file for the site is 500px.
-   JPEG (.jpg or.jpeg), TIFF (.tif), PNG (.png), or GIF (.gif) file formats are required. JPEG is the preferred format. Animated. gifs are not supported by Amazon servers.
-   The background of the main images must be pure white (pure white blends in with the Amazon search and product detail pages—RGB colour values of 255, 255, 255).
-   The main images must be professional photographs of the actual product
-   The main images must show the entire product that is for sale.
-   The main image must show the product outside of its packaging. The main images of multi-pack clothing and accessories must be photographed flat (off-model).
-   The product must be shown on a human model in the main images for both women's and men's clothing.
-   All images of Kids & Baby clothing must be photographed flat (off-model).
-   Main images of shoes must show a single shoe, facing left at a 45-degree angle.

#### **Here is the list of DONTs in Amazon product Images:**

-   Images must not exceed 10,000px on the longest side.
-   Images must not be blurry, pixelated, or have jagged edges.
-   Nudity or sexually suggestive images are not permitted. Children's and baby's leotards, underwear, and swimwear must not be displayed on a human model.
-   Images must not include any Amazon logos or trademarks, variations, modifications, or anything confusingly similar to Amazon's logos and trademarks. This includes, but is not limited to, any words or logos with the terms AMAZON, PRIME, ALEXA, or the Amazon Smile design
-    There are no graphics, illustrations, mockups, or placeholders allowed. Excluded accessories or props that may confuse the customer are not permitted to be shown in the main images.
-   The main image of a clothing accessory must not show any part of a mannequin, regardless of its appearance (clear, solid-colour, flesh-toned, framework, or hanger).
-   Images must not include any badges used on Amazon, variations, modifications, or anything confusingly similar to such badges. This includes, but is not limited to, "Amazon's Choice," "Premium Choice," "Amazon Alexa," "Works with Amazon Alexa," "Best Seller," or "Top Seller". 
-   Main images must not include text, logos, borders, colour blocks, watermarks, or other graphics over the top of a product or in the background.
-   Main images must not include multiple views of a single product.
-   Main images must not show a human model that is sitting, kneeling, leaning, or lying down; however, expressing various physical mobilities with assistive technology is encouraged (wheelchairs, prosthetics, etc.).
-    Images must not touch or be cut off by the edge of the image frame, with exception of Jewelry (e.g. necklaces).
-   Boxes, bags, or cases should not appear in the image unless they are an important product feature.

### **Product Image Tips** 

-   **Use the maximum number of images (9). One MAIN product image and 8 alternative images**  
-   **Give importance to your main image:** Make sure to adhere to the main image requirements provided by amazon. Remember, this is the image that customers will see first when scrolling through Amazon search results. 
-   **Crisp, clear, and of the highest quality possible:** You can use actual product photographs or digital graphics that resemble your product. Because it's easier and my designers are all over the world, I rely heavily on digital graphics.
-   **Use Amazon's ZOOM feature to your advantage**. This feature enables your customer to explore the most appealing details of your product.
-   **Add QUALITY infographics of your product**
-   **Provide images for product installation or assembly**. Is your product going to require any assembly or installation? Show that process through images. 
-   **Get help from professionals.** Instead, if you have the funds, hire a professional graphic designer or photographer. This way, you can ensure that you receive and provide high-quality product images for your Amazon store. Previously, we talked about using Upwork to find a designer for your packaging. There are numerous freelancing platforms, such as Fiverr, where you can hire a professional.
