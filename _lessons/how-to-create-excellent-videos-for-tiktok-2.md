TikTok content focuses on videos and image content on a slideshow. Background music is critical to TikTok content, and the app has an excellent range of sounds to apply to videos. However, you can’t rely on the music of TikTok to ensure maximum performance on the platform. 

Instead, you need to create videos that can help you stand out from the crowd. A video's discovery can take weeks or months on the platform. So in this article, we look at how you can create better videos.

### Keep Videos Short and Sweet

The ideal length for TikTok videos is approximately 40-45 seconds. It isn’t long for videos to be, but that doesn’t mean they have to be low value. Within this time you can create content that has one of several purposes:

-   Inform
-   Educate
-   Entertain

### Don’t Waste Time

Consider every second of the TikTok videos. You should not, for instance, delay speaking at the beginning of the video. Audiences do not need to prepare to watch your content. Instead, you can focus on your content and speak from the start. Audiences can re-watch the content to find out what you are saying.

### Use Text

Nearly four in five people watch TikTok videos without the volume on. Therefore, they might not hear what you have to say. For this, you need to put key concepts into text on the video. Keep the text short. Transcripts/subtitles do not need to be word-for-word, just a sample.

And remember to consider reading time. Longer or smaller text requires more screen time, especially for small screens like phones.

### Be Entertaining

While we say that videos could inform, educate, or entertain, every video must have some entertainment value. Without value, videos will be boring and will not bring back audiences.

### Practice Your Voice

The best hosts of videos have excellent voice control, allowing for the tone of their voice to carry the messages of their videos. Focusing on certain words can improve the uptake of the content. Also, be sure you’re not using ‘pause’ words such as um. These pause words detract from the quality of the videos and lengthen them, making it more likely that someone will skip over your video.

You can take online courses that can help with your presentation skills.

### Look Into the Camera

The point of the video is to talk to the audience. To do this, look directly into the camera, which creates the impression of eye contact with your audience. It helps to build trust and engages the audience higher. Therefore, you can encourage more likes, comments, and shares with ease.

If you find it challenging to look into the video, try taking a few test videos and practice. Play them over to yourself and improve your viewing.

### Call to Actions

At the end of each video, consider the calls to action. You can ask people to share your content or to follow you. These options can be a great way to keep people engaged with you and reach a larger audience.

### Equipment

There isn’t a specific requirement for equipment on TikTok. The majority of the high-performance TikTokers use their phone. But do tests using your phone to check for sound and picture quality.

## Final Word: How to Create Excellent Videos for TikTok

The quality of your videos on TikTok is a massive factor in your brand’s success. Ensuring you have the right camera presence, edited elements onto the videos, and equipment can ensure high-value videos that resonate with audiences.
