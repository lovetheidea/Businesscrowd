The goal of this lesson is to provide recommendations for choosing keywords for your Amazon product. These will appear in your title, description, and later marketing strategies.

![Choosing the Best Keywords to Drive Amazon Sales](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Module-4-images-2-300x185.png)

### **Why Keywords Matter on Amazon** 

Keywords on Amazon are used to match customer search queries to relevant ads and products. The search box is now used by a large percentage of Amazon customers. They type in keywords that describe the products they want and look through the options that appear on the first few pages of search results. As a result, including high volume and, more importantly, relevant keywords in your listings is critical.

Amazon's search results will be more relevant if the search term is more specific and detailed. In the case of product listings, Amazon searches for keywords in several fields, including the product's name, description, and bullet points.

In the next module, you'll learn a detailed strategy for ranking for keywords on Amazon, which is probably the most important thing we'll teach you in this entire training course.

Right now, you'll learn how to choose keywords, and in subsequent articles in Module 4, you'll learn how to incorporate them into your product page.

In order to get buyers, you will use keywords for: 

-   Product title 
-   Product description 
-   Marketing campaigns 

### **How to Choose Keywords for Your Product** 

First things first, please remember we are not talking about SEO, such as getting the keywords you want for your product in Google. 

We're talking about Amazon keywords internally to Amazon Search Algorithm known as A9. This is where your product ranks when consumers search on Amazon for keywords related to your product. 

We want the highest traffic keyword + the lowest traffic keyword. The following is a live example of a product, in this case, an iPhone 5 case. We recommend that you work alongside as you go through this example. 

### **3 Steps to Spot the Perfect Keywords**

**1\. Do the Manual Search.**

In the Amazon search field type in your product name – “iPhone 13 pro case” – without pressing “Enter”. You’ll see the most relevant drop-down suggestions. The image below shows Amazon's suggested search, which shows a list of related keywords. At this point, we want to create a list of keywords that we could target in addition to our primary keyword.

![Steps to Spot the Perfect Keywords](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Step-1-Do-the-Manual-Search-300x137.png)

The next thing you need to do is to create a document that contains the following: 

**I. Product**

**Example:** iPhone 13 pro case

**II. Primary Keyword** 

**Example:** iPhone 13 pro case

**III. Other Keywords**

**Example:** iPhone pro case, iPhone pro case otterbox, iPhone pro case MagSafe, etc.,

**2\. Use Google as another source of keyword data**  

As covered in the First Part of the Amazon Mastery Course, the Google Keyword Planner can be a great source for evaluating and finding keywords for your product.

**3\. Use the sets of keywords throughout your listing and Monitor your performance**

When a customer searches for a product, they want the description to speak directly to their needs. Consider your buyer's journey and use data to connect a user's search terms from start to finish. Listings will boost conversions because you will be speaking to a specific audience that is ready to purchase. Remember to check the status of your sales 3 to 4 times per quarter to see if your product keywords are generating sales. If it is, that's fantastic; if it isn't, it's time to find and create new sets of keywords.
