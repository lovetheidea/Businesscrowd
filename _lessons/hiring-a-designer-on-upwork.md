You'll need to hire your own designer unless the supplier you're using will do it for you (which many wills). It's simple and inexpensive once you know how.

### ![Hiring a Designer on Upwork](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Module-3-Image-10-300x185.png)

### **Design** 

You may need to provide different specifications to the designer depending on the type of graphic design you require. Regardless of the type of packaging or labelling you require, you can obtain a template and/or example design from the product's (or packaging's) provider to give to the designer you hire.

### **Basic Design Specifications to Send to the Designer**

Basic details need to include: 

(1) size and shape; 

(2) file type (.jpg, .png, .psd, etc.); 

(3) any text needed on the label/packaging. 

With these 3 items, your designer will have little trouble designing your label or packaging. 

### **Finding Your Designer from Upwork** 

First and foremost, when hiring a freelancer, you must define your budget. When estimating project costs, consider all of the deliverables you anticipate, and then set a reasonable price.

If you are unsure about how much it is reasonable to pay a designer, ask your network how they price the same project you will have. You could also look online for related job postings for inspiration.

Next, start creating your job post. To hire a designer from Upwork.com you must have an account on Upwork, you must first go to [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) and click "Post Your Job". You can then post your job to Upwork once you've created your account. 

Include as much information as possible about your product and design specifications in your job description so you attract the most qualified candidates. It's important to indicate the final format of the design to make sure you get a designer who can use the right software (e.g., Photoshop, Illustrator). 

Select "Design & Multimedia" under "Category". Select "Graphic Design" under "Subcategory". You can select certain skills or leave them as default. 

Select "Fixed price" or “Hourly”. 

Choose "Less than $500" under "Budget".     

After clicking **Continue**, you do not have to do anything else with Other Options. 

### **Hiring your Freelance Designer**

Be sure to look at their profiles, portfolio, and ratings from their previous work to decide on a designer that best suits your project. 

Understand that when discussing project details, you are both negotiating an agreement that covers when the person can begin, what they will deliver when those deliverables are expected, and payment terms. Unlike an employee, you do not manage or tell freelancers how to do their work; they are in charge of everything.

Once you find a designer who meets your standards for the price (bid), work history, and design style, hire them to work on your design project.
