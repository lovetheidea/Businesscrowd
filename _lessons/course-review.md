Let’s go over what you’ve learned in Beginners' Guide to Google Analytics. We discussed:

-   how digital analytics can help your business
-   showed you how Google Analytics works
-   and walked you through how to create an account, install the tracking code, and set up different views with filters for testing.

You should also be familiar with how to navigate Analytics, read overviews and full reports, and set up dashboards and shortcuts to quickly find the data you need.

![Google Analytics Media](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Topic-5-300x185.png)

We hope you understand how to do basic analysis in the Audience, Acquisition, and Behavior reports as well. Finally, we discussed campaign tracking and how to set up goals to measure conversions.

We also showed how to link your Analytics and Google Ads accounts in order to track and optimize Google Ads campaigns.

But before we go, let's look at a few more ways to improve your business using Google Analytics data.

If you want to view the top-performing pages for new users, go to "Site Content" and select the "All Pages" report under Behavior. Then, as a secondary dimension, add "User Type" to see which of your top pages are being visited by new users. This can help inform your site's content and marketing campaign strategy in order to get even more users.

Go to the "Behavior" reports under "Site Content" and open the "Landing Pages" report to identify ineffective landing pages. Sort by "Bounce Rate" to see which pages are causing visitors to leave your site without engaging with it. Add a secondary dimension of "campaign" or "source medium" if you're running campaigns and want to connect these landing pages to your marketing efforts. Then you can examine which campaigns and landing pages are driving users away and make necessary changes.

It is also essential to understand how users interact with your digital marketing campaigns on different devices. If you'd like to view campaign data across devices, choose "Campaigns" and then "All Campaigns" from the Acquisition reports. Add a secondary dimension of "Device Category" to the report. It's now easier to track how users respond to your digital marketing campaigns across different devices.

You can also use geographical data and Goals to optimize your digital campaigns. Navigate to the Location report in Google Analytics' Geo section. Then, on the right, select a goal from the Conversions pull-down menu and sort by "Goal Conversion Rate." This will allow you to determine which cities or countries had the highest conversion rates and target those areas accordingly.

You should now be able to use Google Analytics to better understand your audience, increase the conversion rate of your website, and increase the efficiency of your digital marketing campaign. Don't forget to configure your Analytics account with multiple views for testing and filters to produce reliable, accurate data. Set up goals to track your website conversions, and make sure your marketing campaigns are properly tagged for tracking.
