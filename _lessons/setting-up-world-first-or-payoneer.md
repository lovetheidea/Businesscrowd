The purpose of this document is to show you how to get set up with World First and Payoneer. Payoneer and Worldfirst are the two most well-known companies used by Amazon sellers to receive payments from the United States and the United Kingdom.

These two services offer bank accounts that are Amazon-approved, but in slightly different ways, and if you plan on selling supplements, you need to use World First, which is our recommendation.

![Setting up World First or Payoneer](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Setting-up-World-First-or-Payoneer-300x185.png)

## **WorldFirst**

World First is actually a currency conversion service, but what they do is give you a bank account number you can use to be paid by Amazon. It isn't a normal bank account. You don't get a checkbook or credit card; it's just a way for Amazon to pay you in America, and then World First pays you where you live. Essentially, they convert the dollar payment from Amazon into your local currency and transfer it to your local bank.

## **Setting up your World First Account on your Amazon Seller Account**

When setting up your WorldFirst account on your Amazon Seller account, please make sure you use the same company and contact details held on your WorldFirst Account. 

1.  1.  Log in to your **Amazon Seller Account**
    2.  In **Settings**, Click **Account Info**
    3.  Under Payment Information select Deposit Methods
    4.  On the **Deposit Methods** page, click **Assign** to choose the marketplace you wish to link the bank account to.

-   -   -   If you would like to change your bank account, click **Replace deposit method** followed by **Add new deposit method**.
        -    If you are adding a bank account for the first time, click **Set Deposit Method**

5\. If this is your second to fifth bank account added, please verify your previous bank account details6. Enter bank account details

6\. Click **Set Deposit Method** to confirm. ‘Deposit method has been updated successfully’ will be shown

## **Payoneer**

Payoneer's setup is a little unique. Payoneer is a cutting-edge cross-border payments platform that enables businesses, online sellers, and freelancers to receive and make payments globally in the same way that they do locally. They give you a debit card, but the account is only for making payments; it is not a full-fledged bank account like World First. The Amazon sellers are allowed to receive payments or disbursements from the sales directly into their Payoneer account and transfer or withdraw that fund into the local bank accounts.

The disadvantage of using Payoneer is that they no longer accept payments into your account if you are only selling supplements, so if you are planning on entering the supplement market, or think that you might in the near future, go with World First.

Payoneer is accepted in the following marketplaces:

## **Setting Up Your Payoneer Account Through Amazon**

To start receiving Amazon Payments in your home currency, apply for a [Payoneer account](https://www.payoneer.com/solutions/) in Seller Central. Here’s how to log in:

1.  1.  Choose **Settings.** And click **Account Info**.
    2.  In the **Deposit Method** section click **Add.**
    3.  Select your home country.

Click **Register for Payoneer** to apply for a Payoneer account.

If you already have a Payoneer account, or once you receive a Payoneer account:

1.  1.  In the dropdown menu on the **Deposit Method** page. Select your **Bank Location Country.**
    2.  Enter the collection account details that Payoneer provides.
