When you're ready to implement, the information, training, and strategies will be available.

### **What we will learn in this module**

1.  **Optimize your Amazon product listing**  
    

After receiving the information from your supplier, you should have created a basic product listing covered in Amazon Mastery Course first part (Product Supplier for your Amazon Business Module). This does not mean your product is available for sale on Amazon; you want to wait until you have sufficient inventory before doing so. Once you've created your product listing, you can optimize it using the techniques covered in this module by:

-   -   Choosing Keywords 
    -   Title, Description, Images, and More 
    -   Creating your Sales Copy

You will get lots of keyword traffic and buyers this way.
