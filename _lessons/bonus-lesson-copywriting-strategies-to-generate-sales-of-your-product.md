### **Copywriting** 

Copywriting is a strategy for creating persuasive content for Marketing and Sales to generate conversions and sales. Emails, websites, advertisements, catalogues, and other forms of communication are examples. You're essentially writing words that will be used in the video, Amazon product descriptions and titles, and other places to entice someone to buy your advertised/marketed product.

The first impression your buyers will have of you will be through written text. They will find your product in a list of other products and based on the effectiveness of your product title (which works similarly to a blog headline), they will click on your product link, read the product description (which is essentially all sales copy), and, as a result of effective copywriting, they will purchase your product.

Good copywriting is how you get them to take it, whether it's buying your product, writing a review for you, or doing something else that will help you grow your Amazon business. This lesson will not teach you Copywriting mastery, but it will teach you everything you need to know to grow your Amazon business.

### **Basics** **of** **Copywriting** 

The goal of copywriting is to persuade and capture the customer's attention so that they will take the action you require of them. The goal of this Copywriting lesson we will be discussing now is to overcome a person's resistance to purchasing in order to persuade them to act. Let us learn the formula most marketers use for their sales copy: **AIDA Formula**

### **AIDA Formula**

In Copywriting we have this formula called AIDA which stands for Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.

### **Attention**

One of the most undervalued currencies in business is attention. Nobody will buy your product if no one reads your copy. That is why we need to grab the attention of our target market.

### **Interest**

There could be several reasons for this, but it's critical to consider which ones are most relevant to your product. This will assist you in developing a title, description, and other pertinent information for your product. This is where you will expand on what initially piqued the reader's interest, which is essential for maintaining interest.

### **Desire**

Consider what your readers or customers truly desire. Perhaps it's a primal desire they're not aware of, or it could be something more obvious.

In any case, focusing on that aspect at this point in your copy will have them jumping out of their seats for more. 

### **Action**

After capturing your target customer's attention, interest, and burning their desire. Now comes the crucial part: you must assist them in determining what the next step is. Of course, you've come here to generate sales for your Amazon product. This is where your CTA, or Call to Action, comes into play. 

### **Core Components** 

Here are the components we'll discuss in detail and learn how to apply each one to different aspects of your Amazon sales campaign: 

**1\. Headline** 

→ Attracts attention    
→ Makes people want to read more   
→ For Amazon, your product title and description headline, possibly your label.   
→ Search in Google for 'great advertising headlines'. You may not want to use any of these verbatims, but you can use different elements.   
→ Keep your headline short but powerful.

 **2. Describe the Problem** 

→ This is the time to make a connection with your readers. They have a core problem that they want to solve, and you are presenting that solution to them through your product.  
→ Here is how to find your target customer problem: Read the Amazon reviews of competitors   
→ Here people are sharing their needs, problems, and solutions related to their product. This will be a great source of information for you to use. 

**3\. Provide the Solution** 

→Make your product claim(s). This describes what your product will do for the buyer.   
→Indicate that your product will assist them in resolving their issue. Obviously, you want to avoid making false statements, especially when it comes to products like health care, weight loss, and so on. For additional information, you can consult the advertising regulations applicable to your specific product.

**4\. Credibility** 

→ Make readers feel secure about purchasing from you. If they are unfamiliar with you, there is a natural element of hesitation or uncertainty. We'll teach you how to create your own brand, among other things, in order to equip you with the 'brand power' necessary to establish credibility with your readers.

**6\. Benefits, then Features** 

→ States the list of your product features that will benefit your target customer

**7\. Your Offer** 

→ If you can include something in your product that sets you apart from the competition, it will help you stand out on Amazon, where so many people sell commoditized products. For instance, if you're selling a yoga mat and include a business card inside the packaging with a URL to a set of ten videos on how to perform various yoga exercises (you could hire someone on Craig's List or elsewhere to do this for you, or perhaps you know someone who would do it for free), you could add to the product description that the buyer will also receive a video series with their yoga mat.

 **8. Call to Action** 

→ Always assume your target customer doesn’t know what they want to do. Give them directions (“Scroll to the top of the page and click this button”) and tell them what to do and when to do it (e.g., “right now”). Do not say "Please buy..." or "If you buy..." Instead, use something like "When you click the buy button, you'll get this product delivered to you in "X-number" of days". This will help reduce any consumer uncertainty.
