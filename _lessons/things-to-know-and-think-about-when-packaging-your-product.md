We will be discussing packaging considerations. The information contained herein is not intended to be definitive or exhaustive; rather, it is to serve as a solid point of reference to familiarize you with the options and terminology you need to communicate with designers and suppliers. 

![Things to Know and Think About When Packaging your Product](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Module-3-Image-9-300x185.png)

Having unique packaging has other benefits: 

-   **More Easily Recognized And Remembered -** Consider how many times you have wandered into a store and simply been drawn to the packaging of the product, regardless of what it is.  

-   **Not Easy To Replicate -** If your packaging design is something that anyone can quickly and easily copy, then know that anyone can come along and copy your product as well. Having greater detail in design and packaging helps remedy this potential issue.  

Packaging is typically something that evolves as you grow your business. Too much customization from the start might not be a good idea because there are often unperceived changes in the product and developments that may only be discovered when you begin selling the product and obtaining customer feedback. Be careful not to blow your budget with the information you have, plus as you gain experience, understand your product and customers more and know what your competitors are doing, it’ll all combine to give you direction on how you would like your packaging to look and evolve. 

### **Things to think about when designing your product packaging**

-   **Hiring a Professional Designer -** Unless you are a designer or exceptionally talented, or have the required software, hire someone to do the work for you. A printing company needs the correct files and formats. Though it will cost a lot, it will be beneficial for you later on.  
-   **Audience -** Does your packaging appeal to your target audience? Think of the style, the colours, etc, that correspond with your demographics.  
-   **Readability -** It's important to keep an eye on the font colours and the background, as well as ensure that the font size is readable.  
-   **Does Your Packaging make sure your product is protected during shipment?** After all, that's what it's for. Different products necessitate varying levels of protection. When shipping, always assume the worst. Constant customer returns due to damaged products are inconvenient, no matter how good the packaging is.

### **What should I include?**

-   **Website URL / Company Email** 

-   **Address:** You must have a US address. Even though you are not located in the US, there are plenty of services that can give you a US address. 

-   **Phone Number:** Have a US phone number and make yourself as accessible as possible to your customers.  

-   **Barcode:** You need a barcode on there, a UPC. If you are European, you should be using an EAN.  

-   **Country Of Origin:** This is very important and maybe a legal requirement depending on your product. Make sure you have this on there if necessary.  

-   **Social Media Accounts:** If you've got the room for it, it's a great way to get some more exposure and build some real credibility behind your brand.  

-   **Include Instruction to Use/Care:** There are certain labelling requirements for products that go in or on your body, including things like ingredients, facts, recommended doses, and other requirements. FDA regulations can be found at the link below, and it's always a good idea to look at what your competitors have on their products.

-   **Net Weight:** May be required, and if so, the appropriate measurement (pounds, ounces, grams, etc.) 

-   **Storage Instructions:** such as temperature, shelf life, etc.  

### **Packaging Requirements for Amazon FBA**

Always keep in mind that when you will be using Amazon FBA. They will require your packaging requirements.

-   Each one-of-a-kind product requires a one-of-a-kind FNSKU. Each product type (size or colour) must have its own FNSKU.
-   Each unit must have an easily accessible exterior scannable barcode or label.
-   Existing barcodes on shipping boxes must be rendered unscannable. This can be accomplished by using opaque tape or a black felt-tip marker to cover existing barcodes. This prevents the incorrect barcode from being scanned during the receiving process by accident.
-   Units that are not contained in secure packaging must be bagged or secured with a nonadhesive band or removable tape.
-   Units sold as a set must be packed in a single secure package.

### **Amazon Approved Packaging materials for FBA**

-   Poly Bags
-   Boxes
-   Stretch wrap
-   Bubble Packs

**TAKE NOTE:** Please keep in mind that the packaging label requirements can change depending on Amazon's requirements, so make sure to check directly with Amazon to see what the current packaging requirements are.
