## **The following logic is used when evaluating any product:** 

-   **BSR -** The lower the BSR, for all three products, the better those products are selling. The 'Linda’s Silicone Stove Gap Cover' is doing very well, with a BSR of 1. This may indicate that the main product is a brand-driven product. They may be driving product traffic to Amazon directly. However, if you have multiple products that are selling well, and it's a product with a bit of a general keyword, then it's a good, solid opportunity. 
-   **Reviews -** It's hard to compete with products that have thousands of reviews. Having thousands of reviews on the main product in comparison to the second and third products will give you a good idea of what you can compete with. You don't have to be a top earner to make multiple thousands of dollars a month off of these products.
-   **Price -** We only highlighted products priced around $10. You want to be between $10 and $50. 

With this logic in mind, and using the examples in this course as a guide, go over your list and choose the top three things you wish to pursue; the next step will be to locate reputable suppliers for those products.
