## **How to Use the Available at Amazon Badge**

Amazon has some strict rules about how you can use the **Available at Amazon badge**. Here are the things you need to keep in mind when using your badge:

-   -   -   Use the badge, ONLY to advertise your products that can be found and available to purchase on the Amazon website.
        -   The original form of the badge provided should not be altered. You might consider changing the colors, fonts, and layout that will be consistent with your brand. That is an emphatic NO.
        -   Don't use the badge in any way that implies Amazon sponsorship or endorsement. 
        -   Other Amazon logos or trademarks should not be used. This badge is the only official Amazon graphic you have permission to use to advertise your presence on the platform.

Complete Guides about the badge can be found here: [https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200573210](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200573210)
