The purpose of this lesson is to guide you on how to make Amazon videos that will help you attract more traffic to your listings.

### **Amazon Product Videos**

This wonderful service, which Amazon has launched for sellers, gives all sellers a high-converting tool to market their items. This feature, however, is not available to all sellers; only those who are brand registered compliant can use it.

### **Why Do I need Video for my Amazon Listing?**

Buyers can learn a lot about a product by looking at high-quality images. Video content, on the other hand, will considerably improve the consumer purchase experience because it provides the buyer or your listing visitor with the most enticing means of learning about your goods. The use of video can help you capture clients' attention, which has been more difficult in recent years as viewers' attention spans have reduced. It contributes to the growth and improvement of your brand's image.

### **Getting Your Video Created** 

If you have never created a video of your own before, don’t worry it is much easier than you think. If you do a web search, you'll discover that there are a plethora of options for getting videos made quickly and cheaply. We'll go over the best and easiest ways to get started in this tutorial.

Here are some video formats you might want to look at: 

Here are the types of videos that are commonly used for Amazon products.

-   **Product Highlight Video**

In this video format, you can usually find the product's key features, applications, and benefits. The product highlights video is typically used to show prospective customers exactly what your product has to offer.

Tips for creating this type of video:

1.  1.  Concentrate on the key features of your product.
    2.  Keep it brief, straightforward, and to the point.

Sample Amazon Listing with a product video highlight: [Wacom Intuos Graphics Drawing Tablet for Mac, PC, Chromebook & Android (small) with Software Included - Black (CTL4100)](https://www.amazon.com/Wacom-Drawing-Software-Included-CTL4100/dp/B079HL9YSF/ref=sr_1_49?qid=1642498995&s=computers-intl-ship&sr=1-49)

-   **Lifestyle Video**

This type of product video drew attention to its audience by demonstrating how your product can be used in real life. It's as if you're giving them a visual representation of how they'll feel when they use your product. Another inventive way to demonstrate how your product can be used.

Tips for creating this type of video:

1.  1.  Understand your target audience, what they are looking for, and how can be your product be useful for them.
    2.  Make an effort to connect them to your product.

-   **Unboxing Video** 

This is a video in which someone unboxes a product from start to finish, demonstrating the condition of the package, describing all items included with the purchased product, and explaining in minute detail how the product works or does not work. This type of video can provide customers with the satisfaction of knowing what their order will look like when it arrives, assuring them that they are making a wise purchase decision. and allows them to virtually unpack the product that will mirror their excited emotions.

For the majority of these video styles, you will only need to offer a brief screenplay to the person who will be generating your film, but they will guide you through the entire process.

Now you must remember to include a call to action in your films, if necessary. Simply ask your video's creator to include it for you, or include it in your script if you're supplying one.

These videos, on the other hand, are wonderful for raising brand awareness and, more importantly, for driving traffic and links to your products and websites.

### **Animoto.com**

[Animoto.com](https://animoto.com/) is a web service that lets you create videos with images and music quickly and easily. There is a free version, but we recommend paying $8 a month at the very least to receive more themes and the ability to download them yourself. We won't go into depth because the directions are great and simple to follow. All you really need are photographs, either of your product or images connected to your specialty or better both. You'll upload these to the Animoto site, select a video theme and music track, and the site will take care of the rest. That's all there is to it.

These aren't going to be high-quality sales videos, but they're still highly powerful, and many amazon sellers have built their entire business around Animoto.

Helpful resources: [https://animoto.com/blog/video-marketing/amazon-seller-videos](https://animoto.com/blog/video-marketing/amazon-seller-videos)
