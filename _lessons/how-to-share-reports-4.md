### **How to share reports**

**Sharing and customizing reports**

Once you've discovered meaningful data in your reports, Google Analytics provides several options for sharing or referring back to that report through the report name at the top.

![Sharing and customizing reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Sharing-and-customizing-reports-300x121.png)

-   **“Save”** lets you create a link to the specific report in the Customization area in the left-hand navigation under **“Saved Reports.”**
-   **“Export”** enables you to save the report to different formats on your desktops such as PDF and CSV.
-   **"Share"** allows you to email a copy of the report as an attachment and schedule regular email updates.
-   **“Edit”** allows you to customize the report content by adding metric groups, filters, or additional views. This will create a new report in the Customization area under "Custom Reports." We'll cover Custom Reports in the advanced course.

![Sharing and customizing reports How to share reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Sharing-and-customizing-reports-How-to-share-reports-300x155.png)

Hover over the green check icon next to the report name to see the percentage of sessions on which the report is based. Sometimes, Analytics must sift through so much data that, to deliver your report quicker, it will only examine a subset of the data gathered. This is known as "sampling" because it provides an estimate of the precise count based on a sample of your data.

![How to share reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/How-to-share-reposts-300x114.png)

To modify the report sampling rate, hover your cursor over the green data quality icon and choose a value from the pulldown menu. Leave this set to "Greater Precision" if you want your data to be more precise and don't mind the extra response time. Select "Faster response" if you want to reduce the time it takes to produce a report and are prepared to forgo more accurate data.
