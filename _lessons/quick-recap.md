Congratulations for making it in here! 

Before we proceed to the next level here is a quick recap of the things you have learned in this module. In this module, we learned:

1.  All the necessary details you need when creating an Amazon Seller Account,
2.  The step-by-step process of setting up your seller account,
3.  The difference between the Professional and Individual Seller Account,
4.  What to do when your account got deactivated, and the reason why it got deactivated,
5.  Setting up Amazon Seller Account if you are outside the US, and
6.  How to set up a World first or Payoneer, and what is the importance and difference between these two.
7.  Determining and finding the right product category for you.

If at all possible, try to make it overlap with yourself, which means it should be something you are interested in and use. The importance of taking this course goes far beyond determining why you're here, where you're going, and even addressing problems that address 'the big question.'
