# **What is Private Labeling?**

Private labeling, as mentioned in the first module, is a business strategy for developing and marketing your own product brand. The process of applying your own brand to an item that has already been manufactured or produced by another company is known as private labeling.

### **Advantages of Private Labeling are:**

-   Control overpricing
-   Lowering of competition
-   Create customer loyalty
-   Build your own brand along the process
-   Make Yourself Stand Out from the competition

It is relatively simple to locate private label products. Everything you see in your home or office, as well as everything you buy in stores, has the potential to be a private label product.

If you've never done private labeling before, you might be nervous. Don't be concerned; it's simpler than you think. When you do it once, you'll realize how simple it is, and you'll be able to private label and source any product.

**The Three Most Common Private Labeling Questions**

There are more questions like this, but the three most common ones in Private Labeling are:

1.  Is it necessary for me to keep inventory?

You'll never have to touch any inventory if you follow what I teach you in the following modules. You'll use Amazon's fulfillment system, so you won't have to do anything.

2.  Is a large initial investment required?

No, you can start with as little as $100 to $200. You won't need to make a large upfront investment once you know how to find good suppliers and negotiate with them.

3.  Why aren't suppliers simply selling on Amazon?

Why don't sellers take advantage of these opportunities themselves? One major reason is that it is not the business model for which they are designed. They are designed to sell to people like you and me who want to go out and sell retail. Their line of work is wholesale.
