This lesson will give you an idea of what you will need to set up your Seller Central Account.

![Setting Up Your Seller Central Account ](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Image-4-300x185.png)

In order to set up an account with Seller Central, you will need the following items:

-   **EMAIL** – This is the email address Amazon will use to send you account-related communications. Customers may send you to order alerts and emails. The customer service email may be the same or different, but you need an email first.

-   **BANK ACCOUNT** –A bank account is required into which Amazon can deposit sales proceeds. If you are outside the US and what to get selling on Amazon, go to the lesson **Getting Started Selling from Outside the USA (under Module 1**), which walks you through all the cool things you can do to get started selling from anywhere in the world.  

-   **ADDRESS** \- You will need an address if you are receiving shipments. You may also get customer returns from time to time, which will need a return address. Finally, a billing address is required.
-   **GOVERNMENT ID -** Identity verification protects sellers and customers.

-   **PHONE NUMBER** – A phone number is required. It helps to contact Amazon every now and then. You may contact them quite easily. Because you can learn a lot quicker by talking to them than by sending them an email, having their phone number is extremely useful and highly recommended.

-   **CREDIT CARD** – There is a $39.99 monthly fee for the Professional account when signing up for Amazon Seller (which we recommend you sign up for). It's actually deferred, so you receive the first month for free. The idea is that regular billings will go on the credit card. You'll probably never be paid for the trial time since it's free and you'll probably make enough sales to cover any recurring charges (Amazon will take from sales).
-   **BUSINESS (optional)** – You'll need a business to sell your Amazon items. The easiest method to sell Amazon items is to hire a business that sells them for you. It isn't required, but it helps. I suggest an LLC. Any state in the US has one. A simple LLC is all you need to start it later. Again this is optional; in fact, you do not need to form an LLC to sell on Amazon; as a sole proprietor, you can start selling right away.

The next lesson, **"The Seller Central Setup Guide,"** will walk you through the steps required to set up Seller Central.
