Frequently, people will simply create a blog post with an image and description of the product and call it a day. The problem is that it does not distinguish itself from the existing Amazon listing, which means it will miss out on targeted traffic from different keyword rankings in search engines. This isn't a good thing because search engines, particularly Google, don't like duplicate content, and if you just post what's already on Amazon, you'll miss out on additional traffic from having your posts ranked.

Another important point to remember is that you want to convert the reader from a "slightly interested" to a "ready to buy" prospect.

For example, I have this  PRODUCT X that I want to promote using a blog post, I wouldn't just list a picture of it with a description of what it is; instead, I'd introduce the product in a way that relates to the potential customer (by understanding your product's primary demographic) and highlight some awesome additional benefits that will encourage the reader to take action.

📝Here are the lists you need to consider:

1.  You must first determine who your target audience is (hopefully you already understand this). 
2.  The second thing you need to do is you need figured out the demographics of your audience.
3.  Next, you'll need to figure out what the primary likes and dislikes of these PRODUCTS X are (hint: look at the competition).
4.  Lastly, write a 250 to 350-word introduction that relates to the audience and draws them in, and keeps them reading.

We and most people in the marketing industry will recommend the A.I.D.A. principle, which stands for Attention, Interest, Desire, and Action. We already discussed this principle briefly in the previous lesson (Copywriting Strategies to Generate Product Sales). Now, in this lesson, we will have a quick recap and discuss how to apply this principle when writing a Blog Post.

### **Attention:**  

Get the reader’s attention with a compelling attention-grabbing title. The title should be no more than 10 to 15 words long and focused on capturing the reader's attention, but make sure to include the keyword phrase you're targeting. Keep the title short to avoid becoming too wordy, allowing you to focus on the benefits. 

However, keep in mind that search engines will only display about 10 words from the title in their search engine listings (they have a character limit), so make the most of it. I also recommend fitting your keyword into the first half of the title, so if the title is ten words long, your keyword should appear in the first five words.

### **Interest:** 

You want to get your reader interested in what you have to say now that you have their attention, and the most effective way to do that is to relate to what they are going through. Give information that will arouse that feeling of interest to read more and know more about the product. This is why it's critical to understand demographics. If the primary audience is females 35 and up, you'll want to relate to them rather than sounding like you're speaking to a 25-year-old man. This section only needs to be one or two sentences long at the start of the content, and I recommend that you use the keyword phrase only once.

### **Desire:** 

Now that you've piqued the reader's interest, it's time to arouse a desire in them to help you convert them into a customer. I recommend mentioning Amazon in this section to emphasize the convenience and comfort that shopping with Amazon provides so that the potential customer feels secure in making a secure purchase. If at all possible, include your keyword in this section at least once (but more important is to ensure the content reads well).

### **Action:** 

Now it's time to tell the reader what they can do to fulfil the desire you've sparked. This is an important point that is frequently overlooked because no one wants to appear pushy. People, on the other hand, need to be told what to do. You've put in a lot of effort to get them to read your content; now it's time to get them to visit your product page. You don't have to get all worked up about it, but you do need to be clear on what they need to do to fulfil the desire you've sparked in them. Keep it brief (one or two sentences) and only use the keyword phrase once.
