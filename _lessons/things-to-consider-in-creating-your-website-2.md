# **I. Creating Personal Website**

Make an impression on your hiring managers and increase your chances of getting a job at your dream company. A personal website's content can vary greatly depending on the industry and position you sought. But here are elements that all Personal websites should consider to include:

## Your Purpose

When visitors arrive at your website, you want them to immediately understand who you are, what you do, and what you are looking for. This could include major corporations for which you've worked, projects you're most proud of, your professional skills, and even a little personality. Make sure to make a big impression.

**Include the Following:**

1.  **Your professional headline** \- This can be either your job title or a more general description of yourself. It could be a section of your website where a visitor remembers you. When writing your professional headline, keep it relevant to your purpose, short and easy to remember, and don't forget to include your SEO keywords, which will help to increase your visibility online. Ideally, it can be found on your homepage or the about page section.
2.  **Your Brief Bio** \- Here you can include any information that isn't on your resume or cover letters here, such as interests, hobbies, or awards and accomplishments but keep it professional and brief. It can usually be found on your About me Page.
3.  **Professional Objective** - This section will give your hiring manager a glimpse of what you can do and how you can be helpful to their company.
4.  **Short Video of Yourself** - Remember that your visitors don’t just want to see your website's pages of text. Make it more engaging and interesting by including video content like a brief introduction video about yourself. People will stay on your website longer if you use videos to engage them with your content. Videos will give you the ability to showcase more than just your skills or services. It also gives a glimpse into your personality, which is important to remember because people want to work with people.

## Where can you be found online?

In today's modern market, a website is not the only place where you can market yourself. This might seem obvious, but remember to provide other contact details where recruitment managers can reach you easily. Include your phone numbers, professional email address, and links to your professional social media accounts, such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and so on.

## Great Sample of your Works

Consider your websites to be online portfolios showcasing your best work. Include your best design samples if you're a graphic designer, your best articles if you're a writer, and your best marketing campaigns if you're a marketing manager. Include all of your best projects; this will assist you in making a good impression on your next employer. Make sure to let your work samples sell your skills.

## Best Professional Blog (Optional)

This is an excellent opportunity to showcase your writing skills, especially if you are a writer. The best place in your industry to express your ideas and strong opinions. Maintain a blog that is engaging, informative, and relevant to your audience. It also helps to drive traffic to your website and significantly improves your search engine optimization. The greater the number of blogs you have, the more content search engines will have to crawl.

## Testimonials from your Previous Employer

Impress your next employer with statements demonstrating your credibility and level of expertise from previous projects or jobs completed. Increase your reputation by expressing the confidence that others have in you and your service offerings. Testimonials can have a significant impact on the success of your career.

# **II. Creating Your Business Website**

Allowing your target market to find you online can help your company's sales. Because research is less expensive than going to the store in person, it is now more convenient for customers to research any product or service before consuming or availing it.

**The following are what every business owner should consider adding to their website:**

## Business Brand Identity

Keep in mind that your website is your primary online salesperson. Use your website as a visual representation of how you want to communicate to your customer. The visual representation of your business identity is how your customers will remember your services or products. Allow your brand to express your core values and connect with your customers. Keep your brand authentic.

Here is what you should list in your brand identity for your website:

-   **Company Name**
-   **Logo**
-   **Brand Color Palette**
-   **Typography**
-   **A unique way of customer interaction**

## Business Purpose

Make it clear to visitors to your website what services your company provides. Inform them of how you can help them. Describes why your company was founded, what your goals are, your missions and visions, and how your company differs from others.

## Behind the Scene of your Business

People enjoy learning about the history of your company. Feel free to share your company and the people behind its journey. It will not only give your site visitors a glimpse of your company's journey, but it will also give them more human interaction, which will foster more trust and connection.

## Call - to- Action

This is essential to include and should be prominently displayed to encourage your site visitors to take the desired action, whether it's to read more, provide feedback, sign up, or purchase. CTAs are frequently used in the development of your sales road map, such as creating an email list for your sales update, and so on.

## Testimonials

As previously stated, testimonials will demonstrate your credibility and expertise, causing your prospects or leads to trust you more, resulting in the success of your business. You can include testimonials from previous customers, partners, and anyone else who can demonstrate your credibility with the services or products you provide.

## Contact information

Make it easy for your site visitors to contact you; you don't want to lose potential leads because they are having difficulty finding ways to contact you. Include information about how your leads can contact you and where they can find you online. 

You can include all of your social media pages related to your business. This will not only allow your leads to find multiple ways to contact you, but it will also provide them with more background information about your company through your social media pages that are available online.
