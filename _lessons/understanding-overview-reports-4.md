### **Welcome to a Guided Tour of the Audience Overview Report**

#### **Date range**

Every report has a date range at the top. This allows you to specify the period within which you want to analyze report data. Click the date range to open up the date range selector.

![Guided Tour of the Audience Overview Report Date Range](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Guided-Tour-of-the-Audience-Overview-Report-Date-Range-1-300x119.png)

#### **Date range selector**

This opens up a calendar on the left where you can select your date ranges. When you change the date range, it affects all reports that are in your view. You may switch between different reports without having to change the date range each time.

You can choose date ranges like last week, last month, or the last 30 days. However, you can also specify specific dates by clicking the start- and end-date fields and selecting calendar dates. If you’d like to select an entire month, simply click on the name of the month in the calendar to the left.

![Date range selector](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Date-range-selector-300x81.png)

#### **Date Range Comparison**

You can also compare data from two different date ranges by clicking "Compare to" and adding in the date ranges you wish to compare. This lets you see how your business changed over time.

![Date Range Comparison](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Date-Range-Comparison-300x189.png)

#### **Segment Picker**

On the top of the report is a segment picker. Segments are ways to look at specific data sets and compare metrics. We’ll learn all about it in the advanced course. For now, notice that the default segment includes all of the Users that visited your site in the given date range.

![Segment Picker](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Segment-Picker-300x23.png)

#### **Line Graph**

Below the segment, the picker is the different metrics of the Audience Overview report shown in different formats. The most prominent is a line graph that by default shows a data point for the number of users on each day over your selected date range.

![Line Graph](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Line-Graph-300x79.png)

#### **Duration Selector**

If you wish to view this data more specifically, you can change the data points to show hourly, weekly, or monthly, as well. This can be especially helpful when looking at large data sets. If you are looking at data over a single day, the view will default to hourly.

![Duration Selector](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Duration-Selector-300x73.png)

#### **Metric Selector**

You can change the metric shown from users to a different metric by selecting the drop-down menu under the Overview tab. Analytics lets you compare this to a second metric over the same time period by clicking **“Select a metric”.**

![Metric Selector](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Metric-Selector-224x300.png)

#### **Graph Annotator**

At the bottom of the line graph, you can see a small arrow. Clicking on the arrow lets you annotate the graph with helpful notes to add business context to your data. The moment you add an annotation, a small indicator appears on the graph that is visible by anyone with access to the view. By clicking any of the metrics below, you will see the data points for those metrics in the line graph above.

![Graph Annotator](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Graph-Annotator-300x27.png)

#### **Metrics**

There are a number of helpful metrics beneath the line graph:

-   **“Sessions”** refers to the total number of sessions for the given date range. 
-   **“Users”** refers to the total number of users that visited the site during the given period,
-   **“Pageviews”** are the total number of times pages that included your Analytics tracking code was displayed to users. This includes repeated viewings of a single page by the same user.
-   **“Pages per session”** is the average number of pages viewed during each session. This also includes repeated viewings of a single page.
-   **“Average session duration”** is the average length of a session based on users that visited your site in the selected date range.
-   **“Bounce rate”** is the percentage of users who left after viewing a single page on your site and taking no additional action.
-   **“Percent of new sessions”** is the percentage of sessions in your date range who are new users to your site.

![Metrics](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Metrics-300x163.png)

#### **New vs. Returning Users**

To the right of the metrics is a pie chart illustrating the percentage of new vs. returning users.

![New vs. Returning Users](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/New-vs.-Returning-Users.png)

#### **Language Dimension**

The default dimension selected in the Audience Overview report is **“Language.”** Note that the table to the right includes the top 10 values for Language. You can also select **"Country"** or **"City"** to view the top 10 values for those dimensions.

![Language Dimension](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Language-Dimension-300x115.png)
