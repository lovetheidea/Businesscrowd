-   **How can I edit my existing listing?** 

To edit details for an existing listing:

1.  1.  On the Inventory menu, select Manage Inventory.
    2.  Select the Active status filter above the product list.
    3.  Find the listing you want to edit, and select Edit from the drop-down menu for that listing.
    4.  Click the links at the top of the page to add, edit, or change the listing's content. You can also type price and quantity updates in the text boxes in the Price and Quantity columns.
    5.  Click Save and finish.

### **Choosing the Best Keywords to Drive Amazon Sales**

-   **If your primary keyword has less than 5000 searches on Google, is that an issue? Are we looking for any particular number of searches for our product on Google?** 

Assess your product opportunity only based on what you see on Amazon and what you've learned in this program, not on Google volume.

-   **When you checked Google, why didn't you sort by keyword ranking before scanning the list? Given that the product page has only so much space, is there really merit in using low search count keywords?** 

Yes, sorting by keyword ranking can lead to the generation of irrelevant keywords.

-   **When doing the search in keywords, what can I do to make the search as if in the U.S.?** 

Under "Discover new keywords" >> "Start with Keywords” in the Planner tool, click on the country field and change it to the United States. You should be able to ignore the remaining options. Refer to the image attached.

-   **How can you tell what the most searched keyword is on Amazon?** 

There's no way. Amazon doesn't tell you that. 

-    **Should we use “phrase” match or “exact” match?** 

For the purposes of this investigation, "exact match" will provide the most accurate results.

-   **Does capitalization matter in Google Planner?** 

For searching, it doesn't matter. 

-   **Is it okay to use brand names as keywords or should we avoid them?** 

People are unlikely to search for your brand name at first, but according to Amazon formulas for writing Product titles, Brand is always included at the start.

### **Top-Notch Product Titles** 

-   **I am going to be selling a product that has different variations (sizes and quantities). Is it enough that I stuff all the keywords into the variation PARENT title so that I can take advantage of putting size/quantity and a few more keywords in the titles of the variation listings, or should I use the same parent title for all variations?** 

The variation parent title is irrelevant if you have variations. The titles of the variations will be specific to those listings, not to the parent.

-   **Is it 200 characters with or without spaces?** 

Spaces are included in the total. Please note that the maximum number of characters in a product title varies by category.

-   **I was allowed to change my title without thinking about the URL. Will this affect my previous promotion effort? It would be sad to have wasted some valuable promotion by having it link to a dead URL.** 

Only a small portion of the previous promotion's power will be lost because the old URL will redirect to the new one. Don't get too worked up about it, but make sure you're satisfied with your new position before looking for other opportunities.

### **Product Descriptions that Generate Sales**

-   **Can I still use HTML tags in writing descriptions?** 

HTML tags in the writing of product descriptions are now banned in Amazon. 

-   **How do we display a list of Technical Information? Is it simply added on at the end of the product description or can there be a LINK to “Technical Information”?** 

Depending on the product type/category, add to the product description OR check the other details tab to see if it fits somewhere else.

-   **Is it better to be neutral or friendly in the bullet points?**

Make sure you aren't a robot - HUMANIZED your content.

-   **Are product dimensions with packaging or without?** 

It should be with. 

-    **Are anyone can join A+ Content?** 

Unfortunately not, here are the eligibility requirements by Amazon in order to join the A+ Content:

A+ Content feature is only available to professional sellers who have been approved as brand owners through the [Amazon Brand Registry process](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200955930?language=en_US&ref=efph_200955930_cont_202102950), as well as emerging brand owners who are part of certain managed selling programs, such as Launchpad and Amazon Exclusives. After you’ve been approved, you’ll be able to add A+ only to products that are part of your approved brand catalog.

### **Images that pique shoppers' interest and convert them into buyers**

-   **Who will take the pictures? And who will make the background white? And how much will this cost?** 

Upwork.com is one option - they can do graphical mockups of any product. You can also find designers on Freelance.com, Fiverr, and 99Designs. 

-   **Can you put animated GIFs up on Amazon? That would be a cool way to get attention!** 

I don't think you can add animated gifs and have certainly never seen one before. 

-   **Is it OK to have your bonus item (eBook) etc. as one of your Images?** 

Yes. 

-   **Will Amazon allow you to change your main product image? Will they allow you to add more images later?** 

Yes, you can change or add images at any time. 

-   **How many images can we use? My product doesn’t let me use 9?** 

While 9 is the most common number, the number of available images will vary by product category. The important thing is to try and use them all with great, high-quality eye-catching images.
