## **Characteristics of a Successful Amazon Opportunity**

-   **What does Bestseller Ranking (BSR) of Top-Ranked Products mean?** 

The BSR or Best Seller Ranking is the ranking in a specific category that a product is given based on how well it is selling compared to other products in that category. When you go to the product page, go to the "Product Details" section and you will see the bestseller ranking for each product.

-   **What's an ASIN??** 

ASINs (Amazon Standard Identification Digits) are unique blocks of 10 letters and/or numbers that are used to identify items on Amazon. The ASIN may be found on Amazon.com's product information page for the item. The ASIN number for books is the same as the ISBN number, but for all other products, a new ASIN is generated when the item is added to our database. The ASIN of an item can be found on the product detail page, along with other information about the item, such as size, a number of pages (if it's a book), or a number of discs (if it's a CD).

ASINs can be used to find items in our catalog. If you know the ASIN or ISBN of the item you're looking for, simply enter it into the search box (located towards the top of most pages), click the "Go" button, and the item will appear in your search results if it's in our catalog.

For example, the ASIN for Hasbro's "Monopoly" game is B00005N5PF, therefore the product URL is http://www.amazon.com/Hasbro—5011634440211—Monopoly/dp/B00005N5PF/, which includes the ASIN.
