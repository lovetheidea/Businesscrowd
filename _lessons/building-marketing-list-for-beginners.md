Email marketing is one of the most business-critical marketing activities you should be doing. It is the best outbound marketing technique for ROI, gaining $36 to $44 per $1 spent. It is permissible and has long-lasting, high rewards associated with the practice.

However, to be sure that you have the results you need, you need to build a marketing list. Building a marketing list is challenging, especially without a website. In this lesson, we’ll discuss some of the ways that you can build a marketing list using all the possible tools at your disposal.

## Invite Those Close

When you’re talking to customers, prospects or even those you’re meeting at a networking session, use this time to collect business cards and get them to subscribe to your marketing list. You will have to manually add these to your list, but it is a quick way to gain subscribers.

To help you keep track, why not use a system to remember what people have said or not? Collect the business card and mark it with a Y or N to state whether they’ve subscribed to the list or not.

## Website Forms

It is critical to use your website to collect emails. You can use an opt-in form in the sidebar. Or you can use an exit-intent form to collect addresses from people who are leaving the website.

You need to give plenty of reasons for the website visitor to subscribe. There are lots of options here, but telling the visitor how frequently they can expect to receive emails, and what will be included in the mailings is a good start.

## Competitions/Contests

Another way to get more people signed up to your mailing list is to create a competition/contest. 

This might be more suitable for those who are going to want to know about affiliate products you’re advertising or something else that is more for consumers. It is critical to make the prize something worth winning.

## Offer Discounts/Offers

Nearly half of Americans (and presumably the rest of the world) join a mailing list to take advantage of deals and discounts. Therefore, offering discounts/offers on products, services or something else can be a good way to encourage subscriptions.

You can do this in several ways. You can create a Facebook/LinkedIn advert where you promise new subscribers a chance to save on their order. Or you can use a popup on your website.

## Offer Learning Materials

Another way of capturing leads is to offer potential subscribers learning materials for subscribing. This opportunity is perfect for those who are looking for other ways to earn revenue, such as through courses or training sessions. Offering a free email-based course can help you build trust with the audience and help with drip marketing opportunities.

## Final Word: How Do You Build a Marketing List for Email?

While many people concentrate on building a marketing list of thousands, you don’t need that many. Just a couple of hundred and you can have a profitable marketing list. Above are some of the ways that you can build a successful marketing list for your developing skills to help you grow and continuously find work/revenue.
