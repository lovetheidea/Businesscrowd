The purpose of this lesson is to address two common questions:

-   "What is the “buy box”?"
-   "What if Amazon is already selling the product I'm looking at?"

We've included this in Module 2 since these are two areas where people who haven't sold on Amazon previously have a lot of assumptions. Following the methodology that we are teaching, these two things will not be important to you, but as an Amazon marketer, we want to make sure that you are aware of them.

![Buy Box and Sold by Amazon](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Buy-Box-and-Sold-by-Amazon-300x185.png)

## **What is the Buy Box?**

To begin, what exactly is a "buy box"? The ability for multiple sellers to sell the same product is a key feature of the Amazon website. The "buy box" is a widget found on the majority of Amazon product listing pages that allows customers to complete their purchase in two or three steps and allows shoppers to choose how they will pay for the product. Many sellers can benefit from Buy Box placement in terms of increased sales.

![What is the Buy Box?](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/What-is-the-Buy-Box-300x141.jpg)

You must compete for the **Buy Box** on a daily basis if you are not the sole seller of a brand on the marketplace. This requires a significant amount of effort. The best price is important, but so are your seller rating, error rate, inventory level, and overall performance.

The good news is that you won't have to worry about any of this because there will be no other sellers for your goods in the business model.

## **What if Amazon is the seller?**

If **"Ships from and sold by Amazon.com"** appears on the listing page, you'll know the product is from Amazon (see below).

![Ships from and sold by Amazon.com](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/What-is-the-Buy-Box-1-300x141.jpg)

Is it true that if a product is sold by Amazon, it is not a product worth competing with, or that it is a product that Amazon owns and will not allow anybody else to sell? It does not. All it implies is that Amazon has contacted the maker of that product (in our case, Headphones) and requested if Amazon may buy it wholesale and sell it on their site. And if you sold the same brand product on Amazon you would be added to that list of other sellers but because you are making your own product, you will have your own listing and will not have to worry about this.

![Product on Amazon](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/258697244_3069497523325307_834474926030687760_n-300x144.png)

So, what's the point of knowing everything? —Well, the point is that we want you to understand what will happen if your desired product that you want to sell on Amazon is also available to other sellers, and we want to avoid the complications that come with the likes in the long run. As a result, you'll be able to choose and sell your own branded products with confidence.
