There is a great way to build an LinkedIn network that doesn’t require a lot of money or time to manage.

### Step 1 - Create a good profile

As discussed in other posts, a good profile is a way to advertise yourself. It is also what potential contacts will see when you try to connect with them. The most vital features are the photos, description, and tagline. Don't worry we will dive deeper on this one at our Lesson 4: [How to Build a Great LinkedIn Profile.](https://academy.lovetheidea.co.uk/courses/want-to-build-a-massive-linkedin-network-quickly/lesson/how-to-build-a-great-linkedin-profile/)

### Step 2 - Create content to promote your interests

Spend about a week creating content and engaging with current network contacts. This content provides a positive image and demonstrates that you’re not just on the platform to sell.

### Step 3 - Find and research ten potential people for your network

The next step is about finding ten people to connect with. Using job titles and interests as parameters to identify them, you want to list ten potentially valuable people for your network. You want a mixture of prospective clients, contacts in your industry, or even those with knowledge that you might be able to use (marketing, accounting, sales, etc.).

The people you choose should be active on the network. They should have lots of content and engage with others.

Send each one an invite. Try to personalise each message by providing reasons why they should connect and how you’ll find them valuable in your network.

* * *

Here are some sample message guide you can follow:

1\. Reaching Out to a Colleague or Former Colleague:

Hi \[Name\],

I hope you're doing well! I enjoyed working with you at \[Company\] and have always admired your expertise in \[specific field or skill\]. I'd love to stay connected here on LinkedIn to keep up with your professional journey and exchange insights about \[industry/topic\].

Best regards,  
\[Your Name\]

2\. Connecting with Industry Peers or Experts:

Hi \[Name\],

I've been following your work in \[specific field/industry\] and was impressed by your recent article on \[specific topic\]. I think we share a lot of interests, and I'd love to connect and exchange ideas.

Cheers,  
\[Your Name\]

3\. Connecting with Potential Clients or Business Partners:

Hi \[Name\],

I came across your profile and was impressed by your work at \[Company\]. Given our shared interest in \[specific industry/field\], I think there could be some great opportunities for us to collaborate. Let’s connect!

Best,  
\[Your Name\]

* * *

**Tips for Personalizing LinkedIn Connection Requests:**

1.  **Use Their Name**: Always address the person by their name to make the message feel personal.
2.  **Mention Common Interests or Connections**: Highlighting mutual interests or connections can create an instant bond.
3.  **State Your Reason Clearly**: Explain why you want to connect and how it can be mutually beneficial.
4.  **Be Concise**: Keep your message short and to the point. People are more likely to read and respond to a brief message.
5.  **Be Genuine**: Authenticity goes a long way. Ensure your message reflects your genuine interest and respect for the person.

By tailoring your messages with these elements, you'll increase the likelihood of forming meaningful and valuable connections on LinkedIn.

### Step 4 - Wait

Now wait about six hours. You’ll find that about two to three will respond during this time. About 50% will probably be accepted within the next week or so.

### Step 5 - Repeat

Now repeat steps three and four every six hours. Do this five days a week. You will find that you will get about 15-20 new contacts per day. So within two months, you should have more than 1,000 contacts.

Continue posting interesting and new content every day, keeping your profile active and looking interesting for prospective contacts.

And your success rate of getting new contacts should improve. And as you grow in popularity, new contacts should also contact you. As a result, you will see a significant rise in the number of contacts in your network.

## How to Build a Large Network on LinkedIn

Want to build a large network on LinkedIn? Above is a process you can trust to help you create a valuable network. It is quick and easy to do. And the long-term impact is a highly effective network that can help you market your products/services.
