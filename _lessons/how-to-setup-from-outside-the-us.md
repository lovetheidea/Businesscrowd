### **Setting up a US-based bank account**

The first way to get a US-based bank account setup is to simply ask one of your local banks if they can set up a US-based bank account for you. This must be a “US-based bank account” and not simply a “US funds” account. There are a number of international banks that can do this, such as HSBC, but we first suggest you try your own local bank to see if they can do this. If so, then you do not need to use the services mentioned next.

### **Third-Party Providers for US Banking**

If you cannot set up a US-based bank account, there are other options that you can choose from that will essentially equip you with a US-based bank account number:

-   -   -   Payoneer: They provide you with a debit card that also comes with a US-based bank account number.
        -   World First: They are a currency company that also provides you with a US-based bank account number.
        -   Transferwise

**Important Note:** Payoneer does not work for sellers that will be selling supplements, so if you will be choosing those types of products, be sure to select World First.
