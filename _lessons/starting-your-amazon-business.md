It is possible to have multiple brands and each brand with its own list of products, so choosing products based on branding isn't a bad idea. When doing your initial research. However, it is best to not limit yourself to specific types of products. Once you have a list of potential products, you can then see how they fit from a branding perspective.

## **Selling on Amazon with Seller Central**

-    **Is my Seller Central account tied to my Kindle Direct Publishing account?**

 No. Even if they are under the same LLC or Corporation, these accounts have nothing to do with each other. The site/service sellercentral.amazon.com is entirely different than [Kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/).

-   **If I have a KDP account and a Seller Central account using the same information and the KDP account got cancelled by Amazon, would my Seller Central account also be affected?**

No. Even if they are under the same LLC or Corporation, these accounts have nothing to do with each other. With that said, however, we are not Amazon and they may at any time changes these rules. It is also a possibility that you may have caused a red flag and As such your seller account will be monitored closely.

-   **I am waiting for my Payoneer setup confirmation. Should I wait until I receive that to open my Seller Central account (Amazon says, "if you cannot comply with the requirements of this registration, please do NOT continue with this registration process")?**

You can proceed and simply go back and update the information once it is received from Payoneer.

-   **Some states have an Internet tax law in place that affects Amazon Associates** **(Amazon rejects associates from those states). Will this affect signing up for a Seller** **Central account?**

No. Amazon sellers and Amazon associates are separate entities. Amazon sellers have no problems in any of the states.

-    **I have an existing Amazon affiliate account. In addition to using a different email address for my Seller Central account, do I need to change up any other information** **(e.g. bank account)?**

 You can use the same information in both accounts. The only time you need a different bank account is if you get permission from Amazon to create a second Seller Central account. You will want a different bank account for the second Seller Central account if you are given permission to have one.

-   **If I open an Amazon Seller Central account now with my current bank account and business name, etc., can I change it later to a new bank account for an LLC I will form?**

 Yes. You can change any of the details of your account at a later date.

-   **Is it okay to use an Amazon Seller free account with this course?**

 It is not recommended that you do so as the free account limits the number of products you can sell as well as imposes fees on you that are covered in the 'Professional' account.

-   **My feedback score went down 20% after someone left a negative review. Is this a problem?**

 It usually doesn't take too long to improve your feedback score. Continue providing the best service possible with a quality product and the positive should easily outweigh the Negative.

-   **If I am working with a partner, can we use the same Seller Central account?**

 It's not an easy question to answer without knowing the full nature of your relationship with your partner. What you may want to consider is one of the following:

1) Joint bank account to receive payments

2) Create an LLC that you are partners with and use that with your Seller Central account

3) Set up separate Seller Central accounts and maybe target different niches

-   **Will the Business LLC name show up on the product page?**

 You can choose your display name in your Seller Central account settings.

-   **If I have an existing Seller Central account with 99% of my product line in one** **articular category, will I be negatively affected using that account to start building a new completely unrelated product line?**

 What you can do is change your display name to something generic (e.g. doesn't represent either product line). It won't make a massive difference if you have a lot of products in your existing product line because customers won't see your other product line(s) unless they click on your storefront or display name link.

-   **Can I have a paid Seller Central account and a free Seller Central account?**

 Amazon only allows you to have one seller account, regardless of whether one is free and the other is paid. If you want to have a second seller account you can request a second account from seller support. They are very strict on this and will not always approve this but if you have a different product line that is completely unrelated to your current product line and want a separate seller account for that, you can ask Amazon for a second account. You will be required to have a separate bank account for this new account.

-   **Should a private domain email address be used for Seller Central or would a public email address (e.g. Gmail) be okay?**

 You do not need to use a private domain email and using a public one such as Gmail is perfectly fine.

-   **Do non-­­US sellers (selling on Amazon.com) need to set up LLC rather than companies in their home location?**

 You do not need to set up an LLC. You can set up a company in your location or just operate as an individual. You do, however, need to have a way to receive money from Amazon.

-   **Do I need to add a customer service phone #?**

 No. You can do all your customer service via email.

-   **Can I change the Seller Central account legal name from my personal name to a business entity at a later date?**

 Yes, you can.

-   **Does Amazon deal with all returns and refunds?**

 Yes, if you are using FBA (Fulfilment By Amazon) they do. If however, you are not using FBA but a 3rd party fulfilment centre, then you will need to arrange this with them.

-   **Does Amazon send out any tax forms and, if so, do they go to the individual or the business on the account?**

 At this time, Amazon sends out 1099-K’s to seller account holders using the name and Tax ID of whomever the account was set up under.

## **Seller Central Setup Walkthrough**

-   **Is there any value in getting the Seller Central to account set up now, seeing that we aren't going to use it until we find a product and supplier?**

 It is highly recommended that you do this now so as to get it out of the way, allowing you can focus on other things. This is especially true if you're outside of the US, as there can be delays in getting set up for your account.

-   **For EU-based sellers, is there a need for a US physical address? If so, please explain why it will be required (e.g. FBA customers returns, US-based suppliers etc.)**

 No, there is no need for that unless you are using a third-party fulfillment center. If that is the case, then you would simply add the fulfilment center address in the "return address" section.

-   **If I set up my Amazon Seller Central account under my personal name, what is the process to change this account to an LLC company later? How long does this process take?**

 Log into your Amazon Seller Central account and change the applicable fields. The update is immediate upon saving.

-   **If you have a TIN (tax identification number) from another business, can it be used for this business with Amazon?**

 This is a state issue and therefore you should contact a local state official for this question

-   .**How do we go about making our DBA an LLC?**

 This is a state issue and therefore you should contact a local state official for this question.

-   **Where does the banking information get entered?**

1.  1.  Log into your Seller Central account.
    2.  Go to Settings >> Account information.
    3.  Scroll down to the Deposit method and click on the edit button on the right-hand side.
    4.  Enter your bank account info.

-   **Is it necessary to have a US bank account for your Amazon Seller Central account?**

 In order to use Amazon.com, you will be required to have a US-­­based bank account and this is covered in detail in the lessons “Getting Set Up Selling From Outside USA” and “Payoneer Quick Approval”.

-   **What type of bank account do I need: checking or savings?**

 Since Amazon deals in direct deposit, the type of account is not a concern.

-   **Do I need to put in a physical address for "returns"?**

 You do not need a physical address for FBA unless you are using a 3rd party fulfilment center. In that case, you would put the address of that fulfilment center.

-   **Does the name on my Amazon Seller Central account need to match the name on my bank account?**

 No.

-   **If I have a "personal" Amazon Seller Central account, can it be upgraded to a** **"professional" account?**

Yes. In your seller central account, for to “settings” and then under “Selling Plan” you can modify the plan.

-   **Why should I not enter my company name as my display name?**

If your company name is a generic name, then it would be okay to use it. When you would not want to use it is if the name was more specific, e.g. Great Toys, because if you decide to branch out to include other niches (recommended), then the company name could be misleading, e.g. if you moved into health products using "Great Toys" as the company name.

-   **I made a mistake configuring my business information when setting up my Seller** **Central account and there is no way to edit it. What should I do?**

It may be that Amazon is still processing the current info, so you can either wait for them to complete that process or submit a ticket to Amazon Support via the "Contact Seller Support" link at the bottom of your Seller Central account page.

-   **Seller Central setup is asking for a "Legal" name. What do I enter here?**

 You may use your personal name here. If, however, you already have an LLC or company, you may want to enter the name of that.

-   **Can I create my Seller Central account now and update the info later?** Yes.
-   **If "Payment Summary" in my Seller Central account says "Bank account information is missing or invalid for your seller account. Verify bank account information,” what do I do?**

 If you have your bank account information ready, go to the account info page. Once there, scroll down to where it says "Deposit method", click on "Edit" and enter your information there.

-   **I have an Amazon Affiliate ID. Will this conflict with opening a Seller Central account?**

 No. These are two separate accounts and are unrelated to each other.

## **Getting Setup Selling from the Outside of USA**

-   **Do I need to have a US address?**

You do not need a US address if:

1.  1.  You are in one of the countries approved by Amazon

-   **Do I need to have bank details when first setting up my Seller Central account?**

You can set up your Seller Central account at Amazon without adding your bank details so go ahead and do that. Once you have bank details to offer (e.g. perhaps you're waiting on final approval of your bank account), you then need to request US payments approval from them. 

-   **I live in the UK and I have a local currency account. Do I have to open a US Dollar** **account (as my bank has an option to open a US Dollar account as well) to be able to** **work with Amazon.com OR could my local UK currency bank account allow me to** **work with Amazon.com?**

Amazon will convert automatically for you so either one is fine.

-   **In order to sell in other Amazon marketplaces, do I require a Seller Central account** **in each of the marketplaces (e.g. amazon.de, amazon.ca, amazon.com, etc.)?**

Yes, to sell in other marketplaces you will need to set up a Seller Central account for each of them.

-   **Should my business be set up as an individual or as a business?**

 To start, you can set up as an individual. You can always change the information in your account later if you switch to a business.

-   **If I Set up a US-­­based Seller Central account, do I have to pay US taxes?**

We are not tax professionals and as such cannot provide you with tax advice and I recommend you consult with a local tax professional. We can tell you, however, that we understand that you do not pay taxes in the USA unless you leave the money in the USA. If you withdraw the money to your own country, you will be responsible to pay taxes there only.

-   **If I am using Payoneer, do I need to apply for a US Tax Identity and a US-­­based** **Address?**

If using Payoneer from a location outside the USA, you do not require a Tax Identity. For address info, you may use your local information to fill in that section.

-   **If we insert our legal business address (outside the US) on the seller information** **page and use a US bank account, is it acceptable for this model or will we get charged with shipping costs and customs duties for returns?** 

If you are using Amazon's FBA, your Amazon info has nothing to do with returns, so there's nothing to worry about here.

-   **Do I have to start with Amazon.com or can I do this in my own country's** **marketplace (e.g. amazon.de)?**

While some of the details are specific to the Amazon.com environment and Amazon.com is the best place to start because of volume, it is perfectly fine to start in the Amazon marketplace of your choice.

-   **Do I require a personal tax ID if setting up my Seller Central account using a UK Ltd. company?**

 No. Only company details are required.0

-   **Is it advisable to set up an LLC even if I have an Ltd Company in the UK?**

 Since you are in the UK, you can do everything without needing any type of US account, etc. However, we do recommend setting up an LLC once your payments start to rise, so if you want to go ahead and do it now you can, even though it is not a requirement for UK residents to start.

-   **I have been through the Option C materials & Payoneer process and am a bit** **confused -­­ do we use our real physical address if we live outside of the USA?**

 If you are using Payoneer you do not need to use any kind of US info you just use your own home info.

-   **In the Seller Central account settings, there is no place to select Check Payments. Is this a default?**

 You cannot get check (aka cheque) payments from Seller Central. Payments from Amazon are direct deposit only.

-   **Is there a benefit from using a US bank account versus a Payoneer account or vice** **versa?**

Absolutely. If you have a USA bank account, use that.

-   **What address should be used for the return address?**

 Since you'll be using FBA, you don't require a return address.

-   **Can I have 2 Seller Central accounts?**

Yes, but you have to apply for it. After you have one account created and established, you can then contact Amazon and make a request. Just go through with one account initially and then once you are established you can work with Amazon to get a second account.

-   **Why do I need an EIN from the US IRS?**

 You do not need this unless you are using a physical address in the US.

-   **Should I use an "individual" or "business" account when signing up for Payoneer?**

Use "individual".

-   **I am planning to sell in two different Amazon marketplaces (e.g. US and UK). Do I** **set up two Seller Central accounts to do this?**

This is not the same as trying to have more than one Seller Central account in one specific marketplace. Each Amazon marketplace is separate from the others and therefore requires an associated Seller Central account.

-   **If I can use my current bank account (e.g. UK), would you recommend still setting** **up a US one if possible? Asking which would be more sensible long-­­term, or are they** **quite equal since Amazon accepts both?**

Down the road when your business starts to really take off, you will want to set up an US bank account.

-   **What is the best kind of company -­­ a corp. or an LLC?**

An LLC is easier to set up, but initially, if you just use "Option C", you can go about creating a corp. or LLC a little later (so you can use your profits to pay for those things).

## **Part 2 of 3 on niche research: High Competition and Low Competition Products**

-   **Is it a good idea to sell a product that presently is only sold to a very small customer base? E.g. vitamins produced by a local doctor and distributed only to his patients.**

 If there are similar products on Amazon that appear to be selling well, then you may find some success with them. Otherwise, from a profit standpoint, you will probably want to go with something else that is a proven product line.

-   **Can we compete with a product when it has good pics and reviews by just doing it better?**

 Absolutely! In Module 4 you will learn how to better optimize than ANY other product. In Module 5, you'll learn promotion stuff that nobody on Amazon (other than us) are doing.

## **Niche Research, Part 3: Passions, Interests, and Your Five Categories**

-   **Would you say categories and subcategories in Amazon are akin to markets and niches(or sub-niches)in the traditional "internet marketing" sense?**

This is a good way to look at it.

-   **Ideally, I'd like to select a niche or market to work within (find multiple products within that one area) as this would be the most efficient way to brand and drive traffic and authority externally to Amazon....so, if I select some categories based on interest, passions or plain old research, am I implicitly also selecting potential markets/niches?**

The biggest thing is only doing one thing at a time. Focus exclusively on finding the best opportunity. Don't start thinking about future product lines, etc. right now. Those things will come naturally once your first product becomes successful.

-   **Do I try to do a private label with a product first or do I proceed to sell the current label/brand to start with and then see about creating my own brand if it proves to be profitable?**

 We strongly recommend using Private Label and starting to create your own brand.

-   **What if a person can't identify a passion or interest that would make for good private label products to market?**

 It is easier to start with something you are passionate about or at least interested in, but that should not limit you from taking advantage of the many great opportunities that exist. If you feel you're one of these people, then just follow the criteria for searching out good products to consider and go with that.
