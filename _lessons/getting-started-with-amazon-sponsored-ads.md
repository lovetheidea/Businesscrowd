This lesson will show you how to take advantage of Amazon's internal advertising program. This program is exclusive to Amazon sellers and is not only the simplest way to get started promoting, but it may also deliver excellent results rapidly.

### **What is a Sponsored Product Ad?**

One of Amazon's most popular and effective forms of advertising is sponsored product ads. This ad was made to draw attention to certain listings and to attract customers shopping for comparable products to yours.

Amazon sponsors ads that display in search results and on product pages. Assume you're a red wine vendor on Amazon who utilizes these ads on your page. Your listing may show on a shopper's page when he searches for red wine on Amazon based on keywords or product relevancy.

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Module-6-img3-300x175.png)

### **How do Sponsored Products Ads Work?**

Sponsored Products on Amazon are keyword or product-targeted advertising that resembles organic search results and lead to the product detail page. This is one of the most effective advertising because when someone searches for a product that is comparable to yours, your ad will appear above the search results, giving customers the impression that your product is one of the options available.

Sponsored Product ads are only somewhat changeable in terms of content, as they are generated automatically based on the advertised product listing. 

It is a cost-per-click ad for individual product listings that appear in shopping results and on product detail pages. It implies you'll only be charged if someone clicks on one of your Product advertisements. 

When creating this type of ad, take in mind that the amount of bidding you use for your listings ad can affect where your ad appears on the page result.

-   If you bid high enough, your listing ad will show on the first page of the search results.
-   Your ad may appear at the bottom of the page or not at all if you bid too low.

#### **Sponsored Product Ad Setup** 

To set up your own campaign, perform the following steps: 

**I.** In your, Seller Central Account go to **Advertising** Tab.

**II.** Choose **“****_Campaign Manager_****”**

**III.** Under Campaign type: 

-   -   -   **Sponsored Products** \- Individual product listings have cost-per-click ads that appear in shopping results and on product detail pages.
        -   **Sponsored Brands** - In shopping results, cost-per-click advertising for companies display with a bespoke headline, brand logo, and several products.
        -   **Sponsored Display** - Help you in engaging audiences who have shown interest in categories related to your promoted goods or who visited the product's detail page but did not buy.

Choose **“****_Sponsored Products”_**  and click **_“Continue”_**

**IV.** Under the **“Settings”** 

Enter a campaign name (it doesn’t really matter what), the date range you want your ad to run, portfolio, daily budget, and targeting type. 

You should set your daily budget at a level that is comfortable for you. I'm not going to promise you'll make money, but this program is currently very profitable. You might not get a lot of volumes depending on your goods, so start little, like $15, and see how it goes. If it goes well, you can easily increase it later.

You'll define your bidding strategy in the next section. We recommend **_"Dynamic Bids, down only"_** for **manual campaigns** because you'll have more control over the data and bidding for each keyword. Your daily budget may rapidly run out if you choose **' Dynamic bids- up and down.'**

**V.** Next, choose an **‘****_Ad Group Name_****’** 

What you choose isn’t all that important, so you can call it whatever you want. 

**VI.** Then choose the products that you would like to add to this **Ad Group.** 

**VII.** Choose your **Targeting Type:**

-   -   -   **Keyword Targeting -** Here you can use the specific search terms your customers usually use when searching your product.

-   -   -   **Product Targeting -** Specific competitor ASINs, categories, brands, or product attributes can be targeted here.

Let us choose **_“Keyword Targeting”_**

**VIII.** Next in line is the **Keywords**  

A **_'Keywords'_** field appears below the area for adding products. It will already suggest keywords depending on the name of your product. After the suggested keyword, you can also enter your own keyword from your keyword search.

**IX.** After you've entered all of the keywords for which you'd like your ad to appear, Amazon will provide the suggested **bid value for each keyword.** It's up to you whether you want to increase, decrease, or leave the amount of bid per keyword as is. When first starting out, we can start small and increase it the next time we run an ad if the traffic is converting well.

**X.** Lastly, **“****_Launch Campaign”_**, and you are ready to go.

One thing to keep in mind is that when you start advertising on different keywords and your products start receiving clicks, you'll see in the stats that your product received clicks and that you'll be charged for those clicks, but the actual sales data on that product may take several days to appear. So, the easiest approach to assess if your campaign is working is to run it for 4–7 days, then pause it for 4–5 days to let the campaign data in, and then look at the results. You can then decide whether to grow your campaign by bidding higher on your top keywords and bidding lower on your underperforming keywords, or by deleting your underperforming keywords entirely.

Remember that the goal of the campaign is to make a profit, so choose and test values to see which ones lead to profit. Adjusting your keywords to locate the ones that work best should also be a part of this process. To ensure a lucrative campaign, run the tests, assess the findings, and choose the best solutions.
