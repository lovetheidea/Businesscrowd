### **What Is Outsourcing?**

Outsourcing is the practice of hiring someone from outside a company to perform services or create goods that were previously performed in-house by the company's own employees and staff. Businesses use outsourcing to save money. It's a common practice among solopreneurs when they have tasks that they don't want to do, can't do, don't have time to do, or shouldn't do.

Professionals Who Can Be Outsourced

-   -   -   Content writer to write press releases or articles for your product
        -   A video creator who can create “how-to” videos or marketing videos for your product
        -   A traffic-generation team or SEO team that can drive more visibility and traffic to your product

When you first start your Amazon business, you can take your time and start with just one outsourcer, keeping in mind that there are two types of outsourcers: those for one-time jobs such as a logo or package design, and those for ongoing jobs.

You will most likely only need one worker at first, so you will not need to hire a project manager; instead, just hire someone to do the jobs you need to be done. This will actually train you to deal with an outsourcer and is excellent practice for when you do need a project manager.

When you hire your first outsourcer, you should obviously give them a chance to prove themselves; however, don't be afraid to fire a worker if they aren't delivering the results you expect; there's no need to feel guilty; this is a business, and you need to do what's best for it.

### **Benefits Of Outsourcing**

-   **Free up your time** to focus on building your business
-   **Accomplish tasks** that you personally don’t have the skills to implement
-   Save yourself time, frustration and money having to learn a new skillset
-   Save money from hiring a full-time employee

To maximize the success of this practice in assisting your business's growth, you must first understand the need for outsourcing in your business as well as the enormous opportunities it can bring to your business and your bottom line profits. The next step is to plan your outsourcing strategy, including what you want to outsource and how you intend to do so.

### **Outsourcing Your Team**

-   Build a team of skilled outsourcers that can help to grow the different aspects of your business.
-   Because of the global talent pool, the opportunities are nearly limitless, especially now that working online is widely accepted in various countries.
-   Leverage their skills and their knowledge to grow your business.
-   Before expecting anything from your outsourced team, make sure you have your work process established. 
-   Communication is essential. There is a good chance that you will be working with different nationalities in different time zones; therefore, you must have a method of communication in place.
