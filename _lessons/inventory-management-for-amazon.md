This lesson will teach you how to manage your inventory when selling on Amazon.

### **Two Main Rules of Inventory Management** 

When managing your inventory, there are two rules you want to follow: 

1.  1.  **Don’t Run Out**  

It's difficult to predict how many units you'll sell when you're just starting out because Amazon doesn't provide that information, so it's fine if you run out. Once you're up and running, though, you never want to run out of inventory because you won't be able to make any money. Additionally, your rankings may decline slightly. If your rating lowers, you won't have to start from scratch; instead, you'll have to rebuild your ranking.

1.  2.  **Don’t Order Too Much Inventory** 

Inventory management is an art and science in equal measure. Ordering too little inventory may result in you running out and being unable to fulfill orders, but ordering too much inventory results in funds being locked up in inventory. Learn to strike a healthy balance between the two.

### **Inventory Lead Time** 

The time it takes from when you order goods from a supplier to when it is physically available for sale is known as **_inventory lead time_**. You'll need to know your inventory lead time to effectively manage your inventory. You'll need to understand every step of the procedure, including:

-   -   -   Contacting your supplier
        -   The order is being entered into your supplier's system.
        -   Getting the things ready for sale
        -   Getting the merchandise to where it'll be fulfilled is the first step.
        -   If you're using FBA, you'll need to factor in the time it takes Amazon to handle your inventory. 

### **\# of Units Sold per Day** 

Next, you should look at the number of units you are selling per day. You can look at this on Amazon quite easily. By knowing how many units you sell every day, you can calculate how much inventory you have left. As an example, if you are selling 10 units per day and you have 100 units in stock, Then you know that you have about ten days of inventory left. When you figure out that your lead time is 14 days, you will have 4 days when you are out of inventory. 

When forecasting how well your product will sell in the days running up to Christmas, you'll need to factor in holidays and other factors. In another column, write down how many days of inventory you have left. People buy products like those as gifts, thus those can be awful sales days for your goods because they aren't bought as gifts. To acquire an accurate estimate of how many days of inventory are left, keep a watch out for large spikes and drops in sales and average them into your calculations.

### **Placing More Orders** 

When you're ready to place more orders with your supplier, consider the following: 

-   -   -   **Know the quantity discounts you can get** 

If you can order a few more units, you may be able to slip into a lower price tier with your supplier and save money on your total order. So, while you may have a little more inventory on hand, you will save a lot of money and make more money per unit sold.

-   -   -   **Every 2-3 times you re-order, ask your supplier for a discount** 

When you save even 10 cents per unit over the long run, that will add up and really give you more money in the long run.
