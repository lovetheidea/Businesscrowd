### **Why is Customer Service So Important on Amazon?** 

There are multiple reasons why customer service is crucial in general and particularly so on Amazon. Here are a few reasons why customer service is so crucial on Amazon:

1.  1.  **Amazon tracking.** 

Everything you do is tracked by Amazon. Knowing this, you must give excellent customer service or your account may be suspended, especially if you are a new Amazon vendor.

1.  2.  **Product reviews.** 

More product reviews can be obtained by treating consumers well by providing them with relevant information, answering their queries, and truly caring for them.

1.  3.  **Customers who come back.**  

Even if you're selling on Amazon, if you treat your customers properly, they'll come back, again and again, to buy additional things, either from you as a respectable seller or from you as a reputable product brand.

### **How can I attain and maintain Good Product Ratings?**

Build more reviews, and build them faster, to ensure your product's success on Amazon. The best way to accomplish this is to use "Review Magnets," which are micro-interactions with your customers that encourage them to review your product.

Follow these ways to build more reviews:

-   **When customers contact you, go above and above to assist them.** Always assume they're correct, and ASK FOR A REVIEW. You could argue that there's almost nothing worth receiving a negative review for, but consistently accumulating positive reviews is one of the most profitable things you can do for your Amazon business.
-   **Avoid using false images of your product or misleading people in your product listing.** Assume you are a buyer and you receive an item that differs significantly from what the product description claims or even from the sample images provided. If that happens to me, I will be extremely disappointed and will undoubtedly leave a negative review. 
-   **Make contact with any buyers who leave negative feedback.** Try to solve the problem or issues that are causing them to leave negative feedback. They do have the ability to edit their reviews, so perhaps attempting to resolve the issue will make a difference.
-   **Provide an excellent customer service experience.** Respond as quickly as you can and as professionally as possible to your customer queries.
-   **Product reviews are essential.** When shopping, people prioritize the rating because they are not interested in any product with a low rating. Here is the other way you can request a review from your customer. Amazon Request a Review Button was introduced in 2019 as a new feature that will perform the task you required for your product review. What exactly is the Amazon Request a Review Button? The "Request a Review" button can be found on the details page for each buyer order in Amazon Seller Central. The feature enables sellers to request reviews (and seller feedback) in a simple manner that adheres to Amazon's complex review solicitation policies. The message is delivered to the customer via email. The message that was sent to the customer was created by Amazon and is not customizable.
