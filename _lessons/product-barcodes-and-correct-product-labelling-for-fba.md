### **The purpose of this lesson** 

1.  To describe the definitions and differences between the following codes:

-   -   Barcode 
    -   UPC 
    -   ASIN 
    -   FNSKU 

2.  To guide you through the process of preparing your products to be sold through FBA 

![Product Barcodes and Correct Product Labeling for FBA](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Module-3-Image-7-300x185.png)

Amazon is one of the world's most well-known brand names. FBA, which stands for Fulfillment by Amazon, is one of the features they provide to users. 

FBA works as follows:

-    you send an item to Amazon that you want to sell, and 
-   Amazon stores your product in one of their warehouses, handling the packaging and shipping when a purchase is made.

However, when using FBA to fulfil orders, you must properly identify the products you are sending. This is due to the fact that Amazon operates on a barcode-based system, and without a suitable identification system to pinpoint your products, Amazon will be unable to send the correct order to the correct customer.

Fulfilment by Amazon uses barcodes to identify and track inventory throughout the fulfilment process. A barcode is required for each item sent to an Amazon fulfilment centre.

There are three types of barcodes that can be used to identify products: 

1.  **Manufacturer barcodes** \- Types of manufacturer barcodes that amazon accepts UPC, JAN, EAN, ISBN. 
2.  **Amazon barcodes** - If your product is not eligible for or does not have a manufacturer barcode, Amazon will require FBA sellers to obtain an Amazon barcode, known as an FNSKU. You can print Amazon barcodes and apply them to your products yourself, or you can pay Amazon a per-item fee to have them printed and applied for you.)
3.  **Transparency authenticity code** \- Transparency codes are item-level authentication stickers that aid in the protection of brand owners and customers against counterfeiting.

You can visit this link to check the complete guide on [FBA product Barcodes Requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/201100910?language=en_US&ref=efph_201100910_cont_201100890).

### **Definitions and differences between the following codes**

### **Barcode** 

-   A barcode is simply a way to represent any number using vertical lines of different thicknesses. 
-   This code can be read and scanned using optical scanners, such as those used in grocery stores. 
-   Barcode is sometimes confused with either UPC or FNSKU (both covered later), but in reality, a barcode can represent either. 

### **UPC** 

-   "UPC" stands for "Universal Product Code" and is a 12 digit number with a barcode used to identify products in commerce. 
-   Almost all products listed on Amazon require a UPC code to be entered into their system (although some exceptions may exist), so we will always need one for our purposes.
-   **_You_** provide this number to Amazon when setting up your product in inventory. 

### **ASIN** 

-   The ASIN stands for Amazon Standard Identification Number, and each product within an Amazon market (Amazon.com, Amazon.uk, Amazon.ca, etc.) will have its own unique ASIN 
-   Example: B00004SPZV 
-   You will see this code in both your Amazon inventory screens and the URL for your product. 
-   **_Amazon_** automatically generates this code for you when you set up your product.

### **FNSKU** 

-   “FNSKU” stands for “Fulfillment Network Stock Keeping Unit”
-   Each unique FNSKU is identified by an identifier used by Amazon fulfilment centres 
-   It can also be represented with a barcode, which can be scanned at FBA warehouses. \* When labelling your products for shipment to FBA, you need to use the FNSKU label/sticker. 
-   If you plan on shipping products to customers as a Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) seller, you must obtain an FNSKU.
-   **_Amazon_** generates this code automatically when you set up your FBA account. 

### **FBA Shipment Label Requirements** 

To use Amazon's FBA service, your products must be properly labelled. Follow these important requirements for shipment labels and inventory packaging to help ensure your products arrive safely and quickly at an Amazon fulfilment centre.

Because there are several variables to consider, you may want to consider whether you should do the labelling yourself or [hire Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200483750?language=en_US) to do it:

1: Incorporate your UPC into the packaging design of your product. While this is not required for all products, more and more categories are being added to the list over time, and there are no disadvantages to doing so. 

2: When you're ready to ship your products to FBA, choose Amazon's "Label Service" to have the necessary "FNSKU" sticker printed and applied to your products.

Here are the guidelines provided by Amazon when labelling your products in FBA: [Requirements for Shipment Labels](https://sellercentral.amazon.sg/gp/help/external/200178470?language=en_SG&ref=efph_200178470_cont_201100890)

While this procedure may appear to be excessive, Amazon's labelling service costs only $ 0.30 per item and ensures that your products are free of defects.

According to Amazon in order to be eligible for this service your product must include the following:

1.  Product Condition whether it’s new, used, collectable, or refurbished
2.  Product Type
3.  Not Prohibited, Restricted, or high-value
4.  Have only one scannable barcode (GCID, UPC, EAN, JAN, or ISBN) that corresponds to an ASIN in Amazon's online catalogue.
