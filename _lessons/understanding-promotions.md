Customers enjoy deals because they enjoy saving money. A promotion encourages a buyer to make a purchase sooner rather than later or to select one product or supplier over another.

When shopping online, customers look at all the promotional offers available. For a promotion to be successful: 

-   The customer must know about it. 
-   The promotion must offer sufficient value to favourably influence the customer's choice. 

In the **Advertising** section, Manage Promotions assists you in creating promotions such as discounts, free shipping, buy one get one, and external incentives such as loyalty points. 

A buyer can also enter claim codes when placing an order. We only show your promotion on the product detail page if it wins the Buy Box. We cannot guarantee that any given seller's listing will be chosen for the Buy Box. 

### **Restrictions** 

Amazon **restricts** the following categories from certain kinds of promotional offers: 

-   Wine: Excluded from all promotions. 
-   Books, Music, Video and DVD (BMVD) products are excluded from promotions.

### **Anatomy of a Promotion** 

This section discusses the common characteristics of a promotion. Each promotion kind has its own set of characteristics. See Creating a Promotion for details on how to utilize Manage Promotions.

#### **Terms & Conditions** 

The criteria and restrictions for the promotions you offer are defined in the Terms & Conditions. Editing the default Terms & Conditions to clearly outline your own policies and restrictions is your responsibility.

Customers must comprehend the eligibility and benefits of your campaign, therefore clarity and accuracy are essential. Errors in the preparation or execution of promotion can lead to outcomes that aren't in line with the claimed Terms & Conditions. This might lead to consumer complaints or if the outcome is favourable to the customer, unanticipated costs.

If you're running many promotions for a single product, be sure none of them has unexpected repercussions. See [Claim Codes and Combinability](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G200212920?language=en_US&ref=efph_G200212920_cont_G60951) for details on how to coordinate your promotions to minimize unexpected fees.

Customers are responsible for adhering to the terms and conditions outlined in the Terms & Conditions. If your Terms & Conditions specify that a customer will receive a bonus item with a qualifying purchase, the consumer must add all of the products described in the promotional offer to their shopping cart to receive the bonus item. Meeting the promotion's qualifications does not automatically add the free product to the customer's shopping cart.

### **Promotion Rules** 

The promotional offer is expressed in the customer's language in the Terms & Conditions. The acts a consumer must perform in order to be eligible, as well as the consequence (or promotion) that applies, are described in promotion rules.

There are two types of promotion rules: 

-   Eligibility rules: Determines if a consumer is qualified for or fits the conditions for a certain promotion. A customer, for example, may be required to purchase a certain quantity, money amount, or product.
-   The benefit or outcome of an eligible purchase is determined by outcome rules. The outcomes may apply to the entire order or only sections of it. A fixed monetary discount or a percentage discount, for example.
