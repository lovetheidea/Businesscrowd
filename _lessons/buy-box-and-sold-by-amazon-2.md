#### **Can you outrank an Amazon-sold product?** 

→ Yes, you can. Most of the products ASM members sell will have Amazon as a competitor. That is not anything to be concerned with, as you will be able to compete easily with them.  

#### **I know it's good to create our own label and own the "buy box" on our own products so that we don't need to directly compete with other sellers under the exact same listing. However, doesn’t our private label product still need to compete with all the other similar products out there? If so, then isn’t our selling price still bound to some degree according to what other similar products sell for? Like the Coconut Oil example, we create our own private label listing, but we still need to compete with the one that ships and sells directly on Amazon.com and compete with all the other Coconut Oil products out there, so our selling price can’t be too much higher than what everybody has been selling, right?** 

→ Yes, there is a sense of that "boundary" you described, but the beauty of it all is that you still, ultimately, control the pricing. So what happens is that you can play with the pricing a lot more than if you were selling someone else's product. That means being able to optimize according to your own design rather than being further restricted to the "boundary" because you have even less or no control over the pricing. Once you establish yourself as the premium choice, you can raise your price and you will find that often that will increase your sales.  

#### **I am still not clear about the 'Sold by Amazon' issue. When people are looking for Coconut Oil, they type in the keyword "coconut oil", not my brand name, so the “sold by Amazon” product comes up first and mine is below that in the list (i.e. mine is NOT in the buy box). The only way this does not matter is if Amazon has bought from me wholesale and they are selling my brand. How likely is that? It does not seem like a non-issue. Can you please elaborate further?** 

→ That is not the only way people look at Amazon. You will also be sending traffic from other sources and people do not ONLY buy the top-listed product.
