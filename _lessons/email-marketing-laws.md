## Email Marketing Laws: Where Trouble Really Starts

Email marketing is one of the best ways to convert a prospect into a customer. ROI of email marketing is approximately $38 to $44 per $1 spent. It is the highest for ROI of any digital marketing channel other than organic SEO. Therefore, there is always the temptation to abuse email marketing.

Email marketing is covered under several laws around the world. And it doesn’t matter where you’re from, it matters where your audience is from. So if you’re sending emails to people in the US, you will need to follow the email laws of the US.

However, it might be a good idea to follow the most stringent email marketing laws in the world, which are Canadian.

## Email Marketing Laws Around the World

There are numerous laws that you have to consider when it comes to email marketing. These can be broken down into these core concepts.

## Permission

The first, and most important, aspect to email marketing is permission to email the recipient. It is a must in many countries, especially for freelancers, sole traders, partnerships or any commercial entity where the email has an identifier in it (i.e. name@company or jobtitle@company).

## Information

Within all marketing emails, you must include several pieces of information, which include your physical address (or PO Box), company registration number (if applicable), that you’re selling something, conditions of the offer, and deadlines.

## Unsubscribe

Not all subscribers want to have access to the mailing list forever. Some will come and go. And you must provide an easy option to opt-out of the mailing list. For some programs like MailChimp, there is an automatic option added. That isn’t always the case for some software providers.

In addition, when requested, you must process the unsubscription within a reasonable amount of time. With automatic systems, it is done quickly. However, it is not always the case, and you need to manually do the job.

Also, some people might email you to request being removed. Again, this should be done in a timely manner.

## How to Comply With the Laws

There are several ways that you can comply with these laws. Some of these are very easy to implement and don’t require a lot of investment. For instance, using MailChimp can help you manage your work.

Another factor is to ensure that you’re not buying email marketing lists. These can break laws in several ways:

-   You’ve not got permission (confirmed or otherwise) that they are happy to be emailed.
-   Email marketing lists are usually outdated.
-   While they are not illegal to purchase, using them can be illegal.

## Final Word: Email Marketing Laws: Where Trouble Really Starts

Email marketing is a great way to earn money. It has one of the highest ROIs of any digital marketing channel, yet it also has some of the strictest rules and laws. Following these is critical for success and to ensure that you’re not fined.
