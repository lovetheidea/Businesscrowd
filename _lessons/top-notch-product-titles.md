### **Why Your Amazon Product Title is Important** 

-   Amazon's search algorithm indexes product titles and relies on them to determine relevance, so good or bad titles have a direct impact on whether customers see your products at all. When you conduct an Amazon search, you will notice that products are sorted in a variety of ways. The default search setting is "relevancy," which refers to how relevant a product is to the keyword entered.
-   It is one of your product's headlines. The image and headline of your product are the first things people will look at when deciding whether or not to click on it. Effectively named Amazon titles increase a product's discoverability, bringing it to the attention of more customers on the search page.
-   It is one of the most underutilized promotional tools on Amazon. The majority of people do not give this much thought. By following the advice in this lesson, you will put yourself ahead of a sizable portion of your competitors.

### **Product Title Guidelines by Amazon**

The following are some guidelines provided by amazon that we should keep in mind when creating our product title:

-   -   The length of the title, including spaces, cannot exceed 200 characters.
    -   The title must not include any promotional context, such as "100% Guaranteed," "Shop Now," "Free Shipping," and so on.
    -   Titles must not include decorative characters such as! ~ ! \* $ ? \_ ~ { } # < > | \* ; ^ ¬ ¦
    -   Titles must include product identification information.

A part of the title will be included in the URL for your product. Amazon does not take the first few words of your product title exactly; it randomly selects a few words from there to include in your URL.

If Amazon refuses to allow you to update, simply click "**Contact Seller Support**" > "**Inventory**" > "**Incorrect Product Information on Product Pages**," and submit your update request. After a product has been sold for a period of time, Amazon will take over the product detail page for whatever reason. This does not mean that your product is no longer available for sale; you can continue to sell it without difficulty.

**TIP:** If your application is denied, wait a few hours and try again. 

### **Key Product Title Tips**

-   Make sure to include your primary keyword at the beginning. It is critical for increasing the relevance and ranking of your product on Amazon. It's never a bad idea to include keywords that are important for both SEO and the customer's understanding of your product.
-   Include a plethora of additional keywords but don’t repeat. For instance, "Blue Laptop Stand" (primary) “Wooden Laptop Stand" (secondary). Because you already have "Laptop Stand" as your primary keyword, you DO NOT need to include it in your other keyword.

**Example of bad Title:** Blue Laptop Stand, Wooden Laptop Stand. ❌

Simply include the word "Wooden" in the title and your product title will remain relevant for the keyword "Wooden Laptop Stand." This will save you time and title space, while also preventing your product title from appearing cluttered. We'll go over some examples later, so don't worry if you're feeling a little lost at this point.

**Example of Good Title:** Blue Wooden Laptop Stand. ✔

-   It is your product title that will be seen by people like me, and keep in mind that when people see the title in search results and other listings, you are also attempting to persuade them to buy your product.  
-   Yes, it is great to include keywords on your product title but please be reminded that stuffing titles with thousands of irrelevant keywords are a bad strategy. Include relevant keywords, and make sure it sounds good. 
-   Adhere to Amazon's naming convention. They want titles written in this manner for a reason: it's good for them, good for you, and good for the customer. Amazon recommends beginning a title with the product's brand name and filling out the brand name field.
-   Whatever product(s) you're selling, the title should include the following information:

→a brand name

→Colour, flavour, and/or variant

→Dimensions and/or quantity

→Keywords

### **Build Your Product Title** 

Using the previously mentioned format, you begin by creating your title with your primary keyword – "iPhone 13 Pro Max Case" (go ahead and include that in your document alongside "Product Title").

To begin, I included the following: 

#### **\[Your Brand Name\]\[ Primary Keyword\]** 

For example, let’s use **ZAYNE** as the brand name:

####  **ZAYNE iPhone 13 pro max case**

Following that, you can add some additional keywords. Like luxury, men, and women.

#### **ZAYNE Luxury iPhone 13 Pro Max Case – For Men and Women**

We can also include the features, benefits, colour, and even the dimension of the product. 

#### **ZAYNE Luxury iPhone 13 Pro Max Case – For Men and Women, Stylish Ultra Thin Anti-slip Cover with Hybrid Technology Shock Proof Edge, 6.7 inch, Clear**
