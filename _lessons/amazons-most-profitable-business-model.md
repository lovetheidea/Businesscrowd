# **Private Labeling** 

Private labeling is a business strategy for developing and marketing your own product brand.

Private labeling occurs when a supplier or manufacturer produces goods that are exclusively retailed, packaged, and sold by a third party (retailer). This means that the first company makes money by selling goods to third parties, while the third party makes money by selling to consumers. As a third-party (retailer), you have to pay to manufacture and deliver it to your store and you specify every aspect of the product: 

-   -   what goes in it,
    -   how it's packaged, 
    -   how the label looks

# **Why Private Labeling?** 

By private labeling, you are establishing a "legitimate business." We refer to "real business" as the following:

1.  #### **Sustainability** 
    

The majority of traditional internet marketing training teaches you how to exploit systemic flaws that will make you money for a few months. Throughout this course, you will develop tangible assets that you may utilize for years and eventually sell or transform into a much bigger company or product.

2.  #### **Scalability** 
    

With a real business, you don't just start a blog and hope it succeeds, only to discover that this isn't a plan that can be scaled because you've already hit your limit. This course will teach you how to sell goods on and off Amazon. Indeed, this course will examine how to apply what you've learned about Amazon to a far larger context.

3.  #### **Delegation** 
    

Even with a sound business strategy such as this, if managed improperly, your company may suffer. By delegation, we mean that after you've set up your company properly following the methods we'll show you, it will be capable of being managed by anybody. Once the company is established, your participation is minimal, enabling anybody to participate. Once you have free time, you may either do anything you want or devote your time and efforts to growing the company.

# **Primary Assets** 

When you develop your real business/product brand, you will create two primary assets:

1.  #### **Customer List** 
    

When you create a customer list for your Amazon business, you can advertise to those customers. 

2.  #### **Product Brand** 
    

Once you have a product performing exceptionally well on Amazon, you begin to attract significant interest even outside of Amazon, as you now hold a leading, authoritative position with the world's largest online retailer, establishing you as a major brand and thus transforming your product brand into a true asset. You'll discover precisely how to accomplish this with Amazon, and then you may extend as far as you want - retail locations, websites, and wholesale. 

What you will learn will not go away or be outdated by next year. In the next 10 or 20 years, this knowledge and skills will prove valuable.
