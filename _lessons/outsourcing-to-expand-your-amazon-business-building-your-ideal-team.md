### **Online Outsourcing Websites**

In this lesson, we will share the best place to find people to outsource your jobs online. There are a variety of different job sites, each with their own unique characteristics

**Common Outsourcing Websites**

-   -   -   [Upwork.com](https://www.upwork.com/) – Formerly known as oDesk or Elance. Here you can find a high-end talent pool. Known and trusted by most freelancers worldwide. Mostly international, low cost.
        -   [Guru.com](https://www.guru.com/) – Freelancer marketplace like Upwork. Can find freelancers offer a mixture of services
        -   [99designs.com](https://99designs.com/designers) – Design community. Great place to look for graphic designers.
        -   [Fiverr.com](https://www.fiverr.com/) – Mixture of services at a low cost
        -   Craigslist.org – Great place to find local outsourced teams.
        -   [Facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com/) - Facebook can be used for more than just entertainment; it can also be a great place to find your ideal team. Join groups where your ideal candidate is likely to be found. For example, you can join FB groups for Upwork freelancers. So instead of contracting them inside Upwork you can just direct-hire them but beware of scammers. Always keep in mind to do background checks on each freelancer you want to engage with.
        -   [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/login)\- Platform commonly used for professional networking. The great palace as well to look for your outsourced team.

**Using Outsourcing Websites**

-   -   -   The first thing you should do is register with the specific job site; similarly, freelancers should register as well.
        -   Place your project in the proper category. Make certain that the requirements for the project, as well as the budget, are communicated upfront.
        -   Those who are interested will make a bid on your job posting.
        -   The final step is to select the best candidate for the job.

**Why Are These Jobsites So Great?**

-   -   -   Every job site has a rating and review system. Assist you in determining whether these freelancers are in good standing and capable of assisting you in growing your business.
        -   Most job sites have some sort of escrow system in place to ensure payment.
        -   You can create detailed project posts, including deadlines, time frames, and budgets. Not to mention that some of these websites have their tracking system to make sure to track your hired freelancer's progress on their assigned tasks. 
        -   You can view a portfolio of their previous work.

### **Posting your project**

Here are the determining factors you should think of in posting your job opening.

-   You need to do is to determine what you would like the outsourcer to do for you.
-   The type of work. Will this job post be open for a part-time position or a full-time position? Will you require your outsourcer to work at a specific time of the day or this is open for flexible hours? This must be determined upfront so you can look for freelancers that will be perfectly suited for the job position.
-   Duration of time for the project. 
-   Determine your budget for the project.
-   Required qualifications you are looking for. Are you looking for someone with extensive experience in this field, or are you willing to work for someone you can teach? Keep in mind that experienced freelancers may charge a premium to provide their services, whereas those at the entry-level may accept low-paying jobs. We also do not recommend being a lowballer employer; remember that well-paid and well-treated employees will love and care for your company as if it were their own.
-   Include any deciding factors (samples, work experience, test project result).

### **Test Project**

You may want to include this type of test throughout your hiring process to determine your new team members. Test projects are thought to be one of the most effective ways to find the best candidates. The goal of administering this type of test is to gain a thorough understanding of your candidate's abilities and personality. It's as if you'll be putting your pre-qualified candidates through their paces in these tasks to see if they're up to the job. Use this time to put their skills, work ethic, and teamwork to the test. Depending on the nature of the Test Project, it may or may not be paid. If they do an excellent job, consider hiring them for a larger project.

### **Checklist when Hiring the Best Candidate**

Here is the checklist you may want to consider when hiring new team members:

-   View the quality of their previous work through their portfolio.
-   Evaluate their ratings and reviews. As we discussed in previous lessons, reviews and ratings will play a significant role in determining whether or not to make a purchase or use a service.
-   Their responsiveness – do they respond to emails quickly and readily available with messaging tools (skype, chat etc)
-   Their pricing. Make sure they are within your budget and worth the skill they can offer to your business
