#### **Account/Property/View switcher**

If you have multiple accounts, properties, or views set up, you can easily switch between them by clicking on the pulldown menu with the title of your View in the upper-left corner.

![Account/Property/View Switcher](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-1-1-300x195.png)

When you open the account picker, you have the option of selecting an account, a property, or a view. You can also search any of these by name. If you wish to close the Account picker, just click anywhere on the screen outside the picker.

#### **Alert icon**

By clicking the bell icon, you can receive alerts about Google Analytics properties and views.

![Alert icon](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-2-1.png)

#### **Alert menu**

#### An example of this could be that data is not being collected correctly or that settings need to be optimized. If you want to close the Alerts menu, simply press anywhere on the screen outside of the alerts.

![Alert menu](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-3-1-300x254.png)

#### **Feedback, Help, and Settings**

At the top right of your Analytics view are two more icons:

-   The **"question mark" icon** lets you send feedback to Google Analytics or search Help articles
-   The **user icon** lets you switch between different Google accounts, manage your current Google account, or sign out

![Feedback, Help, and Settings](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-2-1.png)

#### **Customization**

The Customization section allows you to define custom reports specific to your business. You will learn more about defining custom reports in an advanced course.

![Customization](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-4-1.png)

#### **Left-hand navigation**

To navigate between reports, you’ll use the navigation on the left. By clicking on each of these sections, you'll see all of the reports that belong to that section.

![Left-hand navigation](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Google-Image-5-1-201x300.png)

#### **Real-Time Reports**

Real-Time reports let you look at live user behavior on your website including information like where your users are coming from and if they’re converting.![Real-Time Reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Real-Time-Reports.png)

#### **Audience Reports**

Audience reports show you characteristics about your users like age and gender, where they’re from, their interests, how engaged they were, whether they’re new or returning users, and what technology they’re using.

![Audience Reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Audience-Reports.png)

#### **Acquisition Reports**

Acquisition reports show you which channels (such as advertising or marketing campaigns) brought users to your site. This could include different marketing channels such as:

-   **"Organic”** (or unpaid search)
-   **“CPC”** (“cost per click” or paid search)
-   **“Referral”** (traffic that comes from another website)
-   **“Social”** (from a social network)
-   or **“Other,”** (a group of low volume traffic sources)

![Acquisition Reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Acquisition-Reports.png)

#### **Behavior Reports**

Behavior reports reveal how visitors interacted with your sites, such as the pages they visited and their landing and exit pages. You can also track what your users searched for on your site and whether they interacted with specific elements.

![Behavior Reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Behavior-Reports.png)

#### **Conversion Reports**

A conversion report allows you to track website goals based on your business objectives.

![Conversion Reports](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Conversion-Reports.png)

#### **Admin**

In the Admin section, you will find all of your Google Analytics settings, such as user permissions, tracking codes, view settings, and filters.

![Admin](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Admin.png)

#### **Collapse left-hand navigation**

By clicking this pointer, you will shrink the navigation and save more space for your reports.

![Collapse left-hand navigation](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Collapse-left-hand-navigation.png)
