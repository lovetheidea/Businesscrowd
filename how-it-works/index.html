How It Works

Your business, in the spotlight — every week

{% include bc-topbar-right.html %}
⊞
BusinessCrowd is a weekly rotating business showcase
Every week, a new set of businesses takes the spotlight in the BusinessCrowd feed. Each business gets a slot — a dedicated feature post seen by the entire Crowd. Free members get AI-assigned slots. Pro members pick their own.
The Slot System
1
Sign up free
Create your BusinessCrowd account and business profile in under 2 minutes.
2
Get your slot
Free members: our AI picks the best available slot each week based on your category and audience fit. Pro members: choose any open slot, including BusinessCrowd Hour.
3
Go live
Your business appears in the weekly feed, shared across the BusinessCrowd network. Every member sees your story.
4
Repeat
The slot rotates automatically each week. Build a following, track views, and grow — week after week.
