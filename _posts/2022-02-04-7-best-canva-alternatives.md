> Canva changed the design world by making stunning designs accessible to people of all skill levels. However, it has significant drawbacks, prompting some users to seek out Canva alternatives. Whether you're looking for a less expensive alternative or want to test a new interface, the finest Canva alternatives listed below should be able to help.

##   
  
**1\. Adobe Spark**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image1.png)

**[Adobe Spark](https://www.adobe.com/express/)** has a fantastic feature set, making it an excellent Canva alternative. It is included in the Adobe suite, but you do not need to buy Photoshop or InDesign to utilize it. The free version is fairly limited, however there are thousands of free icons and images to choose from.

## **2.Crello**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image2.png)

**[Crello](https://create.vista.com/)** is regarded as one of the most similar Canva alternatives, as the two share a remarkable resemblance. The tool makes it simple to develop designs for social media, blogs, marketing materials, and other forms of advertisements. In the design module, you can create objects, set color palettes with the branding kit, and move elements like objects and text boxes anywhere you like.

## **3.Visme**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image3.png)

**[Visme](https://www.visme.co/)** is slightly more expensive than other Canva equivalents, however this is because of its concentration on complex drawings such as infographics and marketing papers. It's ideal for creating charts and widgets to share throughout your organization and collaborating with your entire team using the collaboration features.

  
  
Free features has up to five projects, a limited number of templates, JPG downloads, a limited number of charts, and public projects.  
  

## **4.Stencil**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image4.png)

**[Stencil](https://getstencil.com/)** is a simple visual design tool for bloggers, entrepreneurs, and social media marketers. It has 5 million stock pictures, 1,200+ incredible layouts, thousands of quotations, and hundreds of Google Web Fonts. To make images right on your website, you may use Stencil on any web browser, download their Chrome extension, Firefox add-on, or even install their WordPress plugin.

##   
  
**5.Snappa**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image5.jpg)

**[Snappa](https://snappa.com/)** is another online design tool that allows you to quickly generate visuals for social networking, advertisements, and blogs. They feature a simple and minimalistic editor with icons, graphics, images, and a basic range of forms.

Before signing up, you can browse their website for a thousand different templates. The only limitation of this program is that it does not allow you to make animations or GIFs.

##   
  
**6.Befunky**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image6.png)

**[Befunky](https://www.befunky.com/)** is one of the known free website editors that includes three design modes: Photo Editor, Collage Maker, and Designer. The first option allows you to edit your photographs, the second allows you to make amusing collages, and the third mode allows you to make graphics for your social network or blog.

The program has a drag-and-drop editor as well as complex photo-editing features for creating amazing photographs. It is also free and has a paid subscription option.

##   
  
**7.Fotor**

![](https://r2.businesscrowd.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/image7.png)

**[Fotor](https://www.fotor.com/)** is yet another excellent online picture editing tool that includes editing, design, and collage features. If you want to create graphics for your social network or blog, you can use their design option, which includes readymade designs. The edit feature allows you to improve, resize, crop, remove background, rotate, and straighten your photos with a single swipe.

You can also use their collage function to create a photo collage. It is also free to use with the option to upgrade.
