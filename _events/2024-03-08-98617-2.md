Hello, #circle enthusiasts! Get ready to kick off the year with our Monthly Circle Call on **31st January, Wednesday, at noon UK time**. We're thrilled to bring you a dynamic learning experience designed to empower you both personally and professionally.

## 🕛 **Event Highlights:**

#### **Part 1 – Learning Workshop (12:00-12:30 pm)**

🎙 Embark on an insightful journey as David Lowbridge takes center stage to discuss 📚 **"What to do with your free time: Apply for jobs, watch TV, read a book? Learn how to go from reactive to proactive."** **What You Will Learn:**

1.  🔍 Secrets to creating a proactive plan
2.  🌐 How to start expanding your networking circles
3.  📝 How writing can help you find more work
4.  🚀 What platforms are available to help you get started

#### **Part 2 – Social Gathering (12:30-1:00 pm)**

🤝 Grab your favourite drink, introduce yourself, and network with fellow learners. Share your goals in this engaging social session.

#### 🕐 **Options to Join:**

-   Option 1: Attend the Learning Workshop only.
-   Option 2: Participate in the Social Gathering and Networking only.
-   Option 3: Immerse yourself in the full fun and learning experience (12:00-1:00 pm).
